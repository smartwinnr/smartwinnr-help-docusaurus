> **At a glance** - Managers can edit form submissions made by reporters through the SmartWinnr platform. This process involves selecting the appropriate form and making necessary changes before saving.

SmartWinnr allows managers to edit the forms submitted by their reporters. This feature is useful for correcting errors or updating information after submission.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you need to:
- Correct mistakes in a submitted form.
- Update information that may have changed after submission.
- Ensure that the data reflects the most accurate and current information.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to the manager's view
Start by logging into your SmartWinnr account through the manager's view. 

### 2. Access the forms
Click on **Forms** from the left side menu.

### 3. Select the form to edit
Choose the form for which you want to edit the submission.

![List of forms](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-edit-the-form-submission-from-manager-s-view-mt02ktv7.png)

### 4. Choose the duration and status
Select the duration and choose the form with a pending status. Note that you cannot edit the form submission once it has been approved.

![List of form Submissions](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-edit-the-form-submission-from-manager-s-view-mt02nkha.png)

### 5. Click the EDIT icon
Click on the **EDIT** icon located at the right corner.

![Edit the form](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-edit-the-form-submission-from-manager-s-view-mt02q5zs.png)

### 6. Make necessary changes
Here, you can edit the date, visit type, and any other necessary changes.

![Add new values to the field](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-edit-the-form-submission-from-manager-s-view-mt02qnd6.png)

### 7. Save your changes
After editing, click on the **SAVE** button to complete the editing process.
