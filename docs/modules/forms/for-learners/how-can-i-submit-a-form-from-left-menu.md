> **At a glance** - This article explains how to submit a form in SmartWinnr using the Left Menu.

You can follow the steps below to submit a form in SmartWinnr from the Left Menu.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to submit a form assigned to you in SmartWinnr. This process is applicable for various roles including users, managers, and admins.

## Steps

### 1. Go to the Forms section
Navigate to **Left Menu** and select **Forms**.

![Select Forms from left Menu](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt017oow.png)

### 2. Choose your desired form
You will see a list of forms assigned to you in SmartWinnr. Click on the desired form that you would like to submit.

![List of forms assigned to the user](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt018w71.png)

### 3. Add a new submission
This will take you to the submissions that you have made for the selected form. Click on the plus icon at the bottom right corner to add a new submission.

![Click the plus button to submit the form](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt01a4qo.png)

### 4. Fill in the details
Complete all the required fields and click on the **Save** button to submit the details.

![Add the values in the fields and save it](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt01cf1v.png)

### 5. Confirmation of submission
You will receive a pop-up message confirming the successful submission.

![Confirmation pop up](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt01e351.png)

You can see your submission as given below.

![Submissions List](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-left-menu-mt01mwlc.png)
