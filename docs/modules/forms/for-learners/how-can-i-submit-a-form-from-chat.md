> **At a glance** - You can submit a form from Chat when your trainer or editor has added the form to your chat group. Open the group, start a new form submission, and save it in the conversation.

SmartWinnr allows you to make a form submission via Chat if the form is added to the chat group that you are part of. To submit a form from Chat, follow the steps below.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to complete a form directly inside a chat group.

- You want to submit a form without leaving the conversation.
- You want to see form submissions alongside chat messages.
- Your trainer or editor has added the form to your chat group.
- You are already working inside the relevant chat group.

## Steps

### 1. Open Chat
Go to the left menu and select **Chat**.

![take chat from left menu](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr40ppy.png)

### 2. Select a chat group
You can find the chat groups here. Click the group to view all conversations and submissions made from that chat group.

![Chat group](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr42v1c.png)

### 3. Send messages in the group
You can also communicate with other group members and make a submission to the forms assigned to you in SmartWinnr. Type your text messages and click the enter button.

![Chat with other group menbers](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr43n6p.png)

:::note
You find the form in Chat only if your trainer or editor has added it to the chat group.
:::

### 4. Start a form submission
Click the **Form** icon at the bottom right corner to add a new submission.

![click on the form icon](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr53ytg.png)

### 5. Enter details and save
Enter the necessary details and click **Save** to submit the details.

![submit the form](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr54iwr.png)

### 6. Review the submission in chat
After a successful submission, you can find the submission details in the chat.

![submission showing in the chat](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-submit-a-form-from-chat-mtr550u8.png)
