> **At a glance** - Approving or rejecting form submissions allows you to manage user points based on their submissions effectively. Follow these steps to complete the process.

When your form has an approver added to it, users will receive points for their submission only after the approver approves the form. Below are the steps for an editor acting as an approver to approve or reject form submissions.

## When to use this
Use this process when you need to manage form submissions and ensure that users receive points based on your approval. This is applicable in scenarios such as:
- Reviewing submissions for accuracy.
- Ensuring compliance with submission guidelines.
- Managing user engagement through point allocation.

## Before you start
Ensure you have the necessary permissions to approve or reject form submissions as an editor.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Forms Section
Go to **Left Menu >> Editor >> Under the performance section, choose Forms >> View All Forms**.

Here you can find the list of forms that you have created and the forms you have access to.

![Here you can find the list of forms that you have created and the forms you have access to.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-1.png)

### 2. Open the Analytics Page
Click on the **Analytics** icon next to the desired form you want to approve.

![Click on the Analytics icon against the desired form you want to approve.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-2.png)

### 3. View Submission Details
This will take you to the Analytics page of the selected form. Here you can find the list of submissions made for the selected form.

Click on the **View** button next to the pending submission to view the submission details and approve or reject the submission.

![Click on the View Button against the pending submission to view the submission details and approve/reject the submission.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-3.png)

### 4. Approve or Reject the Submission
You can now review the submission details and then approve or reject the submission accordingly.

Select the appropriate status (Approve/Reject) under the **Status** section. Click on the **Change Status** option to update the status of the submission.

![Select the appropriate status (Approve/Reject) under the 'Status' section. Click on the Change Status option to change the status of the submission.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-4.png)

### 5. Confirm Status Change
A pop-up will appear asking for confirmation to change the status. Click on the **Yes** button to save the status.

![This will show you a pop-up asking for a confirmation to change the status. Click on the 'Yes' button to save the status.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-5.png)

### 6. Status Updated
This will change the status of the submission to Approved or Rejected.

![This will change the status of the submission to Approved/Rejected.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-approve-reject-a-form-submission-6.png)
