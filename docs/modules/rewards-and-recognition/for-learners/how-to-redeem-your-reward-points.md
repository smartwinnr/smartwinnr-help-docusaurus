> **At a glance** - Open the rewards catalog, choose an available reward, and confirm the redemption. You can also review your remaining points and redemption status.

Use the rewards catalog to exchange your points for available rewards. After you redeem a reward, you can check your remaining balance and track past redemption requests.

## When to use this

Use this when you want to spend earned points on a reward.

- You want to browse rewards that are available for redemption.
- You want to redeem a reward you can afford with your current points.
- You want to confirm that a redemption went through.
- You want to review your remaining points or past redemption requests.

## Steps

### 1. Open **Rewards**
Go to the left menu and select **Rewards**. This takes you to the reward catalog.

![Select the rewards from the left menu](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2do3yq.png)

### 2. Review available rewards
In the **Available Rewards** tab, review your available points and the rewards you can redeem.

![Reward catelogue](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2dq5lq.png)

### 3. Redeem a reward
Select a reward you want to purchase, then click **Redeem**. You can redeem the reward when you have enough points.

![Reward purchasing](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2dxb55.png)

### 4. Confirm the redemption
In the confirmation message, click **Ok** to continue.

![click on the ok button](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2e0uih.png)

### 5. Check the success message
If the redemption succeeds, you see a confirmation pop-up.

![Successful redemption pop up](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2e4ko0.png)

### 6. Review your points
Open the **Points History** tab to see your remaining points.

![Points History](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2e6vks.png)

### 7. Review redemption status
Open the **Redemption History** tab to see your redemption requests and their status.

![Redemption History](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2e8af5.png)
