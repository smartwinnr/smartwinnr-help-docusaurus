Open **Editor > Video Coaching > Coaching Dashboard** to review coaching analytics in two places. The **Participation Insights** tab shows attempt activity, and the **Overview Analysis** tab shows score trends.

## When to use this

Use these charts when you want a clearer view of learner effort and score improvement across coaching assignments.

- Review how many attempts learners needed to reach completion.
- Compare total attempt volume with the attempt distribution chart.
- See whether learners usually improve from their first attempt to their best attempt.
- Spot assignments where many learners need more than five attempts.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Coaching Dashboard
Go to **Editor > Video Coaching > Coaching Dashboard**. Use this page to review coaching analytics for your assignments.

### 2. Review attempt activity
Open the **Participation Insights** tab to see attempt-based metrics. The **Attempt Distribution** chart shows five buckets, including **6-9** and **10+**. The **Total Attempts** card shows every qualifying attempt submitted during the selected period.

### 3. Compare score progression
Open the **Overview Analysis** tab to view score trends. The **Score Progression** chart compares **First Attempt** and **Peak Score** side by side, so you can see how scores change between the first submission and the best result.

## Things to know

:::note
The **Coaching Dashboard** may not be visible to everyone. If you do not see it, contact SmartWinnr support.
:::
