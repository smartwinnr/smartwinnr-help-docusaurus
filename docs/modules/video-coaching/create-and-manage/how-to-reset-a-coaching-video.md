> **At a glance** - Only the editor who created or have access to the coaching can reassign a video coaching assignment for the learner.

Reassigning a video coaching assignment is an important task that can only be performed by the editor who has access to the coaching. Follow the steps below to reassign the coaching video for a user.

## When to use this

Use this process when you need to reassign a video coaching assignment for a learner. This may be necessary if the user requires another attempt or if there was an issue with the original submission.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Analytics page

Go to **Editor > Coaching > Video Coaching** and click on the **Analytics** button on the right side.

![Go to Editor > Coaching > Video Coaching > Click on Analytics button on the right side.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-reset-a-coaching-video-1.jpg)

### 2. Select the submitted video

Once you are on the **Analytics** page, scroll down to the **Coaching Status** tab and select the user/learner whose submitted video need to be reassigned:

![Once you are on the Analytics page scroll down to the Coaching Status tab and select the submitted video as shown below:](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-reset-a-coaching-video-2.jpg)

### 3. Reassign the coaching video

After opening the response video page, click on the hamburger menu in the top right corner, then click on the "**Reassign Coaching**" button.

![Once you open the video page click on the hamburger menu on the top right and then click on "Reaasign Coaching" button.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-reset-a-coaching-video-ms93f9cu.png)

### 4. Confirm the action

A final prompt will appear asking for confirmation. Click on "**Yes**" to proceed.

![final confirmation](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-reset-a-coaching-video-ms93grpj.png)
This will reset the coaching video for the particular user and redirect you back to the "**Analytics**" page.
