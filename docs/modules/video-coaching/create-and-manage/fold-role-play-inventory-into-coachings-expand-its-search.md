> **At a glance** - Access the Role Play Inventory from the Coaching module and use the enhanced search functionality to find specific coaching scenarios.

The Role Play Inventory is now integrated within the Coaching module, providing a more streamlined experience for users. This change allows you to utilize improved search capabilities, making it easier to find specific scenarios, skills, tags, or competencies.

## When to use this
Use the Role Play Inventory when you need to:
- Find specific coaching scenarios or skills.
- Search by tags or competencies to tailor your coaching sessions.
- Access coaching resources directly from the Coaching module for better organization.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Role Play Inventory
Access the **Role Play Inventory** from the **Coaching** module's topbar menu or the list page's hamburger menu.

### 2. Use the Search Functionality
Utilize the **Search by** dropdown next to the search box to scope your search to specific fields such as Title, Scenario, Skill, Tag, or Competency.

### 3. Search for Coaching Items
Enter your search criteria in the search box and view the results based on the selected field.

### 4. Explore the Results
Review the search results to find the coaching items that match your criteria.

## Tips
- Use specific keywords to narrow down your search results effectively.
- Experiment with different fields in the **Search by** dropdown to find the best matches for your needs.

## Things to know
Newly-added items may take a short time to become searchable. If you encounter any issues or the Role Play Inventory option is not visible, contact SmartWinnr support for assistance.
