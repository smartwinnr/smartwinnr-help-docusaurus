> **At a glance** - Setting reminders for your tasks helps you stay organized and ensures you never miss important coaching activities.

You can create reminders for your coaching tasks to help manage your schedule effectively. This feature allows you to set reminders based on specific dates and participant types, ensuring you stay on track with your coaching responsibilities.

## When to use this

Use reminders to:

- Keep track of important deadlines for coaching tasks.
- Ensure participants are notified in advance of upcoming sessions.
- Organize multiple tasks efficiently by setting different reminders.

## Before you start

Ensure you have access to the coaching session you want to modify.

## Steps

### 1. Open Coaching and Click Edit

Navigate to the coaching session you want to set a reminder for. Click **Edit** in the top right corner.

![List of coachings created](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxrnr.png)

### 2. Add Reminder in Settings

In the settings section, scroll to the bottom and look for the option to add a reminder. Fill in the necessary details by clicking the **Add** button.

![Add reminder in settings](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxrvg.png)

### 3. Select Participant Type

Choose the type of participants for whom you want to set the reminder. This ensures that the right people receive notifications. You can select either "participants" or "coaches." Selecting "participants" will notify users who have not submitted coaching, while selecting "coaches" will send reminders to coaches who have not completed their reviews.

![Participant type](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxraz.png)

### 4. Choose Send Type

Decide on the type of date for your reminder. You can set reminders based on specific dates or based on the end date of a task.

- **Date to send**: Select the date and time when you want to send the reminder.

![Type of date](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxr56.png)

- **Send before end (In Days)**: If you select "Depends on Quiz End Date," you will see this field. Enter the number of days before the end date of the quiz when you want to send the reminder.

![Send before end](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-set-reminders-to-coachings-mrxeyrrf.png)

### 5. Send To

Here you can choose to send it to "Everyone" or a "Group."

![Send To](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-set-reminders-to-coachings-mrxf1fw5.png)

### 6. Teams

If you have selected "Group" in the **Send To** field, then you must select the teams to whom you want to send the reminder.

![Teams](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-set-reminders-to-coachings-mrxf2m8t.png)

### 7. Filter By

This option allows you to select the status of users and managers. You can filter by "All," "Completed," "Review Not Completed" (for coaches), or "Pending Submission" (for participants).

![Filter by](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxqu5.png)

### 8. Email Subject and Message

This will display the email subject message that will be sent in the reminder. This field will have an auto-generated message, but you can change it according to your requirements.

![Email Subject and Message](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-set-reminders-to-coachings-mrxf5frn.png)

After setting the reminder as per your requirements, click **Save**. You will then be able to see the saved reminder, and the details of the reminders will be visible.

![How visible after setting reminder](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxprr.png)

### 9. Visibility of Reminder

Determine how visible the reminder will be to users. Adjust the settings according to your preferences.

![How visible to user](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrxcxpki.png)

## Tips

- Regularly check your reminders to stay updated on your coaching tasks.
- Use different reminder types for various tasks to keep your schedule organized.
- Consider setting reminders well in advance to give participants ample notice.

## Things to know

:::caution
Ensure that you have the correct participant type selected to avoid sending reminders to the wrong individuals.
:::
