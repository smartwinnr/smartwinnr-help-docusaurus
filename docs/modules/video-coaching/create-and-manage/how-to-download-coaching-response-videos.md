> **At a glance** - Only the editor who created or has access to the coaching can download a response video submitted for the coaching.

To download coaching submission videos, you need to access the Analytics page and follow a few simple steps. This process allows you to retrieve the response videos submitted by learners.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you need to:

- Retrieve a coaching submission video for review.
- Share feedback with a learner based on their video submission.
- Archive coaching sessions for future reference.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Analytics page

Go to **Editor > Coaching > Video Coaching** and click on the **Analytics** button on the right side.

![Coaching list](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-coaching-response-videos-ms95uwk1.jpg)

### 2. Select the submitted video

On the **Analytics** page, scroll down to the **Coaching Status** tab and select the user/learner whose response video you need to download.

![select the submitted video which need to be downloaded(/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-coaching-response-videos-ms9604of.jpg)

### 3. Download the coaching video

Once you open the video page, click on the **hamburger menu** at the top right and then select the **Download Video** option to download the response video.
![download video](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-coaching-response-videos-ms962694.png)

This will open the video in a different tab. Go to the **hamburger menu** at the bottom of the video and choose **Download**. This will successfully download the video.

![Download video](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-coaching-response-videos-ms963jev.png)
