> **At a glance** - Designate exemplary coaching videos to help your team learn from the best performances.

You can mark a video as a top video using the **Enable Top Video** setting in the coaching configuration. This feature allows Editors to highlight specific coaching videos as best-practice examples, making them visible on both the coachee's page and the Editors analytics pages.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to:
- Highlight exemplary coaching videos for your team.
- Provide best-practice examples in your coaching sessions.
- Help coachees learn from top performances.

## Steps

### 1. Access Coaching List
Go to **EDITOR > Coaching > View Coaching** to see the list of created coachings.

![List of coachings created](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl2ku89.png)

### 2. Open Coaching Analytics
Click on the analytics of the coaching you want to review and mark the video.

![Analytics marked](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl2pehz.png)

### 3. Select Video to Mark
You will see all the submitted videos along with some basic details. Choose the video you want to check and mark as a top video.

![Basic details of coaching](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl3ob0p.png)

### 4. Mark Video as Top Video
Play the video to verify its quality. Click on the **Top Video** button located at the top right to mark it.

![Opening page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl313a5.png)

### 5. Provide Feedback
After marking the video, you can add feedback and send congratulations to the respective participant. You can also notify coaches and participants using the available checkboxes.

![Page after clicking top video](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl34zoz.png)

After clicking confirm, a top video tag is added next to the video.

![Editor can view a tag "Top Video" next to the video](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl3y9v4.png)

### 6. View Top Video
Once marked, a tag will appear next to the video. Managers can view the top video from the **Top Video** tab.

![Manager top video tab](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtl3xhxs.png)

## Tips
- Ensure the **Enable Top Video** setting is set to true to access this feature.
- Use feedback to encourage participants and improve future submissions.
