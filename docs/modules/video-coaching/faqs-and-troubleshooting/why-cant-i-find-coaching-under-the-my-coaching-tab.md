> **At a glance** - If you can't find your coachings under the My Coachings tab, check your filter settings.

When you create coaching sessions, they appear under the **My Coachings** tab in **Left Menu > Coaching > Video Coaching**. If you can't see your coaching sessions, it may be due to filter settings applied on the coaching list page.

## When to use this

This troubleshooting guide is useful when:

- You created coaching sessions but can't find them.
- You suspect filters are hiding your coaching sessions.
- You want to verify the visibility of your coaching content.

## Steps

### 1. Check the Filters

Verify if any filters are applied that may be excluding your coaching sessions.

![This is the coaching list page where you can find all the coaching that you have created in smartwinnr](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms8l8nxk.png)

### 2. Adjust the Date Range Filter

By default, the date range filter is set to the last year. If your coaching session was created before this period, it won't appear. Update the date range filter to include earlier dates.

![These are the different filter options available in the coaching list page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms8l8rmc.png)

### 3. Review Other Filter Options

Check other filter options that may be applied. For example, if your coaching session is archived but the filter is set to show only published sessions, it won't be visible.

![If any filter applied was not applicable for the scenario you are looking for, then you cannot find the scenario in the list after applying the filter](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms8l8y04.png)

## Tips

- Clear all filters to see all coaching sessions.
- Use specific filters to narrow down your search effectively.
- Regularly check filter settings to ensure you're viewing the correct content.
