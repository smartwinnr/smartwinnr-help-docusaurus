On the **Coaching List** page, the **Other Coaching** tab includes archived coachings when you select **All** in **Scenario Status**. Archived coachings show with the red **Archived** label, and you can find them with **Coaching Title** or **Code** search.

## When to use this

Use this when you want to confirm that archived coachings appear in the default status view on **Other Coaching**.

- You open **Coaching List** and switch to the **Other Coaching** tab.
- You select **All** in **Scenario Status** next to **Tags**.
- You expect archived coachings to appear with active and other statuses.
- You search for an archived coaching by exact title or code.

## Things to know

On **Coaching List** > **Other Coaching**, selecting **All** in **Scenario Status** shows archived coachings together with every other status. Archived coachings still appear with the red **Archived** label.

You can also find an archived coaching by entering its **Coaching Title** or **Code** in the search field.

This behavior applies to the **Other Coaching** tab only. The same status filter on **My Coaching** and the coaching report download are unaffected.
