Open a coaching tile or row in **Coaching > Role Play Inventory** to reach its preview page. From there, you can open the coaching’s own page and review its full details and settings.

## When to use this

Use **View** when you already have a coaching open in preview and want to inspect the full coaching page.

- You want to move from the preview page to the coaching’s own page.
- You want to review the coaching’s details and settings in one place.
- You are comparing a coaching before deciding what to do next.

## Steps

### 1. Open the coaching preview
Go to **Coaching > Role Play Inventory** and select any coaching tile or row. This opens the coaching preview page.

### 2. Select **View**
On the preview page, find **View** in the top right, before **Duplicate**. Select it to open the coaching’s own page with all its details and settings.
