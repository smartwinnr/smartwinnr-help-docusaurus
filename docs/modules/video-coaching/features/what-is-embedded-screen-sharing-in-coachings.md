> **At a glance** - The Embedded Screen Sharing feature allows users to share their screen during coaching sessions, enhancing interaction and clarity in discussions.

The **Embedded Screen Sharing** feature in Coachings enables users to share their screen directly within the coaching session interface. This functionality allows for demonstrations, document explanations, and walkthroughs without needing to switch to external screen-sharing applications. All activities during screen sharing are recorded as part of the final coaching session recording.

## When to use this
Use the Embedded Screen Sharing feature when you want to:
- Conduct product demonstrations.
- Practice sales pitches.
- Present documents or applications.
- Train on specific processes.
- Share knowledge in a collaborative session.

## Before you start
To enable screen sharing in video coaching, navigate to **Editor > Coaching > View Coaching**. The list of coaching sessions you have access to will be displayed.

![List of coaching sessions](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2yn7md.png)

Open the coaching session that you need to enable screen sharing for, go to **Settings**, scroll down to see sharable resources. Select the files that you want to choose and give a title to the file as well. The same files can be shown while screen sharing. The learner will walk through the uploaded PDFs and discuss them with the AI during the session.

![embedded-screen-list](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms315qsw.png)

## Steps

### How Embedded Screen Sharing Works in Coachings

### 1. Start the coaching session
Begin by initiating your coaching session as you normally would. Select the language by clicking on the **CHOOSE LANGUAGE** button.

![embedded-screen-one](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31k7l5.png)

You will be redirected to the next page where you can see coaching details. Read them carefully and click on **START ROLE PLAY**.

![embedded-screen-two](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31lr7d.png)

A pop-up will appear prompting you to enable camera and microphone permissions. Select camera and mic as per your need and click on **Proceed**.

![embedded-screen-three](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31og16.png)

You will then be redirected to the following screen. Select **Entire Screen** and click on **Share Screen** to continue.

![embedded-screen-four](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31sd8g.png)

The following page will appear, where you can select **Entire Screen** and click on **Share**.

![embedded-screen-five](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31tt9d.png)

The next screen will appear, allowing you to see the documents added. You can show the document while the AI avatar is also visible. Start the conversation as needed and show the contents if necessary.

![embedded-screen-six](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-embedded-screen-sharing-in-coachings-ms31unl9.png)

After completion, click on **END ROLE PLAY**.

## Tips
- Ensure your browser permissions are set to allow screen sharing.
- Have the necessary documents or applications ready before starting the session.
- Use screen sharing to enhance clarity during document-based discussions.

## Things to know
:::caution
Screen sharing is only available after the coaching session has started.
:::

The final recording will capture all shared screen content, document walkthroughs, user interactions, and coaching discussions for review.
