> **At a glance** - Use this flow to choose who receives a video coaching scenario and which coaches support it. You must assign at least one coach before participants can see the scenario.

Video coaching assignment lets you target a scenario to specific users and map coaches to that audience. You can select participants manually or by criteria such as Business Unit, Country, Groups, Metatags, and Roles. After that, you assign one or more coaches so the scenario becomes visible to participants.

## When to use this

Use this when you want to deliver a video coaching scenario to a defined audience instead of everyone.

- Assign coaching to individual users.
- Target users by Business Unit, Country, Group, Metatag, or Role.
- Map one to four coaches to the selected participants.
- Extend coach assignment across multiple participant pages when you have more than 50 users.

## Steps

### 1. Open the coaching scenario
Sign in with an Editor account and go to **Editor → Coaching → Video Coaching**. Select **View Coaching**, then open the scenario you want to assign.

Go to the **Select Participants** tab, which is the second tab, and select **Edit** in the top-right corner.

### 2. Select participants
Choose the participants you want to include. You can select users manually, or target them by **Business Unit**, **Country**, **Groups**, **Name and Email**, **Metatag Type** and **Metatags**, or **Roles**.

When you assign by role, only users mapped to the selected roles are included. Save your changes when you finish selecting participants.

### 3. Assign coaches
Open the **Assignment** tab, which is the third tab, and select **Edit** in the top-right corner. Under **Select Number of Coach**, choose how many coaches to assign, then select the coaches.

You can assign up to four coaches to a video coaching scenario. At least one coach is required.

### 4. Apply coach selection across pages
If your participant list has more than 50 users, select **Apply Coach Selection Across Pages** to apply the coach assignment across all participant pages.

When this option is on, **Auto Update Setting** appears. Choose **Manual** to select coaches yourself, or **Respective Managers** to assign managers as coaches.

If you leave the checkbox off, coach selection applies only to the users on the current page.

### 5. Complete the assignment
Select **Assign Coach** to finish. SmartWinnr maps the selected coaches to the chosen participants, and the video coaching becomes available to those users.

## Things to know

:::note
Only users with the Editor role can assign video coaching.
:::

:::note
Selecting participants is not enough. You must assign at least one coach.
:::

:::note
Check that users are mapped correctly to the Business Unit, Country, Group, or Metatag you use for assignment.
:::

:::note
Review both the participant list and the coach list before you complete the assignment.
:::
