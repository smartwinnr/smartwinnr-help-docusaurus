## Types of competitions

SmartWinnr supports several competition types, based on your objective and the
activities you want to include. You can also combine different activities within
a single competition.

### LMS-based competition

An LMS-based competition focuses on learning and training activities.
Participants earn scores by completing the learning activities you include.

You can include activities such as:

- Learning materials (**SmartFeeds**)
- **Quizzes**
- **Surveys**
- **SmartPaths** and courses

Use this type when your main objective is to drive training completion and
assess knowledge.

### Performance-based competition

A performance-based competition ranks participants on their performance against
selected KPIs. You define those KPIs during setup. Participants are then ranked
on the scores they achieve against them.

Use this type when your objective is measurable business or sales performance.

### Combined learning and performance competition

You can combine LMS activities and performance tracking in the same competition.
For example, start with courses and quizzes, then track performance against
selected KPIs. You can also include coaching activities or scenarios when
required.

This approach creates an end-to-end competition that spans learning, practice,
and performance measurement.

### Bracket Challenge

The **Bracket Challenge** is a tournament-style competition. Participants compete
against each other in a series of rounds. A single game can include multiple
stages, such as Qualifiers, Quarter Finals, Semi Finals, and Finals.

The competition runs on one predefined KPI, such as number of products sold or
total sales value. You select that KPI during setup.

- The same KPI applies throughout the competition.
- Participants are evaluated against the selected KPI.
- Performance is compared between the two participants in each matchup.
- The participant with the higher performance advances to the next round.
- The process continues until the final round determines the winner.

This format creates a tournament-style experience while measuring participants
against a clearly defined performance metric.

### League Game

The **League Game** is a multi-level competition. It encourages continuous
participation and performance improvement throughout the game.

Participants may start in a single league. Based on their performance, they are
then distributed across multiple leagues, such as **Gold**, **Silver**,
**Platinum**, and **Bronze**. From the second month onward, participants move
between leagues based on their performance within their current league.

- Top performers are promoted to a higher league.
- Lower-ranked performers move to a lower league.
- You define the promotion and relegation rules to suit the competition.

For example:

| Relegated | Promoted |
| --- | --- |
| Bottom 15 players from Gold move to Silver | Top 15 players from Silver move to Gold |
| Bottom 15 players from Silver move to Platinum | Top 15 players from Platinum move to Silver |
| Bottom 15 players from Platinum move to Bronze | Top 15 players from Bronze move to Platinum |

This structure encourages participants to improve their ranking and progress
toward higher leagues.

:::note
Competition configurations vary with the requirements of each game. This
includes the activities, KPIs, participants, teams, scoring method, duration,
and leaderboard structure.
:::
