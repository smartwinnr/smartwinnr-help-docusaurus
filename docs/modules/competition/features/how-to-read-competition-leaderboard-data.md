> **At a glance** - Use the competition leaderboard to monitor rankings, participation, and scores while a competition is running. You can also download leaderboard data for analysis or sharing.

The competition leaderboard gives you a consolidated view of participant and group performance. Use it to track progress, compare scores, and identify top performers during an active competition.

## When to use this

Use the leaderboard when you need to:

- Check how individual participants are ranking
- Compare group or team performance
- Review participation alongside scores
- Download leaderboard data for offline analysis or sharing

## Before you start

Make sure that:

- The competition is created and active.
- Participants or groups are added to the competition.
- The competition includes activities that contribute to scores.
- You have Editor access to view competition results.

## Steps

### 1. Open the competition
Open the competition you want to review. Use the competition details page to access its results.

### 2. Open the leaderboard
Go to the **Leaderboard** section of the competition.

Depending on the competition setup, you may see an **Individual Leaderboard** or a **Group Leaderboard**.
![Leaderboards Individual and Group](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-read-competition-leaderboard-data-mtwu8pky.png)


### 3. Review individual performance
Use the **Individual Leaderboard** to review participant performance.

You can see each participant's **Name**, **Rank**, **Region**, and **Score**. Use this view to compare individual rankings and track progress.

### 4. Review group performance
Use the **Group Leaderboard** to review group or team performance.

You can see **Rank**, **Group Name**, **Participation**, **Average Score**, and **Score**. Use this view to compare groups and monitor participation.

### 5. Track participation
Review the **Participation** column to see how actively participants or groups are engaging with the competition.

For group-based competitions, this column helps you compare participation across groups.

### 6. Download leaderboard data
1.Click on Download button as shown below.

2. When prompted with “Do you want to download this report?”, click **Yes**.

3. Select the leaderboard(s) you want to download.

4. To download data for all leaderboards, select All.

5. Save the downloaded file to your preferred location.![Download leaderboard data](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-read-competition-leaderboard-data-mtwu9xa9.png)
Use the downloaded file for analysis, reporting, or sharing.


## Things to know

:::note
There is no separate **Analytics** button on the competition leaderboard. Use **Analytics** on an individual test when you need test-level details.
:::

:::note
The available download format and options depend on your SmartWinnr configuration.
:::
