> **At a glance** - Create a detailing contest by setting the competition type, theme, participants, tasks, and review flow.

Use this guide to create a detailing contest in the competition module. You will set the contest details, choose the theme, assign participants, add video coaching tasks, and move submissions through review rounds.

## When to use this

Use this guide when you want to organize a detailing contest using the competition module. This can help you:

- Engage participants through video coaching.
- Create a competitive environment for skill development.
- Track progress and performance in a structured format.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your Editor account

Access your SmartWinnr account with your Editor credentials.

### 2. Navigate to Competitions

Go to **GAMIFICATION AND ENGAGEMENT** and select **Competitions**.

![List of competitions](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruy523h.png)

### 3. Add a new competition

Click **Add Competitions** to create a new contest.

### 4. Set competition details

Select the competition start and end date, and choose **Video Coaching Level** as the competition type.

![Select Video Coaching Level as Competition Type](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruy8ugb.png)

### 5. Choose a competition theme

Select **Brackets Competition** as the theme for your contest.

![Select Brackets Competition as theme](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruyckza.png)

### 6. Select participants

Choose the participants for the competition. After you save the participants, you move to the task list for the competition.

### 7. Add video coaching tasks

Click **Add New Task** to add the video coaching.

This takes you to the next page, where you can create the coaching by adding the details.

Give the name of the coaching, add score for submitting the coaching, add multiplier to the competencies, add score for the top video. Also, select the start date and time and end date and time of the coaching.

**On Response Submission:** This is the point we award users when they submit the coaching.

**Competency Multiplier:** This value multiplies the overall competency weightage received by users through the manager rating. For example, if the manager gives an average score of 8.9 and you multiply it by 10, the user gets a total score of 89.

This helps you manage the total score of the coaching, whether it should be out of 10, out of 100, or another score.

**Marked As Top Video:** This is the point given when the manager selects this submission as the top video.

Once you duplicate the coaching scenario, click **Save and Assign User** to go to the next page.

After you click this button, you move to the coach assignment page, where you can select the coaches for all users. If you want to assign the direct manager as coach, select **Respective Manager** from the coach dropdown as shown below.

Click **Save** once you select the coaches for the users. Then the pop-up appears, and you click **YES** to save the changes.

**Review and Select the video for next level by Manager:** Once the users are assigned to the coaching, they submit the coaching, and the manager can rate it.

### 8. Review and move to the next round

After the review, coaches can move a particular coaching video to the next level using **Move to Next Round**. The next-level coach then reviews and advances the top coaching video until the final selection is made.
