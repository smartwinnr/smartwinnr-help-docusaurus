> **At a glance** - Creating a detailing contest involves several steps, from logging in to selecting participants and themes.

To create a detailing contest, you need to access the **Editor** account and navigate through the **Competitions** section. Follow the steps below to set up your contest successfully.

## When to use this

Use this guide when you want to organize a detailing contest using the competition module. This can be useful for:

- Engaging participants through video coaching.
- Creating a competitive environment for skill development.
- Tracking progress and performance in a structured format.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your Editor account

Access your SmartWinnr account with your Editor credentials.

### 2. Navigate to Competitions

Go to **GAMIFICATION AND ENGAGEMENT** and select **Competitions**.

![List of competitions](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruy523h.png)

### 3. Add a New Competition

Click on **Add Competitions** to create a new contest.

### 4. Set Competition Details

Select the competition start and end date, and choose **Video Coaching Level** as the competition type.

![Select Video Coaching Level as Competition Type](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruy8ugb.png)

### 5. Choose a Competition Theme

Select **Brackets Competition** as the theme for your contest.

![Select Brackets Competition as theme](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mruyckza.png)

### 6. Select Participants

Choose the participants for the competition. After saving the participants, you will be directed to the Task List of the competition.

### 7. Add Video Coaching Tasks

In the Task List, you can add video coaching as a task and select the participants for this task.

### 8. Review and Move to Next Round

After the review, coaches will have the option to move a particular coaching video to the next level using **Move to Next Round**. The next level coach will further review and advance the top coaching video until the final selection is made.
