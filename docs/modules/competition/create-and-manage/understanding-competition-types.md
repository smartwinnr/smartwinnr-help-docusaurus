> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr competitions let you choose the format that matches your goal. Use a standard competition for multiple activities, a bracket challenge for tournament play, or a video coaching level competition for coaching scenarios.

SmartWinnr competitions let you turn learning and coaching activities into structured challenges. You can choose the competition type that matches your goal, from broad activity-based contests to tournament-style matchups.

Each type uses a different scoring format. That helps you match the competition to the experience you want to create.

## When to use this

Use this when you are deciding how to structure a competition.

- You want to combine several activity types in one competition.
- You want participants to compete on a single leaderboard.
- You want a knockout-style tournament with rounds and matchups.
- You want to build a competition around video coaching performance.

SmartWinnr supports three competition types:

**1. Standard**

**2. Brackets**

**3. Video Coaching Level**

Each competition type is designed for a different competition format and use case.

## Steps

### 1. Choose a standard competition
Use a Standard Competition when you want participants to compete across multiple activities. You can include SmartFeeds, quizzes, surveys, forms, scorecards, video coaching, SmartPaths, and other supported learning or performance activities.

Participants earn scores from the activities you include, and those scores appear on the leaderboard. This format works well when you want to track individual performance or team totals across several tasks.

### 2. Choose a bracket challenge
Use a Bracket Challenge when you want a tournament-style competition. Participants face off in rounds such as qualifiers, quarter finals, semi finals, and finals.

Each matchup uses one KPI for the full competition. The participant with the higher performance advances to the next round. This format works well when you want direct competition instead of a single ranked list.

### 3. Choose a video coaching level
Use a Video Coaching Level competition when you want to gamify video coaching. Participants complete assigned video coaching scenarios, and their scores determine their position or level in the competition.

This format works well for role-play, coaching exercises, and skill-building around sales conversations.

## Tips

- Use a Standard Competition when you want flexibility across multiple activity types.
- Use a Bracket Challenge when you want head-to-head competition.
- Use a Video Coaching Level competition when coaching performance is the main focus.
- Match the competition type to the outcome you want participants to see.

## Things to know

:::note
A Standard Competition can combine different activity types in one competition.
:::

:::note
A Bracket Challenge uses one KPI across all rounds.
:::

:::note
A Video Coaching Level competition uses video coaching scores to determine position or level.
:::
