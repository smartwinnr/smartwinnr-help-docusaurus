> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr provides multiple leaderboard views to track participant performance in competitions, including individual and team scores.

You can view scores at both the individual participant level and the team level in SmartWinnr. The **Team Wise Leaderboard** accumulates the scores of all team members, allowing for easy comparison of overall team performance.

## When to use this

Use the leaderboard views when you need to:

- Compare individual participant performance.
- Assess overall team performance during a competition.
- Track rankings of both participants and teams.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Competition Leaderboards

Navigate to the competition section in SmartWinnr to view the available leaderboard options in left menu of a editor.

### 2. Choose the Leaderboard View

Select between the **Participants Leaderboard** and the **Team Wise Leaderboard** based on your needs:

- The **Participants Leaderboard** shows individual scores and rankings.

![You can follow the below steps to check accumulated scores in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-the-accumulated-scores-of-a-team-in-a-competition-ms37vh97.png)

- The **Team Wise Leaderboard** displays the accumulated scores and rankings for each team.
![You can follow the below steps to check accumulated scores in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-the-accumulated-scores-of-a-team-in-a-competition-ms37vzgn.png)

### 3. Monitor Scores and Rankings

Observe the scores and rankings displayed in your chosen leaderboard view. The **Team Wise Leaderboard** automatically updates to reflect the total score of all team members as they earn points.

## Important Notes

- The **Participants Leaderboard** shows individual user scores.
- The **Team Wise Leaderboard** shows the combined score of all members within each team.
- Team scores are automatically updated as participants earn points during the competition.
- No additional configuration is required to calculate team scores once teams have been created and participants have been assigned.
