> **At a glance** - This article outlines the steps to view analytics for your Survey Template and download reports.

Follow the steps below to view analytics in the Survey Template.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to analyze the performance of your survey templates and download reports for further review.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to Surveys
Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys.**

![Go to EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-1.png)

### 2. Select Survey Template
Under the **My Surveys** section, select **Survey Type=Survey Template**, which is present next to the Date Range.

![Under the My Surveys section select Survey Type=Survey Template which is present next to the Date Range.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-2.png)

### 3. Access Analytics
Now, under **Analytics**, click on the chart icon of the survey for which you want to view the analytics. You will be taken to a page displaying the analytics of the selected survey template.

![You will be taken to a page displaying the analytics of the selected survey template.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-3.png)

### 4. View Analytics by Total
Open the tab **By Total** to see the total responses for the survey. You can also filter by a particular question.

### 5. View Analytics by Responses
Go to the next tab, **By Responses**, to view the survey results by user responses. Here, you can filter by a particular question.

## Download Report

### 6. Select the Report Type
Select the hamburger menu located in the top right corner of the page.

![Select the hamburger menu located in the top right corner of the page.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-4.png)

### 7. Choose Your Report
Select the type of report you want to download. The report will be downloaded as an Excel sheet.

#### 1. Aggregate Report
This type of report consists of the survey question, responses, and the percentage of responses for each answer.

![This type of report consists of the survey question, responses, and percentage of response for each answer.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-5.png)

#### 2. User Wise Report
The Excel sheet of the user-wise report consists of the columns: name, email, reference ID, completion status, survey taken date, and survey questions of those users to whom the survey template is assigned.

![The Excel sheet of the user-wise report consists of the columns name, email, reference ID, completion status, survey taken date, and survey questions.](/img/helpscout/editors/survey-template-analytics-6.png)
