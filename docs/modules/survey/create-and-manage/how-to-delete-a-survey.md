> **At a glance** - Learn how to delete a survey in SmartWinnr, whether it has responses or not. 

Deleting a survey in SmartWinnr can be done easily through the platform. You can delete a survey that has not been attempted by any participants. However, if the survey has responses, it cannot be deleted to maintain data integrity.

## When to use this
Use this article when you need to:
- Remove a survey that has not been attempted by participants.
- Understand the restrictions on deleting surveys with responses.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Surveys Section
Go to **Editor > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys**. Click on the survey you want to delete.

![Go to Editor > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys. Click on the survey you want to delete.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-1.png)

### 2. Open Survey Details
This opens the following screen where you can see the details of the survey.

### 3. Click the Hamburger Icon
Click on the **Hamburger icon** in the top right corner.

![Click on the Hamburger icon in the top right corner.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-delete-a-survey-1.png)

### 4. Select Delete
Click on the **Delete** button. A pop-up will appear.

![Click on the Delete button. A pop-up will appear.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-delete-a-survey-2.png)

### 5. Confirm Deletion
Click the **Yes** button in the pop-up to confirm and delete the survey.

### Deleting a Survey with Responses
Once a survey has been attempted by one or more participants, the system **does not allow the survey to be deleted**.

If you try to delete a survey that already has responses, SmartWinnr will display an error message indicating that the survey cannot be deleted because **some participants have already taken the survey**. 

This restriction is in place to ensure data integrity and prevent the loss of collected responses.

![This restriction is in place to ensure data integrity and prevent the loss of collected responses.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-delete-a-survey-3.png)
