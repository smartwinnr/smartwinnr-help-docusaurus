> **At a glance** - Survey templates help you create reusable frameworks for consistent surveys. Follow the steps below to design your own template.

Survey templates allow you to design reusable survey frameworks that can be used to create consistent surveys multiple times. This saves time and ensures standardization across recurring or similar survey requirements.

> Trainer or Editor can create survey templates.

## When to use this
Use survey templates when you need to:
- Create multiple surveys with similar structures.
- Ensure consistency in survey design across different projects.
- Save time by reusing existing survey frameworks.

## Steps

### 1. Go to the Surveys section
Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys**. Then, click on the **Add New Survey** button at the top right corner of the screen.

![Go to EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Surveys. Then, click on the "Add New Survey" button at the top right corner of the screen.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-1.png)

It opens the following page:

![It opens the following page:](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-2.png)

### 2. Fill in the survey template details
**Type:** Select the type of survey that you want to create here—Survey or Survey Template. Since you are creating a survey template, select the type as **Survey Template**.

![Here is how to fill each field in the above page:](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-3.png)

**Status:** In this section, you can make the survey active or inactive. The survey template will be available to the survey takers until the status is **Active**. When you want to remove a survey template from the survey takers list in user view, change the status to **Inactive**.

![Status section](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-4.png)

**Question Visibility Type:** Here you can set the visibility of the questions. Available options are Individual, All, and Section Wise. You can either make all the questions available to the survey takers at once, make the questions appear section-wise, or make each question appear one by one.

**Title:** Give the title of the survey template in the title box.

![Title: Give the title and description of the survey template](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-5.png)

**Description:** Provide a description of the survey template in the description box.

![Give the title and description of the survey template](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-6.png)

**Note:** If you check the **Send response to user** checkbox, then the survey taker will receive their responses to the survey via email.

### 3. Add questions to the survey template
This section allows you to add questions to the survey template.

![This is the section where you add questions to the survey template.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-7.png)

Click on the **Add Question** button at the right corner of the section to keep adding questions. SmartWinnr allows you to create different types of questions as per your requirement.

[Click here to learn how to add different kinds of questions to a survey template](/modules/survey/features/what-are-the-different-question-types-in-a-survey-survey-template).

### 4. Adding sections to the survey template
If you select **Question Visibility Type = Section Wise**, you can add sections to the survey. Click on the **Add Sections** button in the **Sections** area.

![If you select Question Visibility Type = Section Wise, then you will get to add sections to a survey. Click on the 'Add Sections' button in ](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-6.png)

It opens the following popup:

![It opens the following popup:](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-8.png)

Here, you can add as many sections as you wish by clicking on the add button. When you are done adding the sections, click on the **Submit** button.

![Send response to user checkbox](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-9.png)

It will show the created sections in the following way, where you can add questions to each section:

![It will show the created sections where you can add questions to each section](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-survey-template-10.png)

Now click on the **Add Question** button in each section to add questions to the section.

[Learn the types of questions that you can add to a survey template.](/modules/survey/features/what-are-the-different-question-types-in-a-survey-survey-template).

### 5. Save the survey template
Once you have filled in all these details, click on the **Save New Survey** button. This will create the survey template successfully.
