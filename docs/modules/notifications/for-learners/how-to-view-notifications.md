> **At a glance** - You can view your notifications by clicking on the **Notifications** option in the left menu.

Log in to your SmartWinnr account and navigate to the **Notifications** section in the left menu.

### 1. Click on Notifications
Select **Notifications** from the left menu to access your notifications.

![Click on Notifications from the left menu](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-notifications-mt3te7y2.png)

### 2. View your Notifications
The notifications page will open, displaying various notifications related to tasks assigned, reminders received, and other activities in SmartWinnr.

![The following page will open, where the user can see various notifications.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-notifications-mt3tgdnw.png)

### 3. Access Assigned Tasks
When a task is assigned to you, it will appear in the **Notifications** section. For example, if a quiz is assigned, you can find it under Notifications. Clicking on the **Take Quiz** option will take you directly to the quiz.

### 4. Check for Badges or Certifications
Similarly, if you earn a badge or certification, you can view this information in the notifications as well.
