> **At a glance** - Enabling a chat group allows members to communicate effectively within the group. Follow these steps to set it up.

To enable a chat group, you will navigate through the **Left Menu > Editor > settings** and select the appropriate group, where you wish to enable chat option.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to facilitate communication among group members. Enabling chat can be beneficial in the following scenarios:
- When a new project team is formed and requires constant communication.
- To enhance collaboration among members in a specific group.
- When you want to ensure that all group members can receive updates in real-time.

## Steps

### 1. Access Group Settings
Go to **EDITOR > Settings > Groups**. Here, you will see a list of all the groups available in your organization. Choose the group for which you would like to enable chat. 

After selecting the group, click on the **hamburger icon** at the top right corner of the screen. Click on the **Enable Chat** button. 

![Go to EDITOR > Settings > Groups. Click on a group. Then click on the hamburger icon at the top right corner of the screen then click on the](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-enable-a-chat-group-mt3sl1rr.png)


### 2. Enable Chat
A confirmation prompt will appear to verify your action. Confirm your choice to enable chat for the selected group. Once confirmed, chat will be activated for that group.

![Group chat confirmation](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-enable-a-chat-group-mt3sn3yq.png)

### Manage Chat Notifications
You can choose to enable or disable notifications for the chat. By default, notifications are enabled, meaning group members will receive alerts for new messages. If you prefer to disable notifications, uncheck the option labeled **Enable Chat Notification**.

![Manage chat notifications](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-enable-a-chat-group-mt3sosbk.png)

### 1. Edit Notification Settings
To edit the notification settings, click on the **Edit Overview** option. Here, you can uncheck the **Enable Chat Notifications** option if you wish to disable notifications for the group.

## Tips
- Encourage group members to utilize chat for quick updates and discussions to enhance collaboration.

## Things to know
:::caution
Disabling chat notifications means that group members will not receive alerts for new messages, which may lead to missed communications.
:::
