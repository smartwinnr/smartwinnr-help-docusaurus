> **At a glance** - Chat Groups provide a dedicated space for team communication, enhancing collaboration and interaction among members.

Chat Groups allow members of a group to communicate and interact within SmartWinnr. They serve as a platform for sharing information, asking questions, and exchanging ideas, which fosters peer interaction and motivates users to achieve their goals.

## When to use this
Utilize Chat Groups in the following scenarios:
- To share sales wins and achievements among sales representatives.
- To encourage communication and collaboration within specific teams.
- To facilitate interaction among users working in the same territory or country.

## Types of Chat Groups
Chat Groups can be created for different organizational levels, including:

- **Team**: For communication among members of a specific team.
- **Territory**: For users working within the same territory.
- **Country**: For users across a specific country or market.

A user can be a member of multiple Chat Groups simultaneously, allowing them to communicate with different teams, territories, or organizational groups based on their role.
