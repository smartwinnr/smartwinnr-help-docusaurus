> **At a glance** - Chat Groups in SmartWinnr allow employees to communicate and collaborate with their peers within the organization. You must create a group before setting up a Chat Group.

Chat Groups in SmartWinnr enable employees to communicate and collaborate with their peers within the organization. Users can participate in multiple chat groups simultaneously, making it easier to connect with different teams, regions, or employee groups.

## When to use this
Use Chat Groups to facilitate communication and collaboration among employees who belong to the same team, region, or other organizational groups. 

- A sales representative can be part of a local team group to communicate with immediate team members.
- They can also join a regional or country-level group to connect with colleagues across a wider geographical area.

This setup allows employees to communicate with the right set of peers while remaining part of multiple groups based on their role, team, or region.

## Before you start
You need to first create a group in order to create a Chat Group. [Learn how to create a group](/administration/system-management/how-to-create-a-group).

## Tips
- Encourage team members to actively participate in their respective Chat Groups to enhance communication.
- Use Chat Groups to share updates and important announcements within teams.

## Things to know
Employees can be a part of various groups at the same time. For example, a sales rep can belong to a group covering their local team and another group for their region or country. This flexibility facilitates effective communication among sales reps.
