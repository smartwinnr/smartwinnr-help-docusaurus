> **At a glance** - Notifications in SmartWinnr keep users informed and engaged with their assigned activities, tasks, and events.

Notifications in SmartWinnr help keep users (learners) informed and engaged with their assigned activities, tasks, and events. They provide timely information about an activity and encourage users to take the required action. A well-designed notification should provide enough information for the user to understand what the notification is about, what action is required, and when it needs to be completed.

## When to use this
Use notifications to:
- Inform users when a new activity is assigned.
- Remind users to complete an activity that has already been assigned but is still pending.
- Notify users about upcoming or scheduled activities.

## Steps

### 1. Understand the purpose of notifications
Notifications serve as updates and reminders to help users stay on track with their learning and assigned activities. 

### 2. Recognize when notifications are sent
Notifications can be sent to users at different stages, including:
- **When a new activity is assigned**: Users are notified when a new task, learning activity, or other activity is assigned to them.
- **As a reminder**: Notifications can remind users to complete an activity that has already been assigned but is still pending.
- **For upcoming or scheduled activities**: Notifications can help users stay aware of activities or events that are scheduled for them.

## Tips
- Notifications help ensure that users don't miss important assignments or deadlines.
- They encourage timely completion of activities.
