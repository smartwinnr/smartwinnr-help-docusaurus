> **At a glance** - Download chats into a Excel file to keep a record of conversations.

To download a chat into a excel file, follow these steps:

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to export chat conversations for reporting, analysis, or record-keeping purposes.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Chat Group
Go to **EDITOR > Settings > Groups**. Click on an active chat group. Then click on the **chat icon** at the top right corner of the screen.

![Group details page](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-a-chat-mt4903yo.png)

### 2. Open the Chat Conversation
Alternatively, go to **EDITOR > GAMIFICATION AND ENGAGEMENT > Chat**. This opens the chat conversation in the group.

![Chat page](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-a-chat-mt495frs.png)

### 3. Download the Chat
Click on the hamburger icon at the top right corner, and then click on '**Download**' to download the chat conversation.

![Download chat](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-a-chat-mt4975ib.png)

The chat extract will be downloaded into a CSV file on your local system.

**Download Chat History** will download the entire history of the chats that have taken place in the group.
