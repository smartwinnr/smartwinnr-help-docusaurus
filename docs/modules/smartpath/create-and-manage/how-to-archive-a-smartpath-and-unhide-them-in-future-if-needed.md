> **At a glance** - Archiving moves a SmartPath out of your active list without deleting anything. You can restore it any time with **Unarchive SmartPath**.

SmartWinnr allows you to **archive SmartPaths** that are no longer required, while still providing the option to **unarchive them in the future** if needed. Archiving keeps your active list clean and focused without permanently deleting content, batches, or learner data.

## When to use this

Archive a SmartPath when a program has ended and you no longer want it cluttering the active list. Reports and history stay intact, so you can still review past results.

## How to Archive a SmartPath

### 1. Open the SmartPaths list

Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > SmartPaths > View All SmartPaths**.

![Go to EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > SmartPaths > View All SmartPaths.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-archive-a-smartpath-and-unhide-them-in-future-if-needed-1.png)

### 2. Open the SmartPath

Locate the SmartPath you want to archive in the list of active SmartPaths and open it.

![Locate the SmartPath that you want to archive from the list of active SmartPaths.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-archive-a-smartpath-and-unhide-them-in-future-if-needed-2.png)

### 3. Archive it

Click the menu icon at the top right corner and choose **Archive SmartPath**.

![Now on the top right corner click on the menu icon and click on the Archive SmartPath option.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-archive-a-smartpath-and-unhide-them-in-future-if-needed-3.png)

The SmartPath disappears from the active list but remains available in the archived view.

## How to Unhide (Restore) an Archived SmartPath

### 1. Open the archived view

Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > SmartPaths > View All SmartPaths**. Click the menu icon at the top right and choose **Archived SmartPath**. The list switches to archived SmartPaths; use **BACK** to return to the active list at any time.

![Click on the menu icon on the top right and click on Archived SmartPath](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-archive-a-smartpath-and-unhide-them-in-future-if-needed-4.png)

### 2. Open the SmartPath to restore

The page shows all archived SmartPaths. Open the one you need to bring back.

### 3. Unarchive it

Click the menu icon at the top right and choose **Unarchive SmartPath**. The SmartPath returns to the active SmartPaths list immediately.

![Click on the menu icon on the top right and click on Unarchive SmartPath and that SmartPath will now be available on the SmartPath page.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-archive-a-smartpath-and-unhide-them-in-future-if-needed-5.png)
