> **At a glance** - Create a certificate in SmartWinnr to recognize learners who complete a SmartPath. You can add learner details, completion details, and custom text, then award it automatically when completion criteria are met.

Use certificates to recognize completion of a SmartPath or training program. You can tailor the certificate for different programs and include learner-specific details such as the learner name, SmartPath name, completion date, and certificate ID.

## When to use this

Use a completion certificate when you want to:

- Recognize learners for completing a SmartPath.
- Create different certificate designs for different onboarding or training programs.
- Issue certificates automatically after SmartPath completion.

## Steps

### 1. Open **Badges And Certificates**
Log in to the Editor Portal and go to **GAMIFICATION AND ENGAGEMENT → Badges And Certificates**. Select **Achievement Type** as **Course Certificate**, then click **Search** to view available certificates.

Open a certificate to review, edit, or manage its details.
![navigate to badges and certificates](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv6sy4g.png)
### 2. To Create a new achievement
Click **Add Achievement** to create a new achievement. On the **Add Achievement** page, choose the achievement type from the **Achievement Type** dropdown.

### 3. Set the certificate type
Select **Achievement Type** as **Course Certificate**. Enter the **Achievement Title** and **Description**. Under **Certificate For**, select **SmartPath**, then choose the required SmartPath from the **Select SmartPath** dropdown **for which the completion certificate should be awarded**.

### 4. Upload the certificate image
Upload a certificate image to use as the template for your design.
![Kindly fill all the details](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv6y84s.png)
### 5. Add certificate fields
Drag the fields you want onto the certificate layout. You can add:

- Learner Name
- Course Name
- Constant
- Completion Date
- Certificate ID

Place each field where you want it to appear on the certificate.
![Fill The Fields names](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv6zo8k.png)
### 6. Customize each field
Double-click a field to edit its properties. You can change the **Default Value**, **Font Color**, **Font Size**, and **Font Family**.

Make your changes, then click **Save**.
![Customize each field](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv70rp9.png)
### 7. Create the achievement
After you add and customize all fields, click **Create Achievement** to finish setting up the certificate.
![kindly click on create achievement](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv79o00.png)
### 8. Certificate Preview and Edit

The created **certificate** is displayed in the preview area. Review the certificate to ensure all details and fields are displayed correctly.

Click **Preview** to view the certificate.

Click **Edit** to make changes to the certificate details or template.

Review the final certificate and make any necessary updates.
![kindly check the final certificate and make any necessary updates.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-design-a-completion-certificate-mtv745j1.png)
## Tips

- Use a high-quality image for the certificate template.
- Keep the **Achievement Title** and **Description** clear and concise.
- Review achievements regularly to keep certificate designs current.

## Things to know

:::note
You need the required permissions to create and manage achievements.
:::
