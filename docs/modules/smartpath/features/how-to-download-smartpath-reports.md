> **At a glance** - Download per-batch or combined SmartPath reports with filters for business unit, country, meta tags, user status, and email. Four report types cover progress, structure, and evaluation data.

SmartPath reports provide deep insights into learner participation, progress, performance, and feedback across a SmartPath. These reports help trainers track effectiveness and take informed actions.

## Steps

### 1. Open the SmartPath's batches

Go to **Left Menu > Editor > SmartPaths > View All SmartPaths**. From the list, locate the SmartPath whose reports you need. On the SmartPath card, click **Batches** at the bottom right corner. Alternatively, open the SmartPath and click **View Batches** at the top right corner.

![On the particular SmartPath card, click on the Batches button at the bottom right corner.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-view-the-smartpath-analytics-1.png)

### 2. Choose the batch or the combined report

Click the **download icon** against the batch you want. To download one report covering all batches together, click **Download Combined Report** at the top right corner (shown when enabled for your organization). Large combined reports are generated in the background - you are notified when the file is ready.

![Click on the download icon against the batch for which you would like to download the reports.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-mark-the-attendance-for-users-in-learning-sessions-9.png)

### 3. Apply filters and pick the report type

A pop-up opens where you generate the report with filters that narrow down learner data.

![The download pop-up allows editors to generate and download SmartPath reports by applying filters.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-1.png)

### Filter options

* **Business Unit:** The business unit to report on (this field's label can vary by organization). Choose **All** to include every learner in the batch.
* **Country / Sub-division:** Filter learners by their sub-division (this field's label also varies by organization).
* **Meta Tag Type and Meta Tags:** Classify learners further (for example, Role, Designation, Region).
* **User Status:** Report on active, inactive, or all users.
* **Email:** Generate the report for one specific learner by email ID.

### Report Type

Select the type of SmartPath report to download:

* **User Wise Report**
* **Overview Report**
* **Evaluation & Feedback Report**
* **Segment Wise Report** *(shown when enabled for your organization)*

Click **Download** to generate the report.

## The reports in detail

### User Wise Report

Provides per-learner progress for everyone assigned to the batch - ideal for monitoring individual progress and spotting learners who need follow-up. The exact columns are configurable per organization; typically the report includes:

* Learner name and email
* Status (Scheduled / Assigned / In Progress / Completed) with assigned, started, completed, and last-accessed dates
* Segments completed out of total segments
* Completion percentage, score percentage, and total time spent

![User wise report sample](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-2.png)

### Segment Wise Report

Breaks progress down to the individual segment level: each learner's status and score per segment in every module. Use it to find exactly where learners get stuck. This report type appears only when it is enabled in your organization's configuration.

### Overview Report

Provides a comprehensive view of the SmartPath: the modules and segments it contains with the participation percentage for each segment, plus the batches associated with the SmartPath and their start and end dates.

![Overview report sample - modules and segments](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-3.png)

![Overview report sample - batches](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-4.png)

### Evaluation & Feedback Report

Captures the qualitative and quantitative evaluation data collected in the SmartPath, organized into separate sheets:

* **Quiz evaluations** - learner answers and scores for quiz segments marked for evaluation.
* **Coaching evaluations** - coach scores and feedback on video coaching submissions.
* **Survey and feedback responses** - responses to survey segments and trainer/manager/self feedback forms.

Use the SmartPath's **Segments for Evaluation** option (in the SmartPath menu) to control which segments feed this report.

![Written Evaluation report](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-mrpz9s91.png)

![Final Observations report](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-download-smartpath-reports-mrpza41x.png)

## Attendance reports for Learning Sessions

When a SmartPath contains Learning Sessions, attendance-specific downloads are also available from the batches page - a per-batch **Download Attendance** report and a combined **Attendance Summary Report**.

## Related: dashboards

For on-screen analysis without downloading, the **SmartPath dashboard** shows overview, module-wise, and participation insights - including time spent and completion trends - with filters for SmartPath, division, and subdivision.
