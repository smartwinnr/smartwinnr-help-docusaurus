> **At a glance** - SmartPath segments may remain locked until you meet the required unlock conditions. These conditions may include completing a prerequisite segment, achieving a required score, or waiting until the scheduled release time.

## When to use this

Use this when:

- You cannot access a segment in your SmartPath.
- You want to understand why a segment is locked.
- You need to know what is required to unlock the next segment.

## Reasons why a segment may remain locked

A segment may remain locked for the following reasons:

### On Completion

The segment remains locked until you complete the required prerequisite segment. Once you complete it, the next segment becomes available.

### By Day

The segment remains locked until the scheduled day and time. **Day 0** refers to the first day of your batch.

### By Score

The segment remains locked until you achieve the required score in the specified assessment. Check the score requirement and comparison condition to understand what you need to achieve.

### Combining Unlock Rules using AND

A segment may have multiple unlock conditions. When these conditions are combined using AND, you must meet all the conditions before you can access the segment.

For example, you may need to complete a previous segment, achieve the required score, and wait until the scheduled release time.

Even if you complete the segment and achieve the required score, you cannot access the next segment until the scheduled release time arrives.

You can access the segment only after all the required conditions are met.

## How segments unlock

Complete each segment according to its configured unlock rules to access the next segment.

## SmartPath Progress

The progress bar helps you track your overall progress through the SmartPath as you complete the assigned segments.

### Segment Status

You can identify the status of each segment using the icons displayed:

- **Green checkmark:** The segment is completed.
- **Play icon:** The segment is available to start.
- **Lock icon:** The segment is locked and not yet available.

Once you complete all required segments, your SmartPath will be marked as completed.

**Note:** If the unlock rules were updated after your batch was created, the changes may not apply to your batch.
