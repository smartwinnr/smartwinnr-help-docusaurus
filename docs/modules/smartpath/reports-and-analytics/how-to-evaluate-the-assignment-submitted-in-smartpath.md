> **At a glance** - Follow these steps to track assignment submissions and review the assignments submitted for SmartPath.

Editors can effectively monitor assignment submissions and support timely reviews, ensuring accurate tracking of learner progress and performance.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to evaluate assignments submitted by learners in SmartPath. This process allows you to:

- Monitor assignment completion status.
- Review individual learner submissions.
- Provide feedback and scores on assignments.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to SmartPaths
Go to **Left Menu > Editor > SmartPaths > View All SmartPaths**.

### 2. Locate the SmartPath
From the list of SmartPaths, find the one where you want to review the assignment submission.

### 3. Access Batches
On the specific SmartPath card, click on the **'Batches'** button at the bottom right corner. Alternatively, you can click and open a particular SmartPath and then click on the **'View Batches'** button at the top right corner of the screen.

![On the specific SmartPath card, click on the 'Batches' button at the bottom right corner. Alternatively, you can click and open a particular SmartPath.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-view-the-smartpath-analytics-1.png)

### 4. Open Analytics
Click on the analytics icon against the batch for which you would like to review the assignment submissions.

![Click on the analytics icon against the batch for which you would like to review the assignment submissions.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-evaluate-the-assignment-submitted-in-smartpath-1.png)

### 5. Find Assignment Section
You will be taken to the analytics page of the selected batch. On the Analytics page, you will find a section with the name of your assignment. Click on the analytics icon as shown below.

![You will be taken to the analytics page of the selected batch. On the Analytics page, you will find a section with the name of your assignment.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-evaluate-the-assignment-submitted-in-smartpath-2.png)

### 6. Access Assignment Status Page
This will take you to the **Assignment Status page**, where you can track completion status and review individual learner submissions. To view and review the assignment submitted by each user, click on **'View Submission'**.

![This will take you to the Assignment Status page where you can track completion status and review individual learner submissions. To view and review the assignment submitted by each user, click on 'View Submission'.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-evaluate-the-assignment-submitted-in-smartpath-3.png)

### 7. Review Learner Submissions
You will be directed to a page that provides a detailed view of a learner’s progress and submission for a specific assignment. This enables editors to evaluate responses, review uploaded files, and provide feedback. Here you will find the following details:

- **Learner Details:** Displays the learner’s name and profile information.
- **Assignment Status:** Shows the current status of the assignment (e.g., _In Progress_, _Completed_).
- **Score:** Displays the score awarded for the assignment once it has been evaluated.
- **Review Status:** Indicates whether the assignment has been reviewed by the editor (_Yes / No_).
- **Instruction Resources:** Displays any reference materials or resources provided for the assignment.
- **User Response:** Shows the learner’s submitted response, including typed answers and uploaded files such as PDFs or images.
- **Editor Feedback:** Allows the editor to provide qualitative feedback or comments on the learner’s submission.

![This will take you to a page that provides a detailed view of a learner’s progress and submission for a specific assignment.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-evaluate-the-assignment-submitted-in-smartpath-4.png)

### 8. Provide Feedback
Click on **'Give your feedback'** to provide feedback and score for the assignment submitted, and then **Save** your feedback.

![Click on 'Give your feedback' to provide feedback and score for the assignment submitted and save your feedback.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-evaluate-the-assignment-submitted-in-smartpath-5.png)

This will successfully update the status to completed and add the scores to the learner's SmartPath.
