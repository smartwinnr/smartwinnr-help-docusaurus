> At a glance – The Participation Insights tab provides a detailed view of learner participation, SmartPath completion, progress, and scores. It includes an overview of participation metrics, a completion trend chart, and a learner-wise progress table.

The Participation Insights tab helps you understand how learners are progressing through their assigned SmartPaths. You can view overall participation and completion metrics, track completion over time, and review individual learner progress.

### Key Metrics

Total Participants

- Shows the total number of unique learners assigned to SmartPaths.
- Example: 98 means 98 unique learners have been assigned.

Total Completed

- Shows the total number of completed SmartPaths.
- The completion percentage is displayed below the count.
- Example: 15 with 15.3% completion indicates the number of completed SmartPaths and the completion percentage for the selected period.

Average Progress

- Shows the average progress across all learners.
- Example: 25.9% means the average progress across all learners is 25.9%.

Average Score

- Shows the average score across scored SmartPaths.
- Example: 88.1% means the average score across scored SmartPaths is 88.1%.

- Average Score for quiz activity includes completed quiz segments only.

## Completion Over Time

This chart shows the completion of SmartPaths over time.

What it shows:

- SmartPath completion activity over the selected time period.
- A visual representation of completion trends.

How to read it:

- The horizontal axis represents dates.
- The vertical axis represents the completion values displayed in the chart.
- Each plotted point represents completion activity for the selected period.

Use this chart to review completion trends over time.
![key matrics and completion over time](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvyw10ke.png)

- When you choose a custom date range, the chart includes every day or month in the selected range and shows zero for periods with no completions.
- Completions are grouped in the viewer's timezone, so activity near midnight stays within the selected range.

## Learner Progress

The Learner Progress section provides a learner-wise view of SmartPath status, progress, score, and time spent for the selected period.

### Information Displayed

The table includes the following columns:

Learner

\- Displays the learner's name and email address.

SmartPath

\- Shows the name of the SmartPath assigned to the learner.

Status

\- Shows the learner's current SmartPath status, such as Completed or In Progress.

Progress

- Displays the learner's progress as a completed module count and percentage.
- Example: 4/4 - 100% means the learner has completed all 4 modules.

Score

\- Displays the learner's score for the SmartPath, when available.

Time Spent

\- Shows the time spent by the learner on the SmartPath.

Last Activity

\- Displays the date and time of the learner's most recent activity.

Business Unit

\- Shows the business unit associated with the learner.

Territory

\- Displays the learner's assigned territory.

District

\- Displays the learner's assigned district.

Region

\- Shows the learner's assigned region.

Zone

\- Displays the learner's assigned zone.

Designation

\- Shows the learner's designation.

### Filtering and Reports

The Learner Progress section includes Filter and Report options.

- Click Filter to filter the learner progress data.
![filter by learner name and learner email](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvyxielp.png)
- Click Report to access the report option.
![download learner progress report](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvyxb1g7.png)
The table also includes pagination at the bottom, allowing you to navigate through the available learner records. The current page and total number of pages are displayed.
![learner progress](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvz1h826.png)

- The Score value in the learner progress table does not exceed 100%.
## Using Filters to Customize Your View

The SmartPath Dashboard provides time range and filter options at the top of the page.

### Time Range

You can select the required time period using the available options:

- 30D: Displays data for the last 30 days.
- 90D: Displays data for the last 90 days.
- Calendar icon: Allows you to select a custom date range.
![select the date range](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvyx9gxv.png)
### Filters

1. Click the Filters button in the top-right corner of the dashboard.
2. Select the required filter options.
3. Apply the filters to view the corresponding dashboard data.
![smartpath filters](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-smartpath-dashboard-mvyxa1n8.png)
The selected time range and filters help you focus on the relevant participation data.

After reviewing the Participation Insights tab, you can:

- Identify learners who have completed their assigned SmartPaths.
- Review learners whose SmartPaths are still in progress.
- Check individual progress, scores, and time spent.
- Monitor completion trends over the selected period.
- Use the learner progress details to review participation across business units and territories.

## Things to Know

- Total Participants represents unique learners assigned to SmartPaths.
- Average Progress is displayed across all learners.
- Average Score is displayed across scored SmartPaths.
- The Learner Progress table provides individual learner details for each SmartPath.
- The table includes pagination to navigate through learner records.

- Quiz, Survey, and SmartFeed cards include segments whose batch ends on or after the start of the selected range.
- The Top SmartPaths by Participation card uses the same range-based segment inclusion.
- The Participation Summary average score does not exceed 100%.
