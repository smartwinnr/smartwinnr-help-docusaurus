> **At a glance** - Use SmartPath Analytics to track learner progress over time, review completion against due dates, and export results for reporting.

SmartPath Analytics helps you measure how learners are progressing through a SmartPath over time. You can review completion trends, learner-level progress, module performance, and regional comparisons to spot improvement and areas that need attention.

## When to use this

Use SmartPath Analytics when you want to:

- Track learner progress over a selected time period.
- Monitor SmartPath completion against due dates.
- Follow an individual learner’s journey through a SmartPath.
- Compare progress across countries, teams, regions, or other organizational filters.
- Measure how quizzes, SmartFeeds, surveys, and coaching activities contribute to learner improvement.
- Export analytics for reporting and further analysis.


### 1. Review the Overview tab
## Overview Tab

The Overview tab provides a high-level summary of SmartPath activity, learner engagement, and completion trends for the selected time period. It helps trainers, managers, and admins monitor overall SmartPath performance and identify learning progress across active SmartPaths.

### Active Courses

Active Courses displays the total number of SmartPaths that are currently active and available to learners during the selected time period. This metric helps you understand how many learning programs are running at a given time.

### Active Learners

Active Learners shows the number of learners who have participated in at least one SmartPath activity during the selected period. A learner is considered active after engaging with any SmartPath component, such as a quiz, SmartFeed, survey, SCORM course, or learning session.

### Learning Hours

Learning Hours displays the total time learners have spent on SmartPath activities during the selected period. This metric helps measure overall learner engagement and the amount of time invested in learning.

### Average Learning Hours

Average Learning Hours shows the average learning time spent by each active learner during the selected period. It helps you understand the typical engagement level per learner.

### Completion Over Time

Completion Over Time displays a trend chart showing how SmartPath completions change throughout the selected period. Use this chart to monitor completion patterns and identify increases or decreases in learner progress over time.

### Learning Component Distribution

Learning Component Distribution shows the breakdown of learning components available across active SmartPaths, including SmartFeeds, Quizzes, SCORM courses, and Surveys. This helps you understand the overall composition of learning content within your SmartPaths.

### Top SmartPaths by Completion

Top SmartPaths by Completion lists the SmartPaths with the highest completion rates. It displays the SmartPath name, learner count, average score, and completion percentage, making it easy to identify the best-performing learning programs.

### Top Learners by Score

Top Learners by Score displays the highest-performing learners based on their SmartPath scores during the selected period. It helps managers recognize top performers and compare learner performance across SmartPaths.

### Certification Overview

Certification Overview provides a summary of certification-based SmartPaths. It shows the total number of certification SmartPaths, enrolled learners, completed certifications, and recently achieved certifications, helping you track certification progress and completion.

### 2. Open Module Wise Analysis
## Module Wise Analysis Tab

The Module Wise Analysis tab provides detailed insights into the performance of individual learning components within SmartPaths. It helps you measure learner participation, completion status, and performance for Quizzes, Surveys, and SmartFeeds.

### Quiz Module Analysis

The Quiz Module Analysis section provides detailed analytics for all quizzes available across SmartPaths. It helps you evaluate learner participation, quiz completion, and overall quiz performance.

The metrics include:

* Active Quizzes – Displays the total number of quizzes available across active SmartPaths.

* Created Quizzes – Shows the total number of quizzes created within SmartPaths.

* Active Learners – Displays the number of learners who participated in at least one quiz during the selected period.

* Participation – Shows the percentage of learners who attempted the assigned quizzes.

* Average Score – Displays the average score achieved across all quiz attempts.

* Quiz Hours – Shows the total time learners spent completing quizzes.

* Quiz Completion Status – Displays the number of quizzes that are Completed, In Progress, and Assigned.

### Survey Module Analysis

The Survey Module Analysis section helps you monitor learner participation and completion of surveys assigned through SmartPaths.

The metrics include:

* Active Surveys – Displays the total number of surveys available across SmartPaths.

* Active Learners – Shows the number of learners who participated in surveys.

* Participation – Displays the percentage of learners who responded to assigned surveys.

* Survey Completion Status – Shows the number of surveys that are Completed, In Progress, and Assigned.

### SmartFeed Module Analysis

The SmartFeed Module Analysis section provides insights into learner engagement with SmartFeed content included in SmartPaths.

The metrics include:

* Active SmartFeeds – Displays the total number of SmartFeed items available across SmartPaths.

* Active Learners – Shows the number of learners who accessed SmartFeed content.

* Participation – Displays the percentage of learners who engaged with assigned SmartFeeds.

* SmartFeed Completion Status – Shows the number of SmartFeeds that are Completed, In Progress, and Assigned.

These module-wise analytics help you identify which learning components have the highest participation, completion, and engagement within your SmartPaths.

### 3. Check Participation Insights
## Participation Insights

The Participation Insights section provides a summary of learner participation and overall SmartPath progress for the selected batch. It helps you quickly understand completion, progress, and performance across all assigned learners.

### Total Participants

Total Participants displays the total number of unique learners assigned to the SmartPath batch. This metric shows the overall learner count included in the analytics.

### Total Completed

Total Completed shows the number of learners who have successfully completed the SmartPath and the overall completion percentage for the batch.

### Average Progress

Average Progress displays the average completion percentage across all assigned learners. It reflects how far learners have progressed through the SmartPath, including those who have not yet completed it.

### Average Score

Average Score shows the average score achieved by learners across scored SmartPath activities, such as quizzes and other evaluated modules.

## Completion Over Time

The Completion Over Time chart displays how SmartPath completions change over the selected time period. It helps you monitor completion trends, identify peak completion periods, and understand whether learner progress is increasing, remaining steady, or slowing down.

## Learner Progress

The Learner Progress section provides learner-level analytics for the selected SmartPath batch. It helps managers track the progress and performance of individual learners throughout the SmartPath.

This section includes:

* Completion Status – Shows whether the learner has completed, is in progress, or has not started the SmartPath.

* Progress Percentage – Displays the learner's overall SmartPath completion percentage.

* Score – Shows the learner's performance score for completed activities.

* Time Spent – Displays the total time the learner has spent on the SmartPath.

* Last Activity – Shows the learner's most recent activity in the SmartPath.

* Organizational Details – Displays learner information such as Business Unit, Country, Region, Team, Designation, and other organizational attributes.

### 4.Overall Participation
This section provides a summary of learner progress in the SmartPath, showing the number of learners who have **completed**, are **in progress**, or have **not yet started**.

* **Completed** – Learners who have finished the SmartPath.
* **In Progress** – Learners who are currently working through the SmartPath.
* **Not Completed** – Learners who have not yet started the SmartPath.

### 5. Compare participation by country
Use **Participation by Country** to compare how learners in each country are engaging with the SmartPath. You can also reconfigure this view to show participation by a custom metatag when that grouping is more useful.
![participation by country](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-track-improvement-in-smartpath-progress-mtu0wkrb.png)
### 6. Review Quiz Analytics
Category-wise Performance (All Countries)

Use **Quiz Analytics** to compare quiz performance across categories and countries. The category view helps you spot topics that need reinforcement, and the country comparison helps you identify regional strengths and gaps.
![Review Quiz Analytics](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-track-improvement-in-smartpath-progress-mtu0zsd4.png)

### 7. **Performance by Category by Country**

The heatmap provides a visual overview of quiz performance across different categories and countries. Color indicators make it easy to identify **strong and weak performance areas** across regions.
![Performance by Category by Country](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-track-improvement-in-smartpath-progress-mtu1bses.png)

### 8. Open Module and Segment Analytics
Use **Module and Segment Analytics** to review each module and its segments. You can see the segment name, segment type, participation, and an **Analytics** shortcut for drilling into segment details.

### Drill into segment analytics
Select the **Analytics** icon next to a segment to open its detailed report. For quizzes, this view shows **Last Activity**, **Overall Participation**, **Average Score**, participation by country, category-wise performance, and a leaderboard.

![Module and Segment Analytics](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-track-improvement-in-smartpath-progress-mtu126i9.png)
### 9. Review User-Wise Performance
Use **User-Wise Performance** to see each learner’s status, completion count, and percentage completion. You can filter by **Status**, **Name**, and **Email** to find specific learners or groups quickly.
![User-Wise Performance](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-track-improvement-in-smartpath-progress-mtu114p3.png)

***What does a flat progress trend indicate?***

A flat progress trend usually indicates that learner completion or engagement has not improved during the selected period. This may suggest low participation, missed due dates, incomplete modules, or reduced engagement with quizzes or other learning activities.
