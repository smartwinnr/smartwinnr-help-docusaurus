> **At a glance** - After you complete a SmartPath or eligible module, you can open your certificate and download a copy.

When you complete a SmartPath or a module with a certificate configured, SmartWinnr awards the certificate based on the completion criteria. You can open it to review your achievement and download a copy for your records. Depending on the setup, you may receive a certificate for the entire SmartPath or for an individual module.

## When to use this

Use this when you have completed a SmartPath or module and want to:

- Find the certificate awarded after completion.
- Open and view your certificate.
- Download a copy of your certificate.
- Check whether the certificate applies to the entire SmartPath or a single module.

## Steps

### 1. Open **My SmartPaths**

Log in to the **User Portal** and go to **Learning > SmartPath > My SmartPaths**.
![login to user view](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu2nsra8.png)
### 2. Select the SmartPath


Select the SmartPath containing the assignment you need to complete.

![kindly select the smartpath](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu2nuis4.png)
### 3. Complete the required activities

Complete all required modules and learning activities in the SmartPath. When you meet the completion requirements, the SmartPath is marked as **Completed**, and the certificate is awarded if one is configured.

A completion certificate is awarded for each module once all required activities are completed, if a certificate is configured for the module.

![kindly complete the assignment](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu2nzf26.png)

### 4. Download the certificate
Once the module is completed, click **Download Certificate** to download the certificate, if one is configured for the module.

![kindly click on download button to download the certificate](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu3wiuvo.png)
After completing the SmartPath, click **Download** to save your certificate.

![kindly click on download button to download the certificate](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu2o1qa7.png)

### 5. Notifications
You can also open the certificate from **Notifications**. Open the certificate notification, click **View**, and then download the certificate.

On completion of the module, click **View** to view and download your certificate.

![click on view to view and download the certificate](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu3wqgwm.png)

On completion of the SmartPath, click **View** to view and download your certificate.

![click on view to view and download the certificate](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-and-download-your-smartpath-certificate-mu2o5m24.png)
