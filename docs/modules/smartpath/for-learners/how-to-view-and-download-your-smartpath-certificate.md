> **At a glance** - After you complete a SmartPath or eligible module, you can open your certificate and download a copy.

When you complete a SmartPath or a module with a certificate configured, SmartWinnr awards the certificate based on the completion criteria. You can open it to review your achievement and download a copy for your records. Depending on the setup, you may receive a certificate for the entire SmartPath or for an individual module.

## When to use this

Use this when you have completed a SmartPath or module and want to:

- Find the certificate awarded after completion.
- Open and view your certificate.
- Download a copy of your certificate.
- Check whether the certificate applies to the entire SmartPath or a single module.

## Steps

### 1. Open **My SmartPaths**

Log in to the **User Portal** and go to **Learning > SmartPath > My SmartPaths**.

### 2. Select the SmartPath

Select the SmartPath that contains the assignment you need to complete.

### 3. Complete the required activities

Complete all required modules and learning activities in the SmartPath. When you meet the completion requirements, the SmartPath is marked as **Completed**, and the certificate is awarded if one is configured.

### 4. Download the certificate

After you complete the SmartPath, click **Download** to save your certificate.

You can also open the certificate from **Notifications**. Open the certificate notification, click **View**, and then download the certificate.
