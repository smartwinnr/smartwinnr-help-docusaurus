> **At a glance** - Journey View shows your SmartPath as a visual map. Use **My Journey** to see the trail, or **Details** to view the module list and progress cards.

Journey View gives learners a visual way to track progress through a SmartPath. It shows each module as a stop on a journey map, along with a progress card and a What's next card. You can use it to see where you are, what you have completed, and what to do next.

When Journey View is enabled, you can switch between **My Journey** and **Details** at any time.

## When to use this

Use Journey View when you want a visual summary of your SmartPath progress.

- You want to see your modules as a journey map.
- You want to check your overall progress and readiness metrics.
- You want to open the next activity quickly.
- You want to compare the visual map with the standard module list.

## Steps

### 1. Open your SmartPath
Sign in to the SmartWinnr app or web app, then open your assigned SmartPath. If Journey View is enabled, you see the **My Journey** and **Details** tabs at the top.

### 2. Review the My Journey tab
The **My Journey** tab shows your SmartPath as a visual trail. Each module appears as a stop on the journey map, so you can see your learning path at a glance.

Completed modules show a check mark. Your current module appears with a ring. Locked modules show a lock. A highlighted module appears larger and includes its name.

The trail connects modules in learning order. The completed part uses the progress color, and the remaining part shows what is ahead.

The map also shows a traveler and a destination. The traveler marks your current position. The destination marks the end of the journey, and its appearance depends on the selected theme.

### 3. Open a module or activity
Tap a module stop on the journey map to view the activities in that module. Then tap an activity to open it.

### 4. Check the progress card
The progress card appears at the top of the journey map. It shows your overall completion percentage and any configured readiness metrics.

Overall progress is based on completed activities compared with total activities in the SmartPath. Every activity counts equally.

Readiness metrics can show one of several views, depending on how the SmartPath is configured. These include activities completed, average quiz score, modules completed, last activity, and due date. If no readiness metrics are configured, the card shows your average quiz score.

### 5. Use the What's next card
The What's next card shows the next activity you need to complete. It also shows a short reason to continue, based on the configured What's next rules.

Tap **Proceed** to open the next activity directly. When you complete the SmartPath and earn a certificate, the card shows an option to view the certificate instead of **Proceed**.

### 6. Switch to Details
The **Details** tab shows the familiar SmartPath module list. It also includes the progress card and the What's next card, so you can track progress while using the standard list view.

### 7. Choose a layout
The look of **My Journey** depends on the selected theme and layout.

The path layout shows modules along a winding trail. You can scroll through the journey, and it works with SmartPaths of any length.

The picture map layout places modules on a single background image. It is best for shorter SmartPaths with a strong visual theme.

## Tips

- Use **My Journey** when you want a visual overview.
- Use **Details** when you want the familiar module list.
- Tap **Play** on the progress card to replay the journey animation.
- Check the progress card and What's next card in either tab.

## Things to know

:::note
Journey View shows the same SmartPath content and progress as **Details**.
:::

:::note
Journey View does not change activities, due dates, scores, completion rules, or certificates.
:::

:::note
You can see only your own progress in Journey View.
:::
