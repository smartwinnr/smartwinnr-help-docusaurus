> **At a glance** - Use SmartPath to find a scheduled learning session, review its details, and join it in person or online.

A learning session is an instructor-led session in a SmartPath. You can open the session from **My SmartPaths**, review the session details, and attend it based on the session type. Classroom sessions show a location, while virtual sessions include a join link.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to attend a scheduled learning session as part of your SmartPath journey.

- You want to find a session assigned to you.
- You want to review the session type and timing before attending.
- You want to join a virtual session from its link.
- You want to confirm whether a session is in person or online.

## Steps

### 1. Open **My SmartPaths**
Log in to the **User Portal** and go to **Learning > SmartPath > My SmartPaths**.
![login to user portal](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwgvfu3.png)
![smartpath](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhedjd.png)
### 2. Filter the list
Use **View Type** and **Filters** in the top-right corner to narrow the list. You can filter by **Type**, **Course Type**, and **Year**. After you choose your filters, select **Apply**.
![Apply the filters](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwh0dzj.png)

### 3. Select a learning session
Click the Assigned tab and open the Learning Session you want to attend. The session page displays the session name, description, and available session types: Classroom Session and Virtual Session.
![Kindly click on assigned tab](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhnbo4.png)
![Select the session type and attend the session](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhom6n.png)
### 4. Access Session Details
For a **Classroom Session**, you can view the **location, instructor, start date and time, and end date and time**.

![Classroom session](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhq2la.png)
For a **Virtual Session**, you can view the **session link**, **instructor**, **start date and time, and end date and time**. Select the **session link** to join the virtual session.
![Virtual Session](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhqrhx.png)
### 5. Check your email invitation
If **Calendar Invitation** is enabled, you receive an email invitation for the scheduled learning session.

For a **Classroom Session**, the email includes the session details and the invitation to attend in person.
![Calender Invitation for classroom session](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhxdnr.png)
For a **Virtual Session**, the email includes the session details and a link to join online.
![Calender Invitation for virtual session](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-learning-session-mtwhxobo.png)
Check your email and use the details or link to attend the session.
