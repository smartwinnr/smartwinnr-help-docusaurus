> **At a glance** - Open your assigned SmartPath, review the assignment details, and submit your response with any required files.

Submitting an assignment is part of your SmartPath learning journey. You use it to complete tasks that need a written response or human review. You can also attach supporting files when the assignment asks for them.

## When to use this

Use this process when you need to complete and submit a SmartPath assignment.

- You need to answer an assignment for review.
- You need to attach supporting files, such as PDFs or images.
- You need to complete the assignment from your assigned SmartPaths list.

## Steps

### 1. Open your assigned SmartPaths
Log in to the User Portal with your SmartWinnr credentials. 
Then go to **Learning > SmartPath > My SmartPaths**.
![login to user portal](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mudwjii0.png)
The **Assigned** tab shows the SmartPaths assigned to you.
Select the assignment you need to complete.
![Select the assignment you need to complete.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mue38ki1.png)
### 3. Filter the list
Use **View Type** and **Filters** in the top-right corner to narrow the list. You can filter by **Type**, **Course Type**, and **Year**. After you choose your filters, select **Apply**.
![Filter the list](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mue3cjfq.png)
### 4. Open the assignment
After opening the assigned assignment, you can view the **assignment name, description, and the modules included in the assignment**.
![Open the assignment](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mudwqh5a.png)
### 5. Review the module details
Open the required **assignment module** to continue.
Read the module description, instructions, and any reference files before you respond.

Open a reference file to review it in the file viewer. The page provides Security, Screenshot, Sidebar, and Page Settings options. You can also adjust **Zoom**, turn on **Pan Mode**, and use **Search**. If needed, enable **Case Sensitive** or **Whole Word** search, or download the file for offline access.
![file settings](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mudx8bqv.png)
### 6. Enter your response
Under **Give Your Response**, type your answer in the **Answer** field.

Use the available formatting options to present your response clearly.

### 7. Upload Files

If the assignment requires supporting documents, select **Upload File**, attach the required file, and click **Done**.
![kindly upload supporting files](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mue3mn4v.png)
### 8. Submit the assignment
After you finish your response and add any required files, click the submit button to send the assignment for review.
![kindly click on submit button](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-submit-an-assignment-in-a-smartpath-mue3se1x.png)
