> **At a glance** - Create and download AI-generated video tutorials for your training programs and educational content using the Video Lab feature.

The **Video Creation** feature allows you to transform PDF-based content into engaging AI-powered video tutorials with voiceover. You can customize the video with various options, including title, description, duration, language, gender, voice, and background music. Once generated, you can preview and download the video for use in training programs, educational materials, or learning resources.

## When to use this
Use the **Video Creation** feature when you want to:

- Create engaging video tutorials from training or educational content.
- Convert PDF-based learning materials into AI-powered videos.
- Provide learners with a more visual and interactive learning experience.
- Customize videos with different durations, languages, voices, and background music.
- Enhance educational content with AI-generated voiceovers and video presentations.

## Steps

### 1. Access Video Lab
Log in to the **Editor Portal** and navigate to **Authoring Tools → Video Lab**. Click **Create New Video** to start.

![Create video lab](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mth3qxak.jpg)

### 2. Configure Video Information
Provide the basic details for your video:

- **Title**: Enter a suitable title (required).
- **Description**: Optionally, add a description for context.
- **Video Duration**: Select from preset options (1, 2, 3, or 5 minutes) or choose **Custom** to specify a duration between 1 and 10 minutes.

![The image describes the "create new video" option available in the top right corner](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-a-video-with-video-lab-mth7yqrt.png)


### 3. Configure Language 
Choose the language for the video. **Video Lab** supports multiple languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, and more.

![Kindly select the language](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-a-video-with-video-lab-mth681db.png)

Configure the AI video by selecting the following:

- **Gender**
- **Voice**

### 4. Select Background Music (Optional)
Enhance your video by selecting background music from the **Music Track** dropdown. If no music is needed, select **None**.

### 5. Upload Supporting Resources
Upload the content you want the AI to create a video from. Supported file types include PDF only, with a maximum file size of 20 MB.

### 6. Generate the Video
Review your settings and click **Start Processing**. Wait for **Video Lab** to generate the video; processing time varies based on duration and content.
click on **Start Editing**.

![kindly select the option start editing to edit video](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-a-video-with-video-lab-mth8o20b.png)


### 7. Track Your AI Video
You can customize your video before generating it.
You can make changes to the following options:

- **Slide Transitions** – Choose how one slide moves to the next (for example, Fade).
- **Music** – Add background music or keep it as None.
- **Voice** – Select an AI voice from the available voice options.
- **Content** – Edit the title or script for the selected slide if needed.

Once you have finished editing your video settings, click ***Generate Video*** in the top-right corner of the screen.

![generates video ](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-a-video-with-video-lab-mth8f2le.png)


### 8. Video Generation Submitted

After clicking ***Generate Video***, a confirmation message appears indicating that your request has been submitted successfully.

The pop-up message displays:

***Video Generation Submitted***

Your video generation process has been successfully initiated. You can track the progress in the Generated Videos section. The video will be available for download once processing is complete.

Click ***Close*** to return to the editor.

![kindly select the option generate video for video generation](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-a-video-with-video-lab-mth8fhub.png)


The generated video will appear in the ***Generated Videos panel***.


## Tips
- Experiment with different AI voices to find the best voice for your video and audience.
- Use background music to create a more engaging listening experience.

***Note*** : Video Lab does not have an option to export videos directly to SmartFeed. To publish a video in SmartFeed, first download the generated video from Video Lab, then create a new SmartFeed and upload the downloaded video.

## Things to know
:::caution
Ensure that the uploaded files meet the supported formats and size limits to avoid processing errors.
:::
