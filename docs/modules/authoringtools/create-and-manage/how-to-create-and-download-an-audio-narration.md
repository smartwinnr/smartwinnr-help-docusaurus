> **At a glance** - Create and download AI-generated audio narrations for your training programs and educational content using the Audio Lab feature.

The **Audio Narration** feature in **Audio Lab** allows you to convert written content into natural-sounding AI-generated audio. You can customize the narration with various options, including title, duration, language, narrator voice, and background music. Once generated, you can preview and download the narration for use in training programs or other educational materials.

## When to use this
Use the Audio Narration feature when you want to:
- Create engaging audio content for training or educational purposes.
- Provide learners with an alternative to written text.
- Enhance your content with customizable audio narrations.

## Steps

### 1. Access Audio Lab
Log in to the **Editor Portal** and navigate to **Authoring Tools → Audio Lab**. Click **Create New Audio** to start.
![You can follow the below steps to create a audio narration in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-and-download-an-audio-narration-ms34tkly.png)

### 2. Select Audio Narration
Choose **Audio Narration** from the available options, which also include **Podcast** and **Interview**.
![You can follow the below steps to create a audio narration in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-and-download-an-audio-narration-ms34v4y9.png)

### 3. Configure Narration Information
Provide the basic details for your narration:
- **Title**: Enter a suitable title (required).
- **Description**: Optionally, add a description for context.
- **Narration Duration**: Select from preset options (5, 10, 15, or 20 minutes) or choose **Custom** to specify a duration between 5 and 60 minutes.
![You can follow the below steps to create a audio narration in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-and-download-an-audio-narration-ms34vg5o.png)

### 4. Configure Language and Narrator
Choose the language for the narration. Audio Lab supports multiple languages, including English, Spanish, French, German, and more. 

Configure the AI narrator by selecting:
- **Name**
- **Role (Narrator)**
- **Gender**
- **Voice**
- **Narrator Description**: Include details like speaking style and tone.
![You can follow the below steps to create a audio narration in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-and-download-an-audio-narration-ms34vxmg.png)

### 5. Select Background Music (Optional)
Enhance your narration by selecting background music from the **Music Track** dropdown. If no music is needed, select **None**.

### 6. Upload Supporting Resources
Upload the content you want the AI to narrate. Supported file types include PDF, JPG, PNG, MP3, and more, with a maximum file size of 20 MB.

### 7. Generate the Audio Narration
Review your settings and click **Start Processing**. Wait for Audio Lab to generate the narration; processing time varies based on duration and content.

### 8. Preview and Download
Once processing is complete, you can:
- Preview the generated narration to check voice, pacing, and quality.
- Download the narration for offline use or distribution.

## Tips
- Experiment with different narrator voices and styles to match your content and audience.
- Use background music to create a more engaging listening experience.

## Things to know
:::caution
Ensure that the uploaded files meet the supported formats and size limits to avoid processing errors.
:::
