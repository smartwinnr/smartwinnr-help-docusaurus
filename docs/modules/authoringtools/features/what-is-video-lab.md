> **At a glance** - Video Lab is an AI-powered authoring tool that transforms PDF-based learning content into customizable video tutorials with AI-generated voiceovers, slide transitions, and background music.

Video Lab helps you create engaging training and educational videos from PDF documents in just a few steps. Upload your content, configure video settings such as duration, language, AI voice, gender, and background music, and let AI generate a draft video. Before generating the final video, you can review and edit the script, voice, music, slide transitions, and other video elements to ensure the video is accurate and ready for use.

## When to use this
Use Video Lab when you want to:

* Convert PDF-based training or educational content into AI-generated video tutorials.
* Create engaging videos with AI voiceovers instead of recording narration manually.
* Customize videos by selecting the language, duration, voice, gender, slide transitions, and background music.
* Review and edit video content before generating the final version.
* Download AI-generated videos for use in training programs, educational content, or learning resources.

## Supported File Types
Video Lab supports the following file format for video generation:

* PDF documents

Maximum file upload size: 20 MB.

## How Video Lab Works
Video Lab processes the uploaded PDF and generates an AI-powered video based on the selected settings. Once processing is complete, you can open the draft video, edit the content, voice, music, and slide transitions, and then generate the final video.

## Review and Editing
Before generating the final video, you can:

* Edit the title and slide content.
* Change the AI voice and gender.
* Update the video language.
* Select or remove background music.
* Modify slide transitions.

## Tips
* Review the generated script and voice before generating the final video.
* Choose a voice and background music that best match your training content and audience.

## Things to know
Video Lab allows you to download generated videos after processing is complete. Videos cannot be exported directly to SmartFeed from Video Lab; download the video first, then upload it while creating a SmartFeed.
