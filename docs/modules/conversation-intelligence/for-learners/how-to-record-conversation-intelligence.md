> **At a glance** - Conversation Intelligence records live conversations, transcribes them, and generates AI-powered feedback. Use it to review what was discussed, assess performance, and check compliance.

Conversation Intelligence helps you capture a live conversation between a sales representative and a healthcare professional. After the recording is processed, you can review  analytics, and feedback in SmartWinnr.

Use it when you want to understand what was discussed, see how the conversation flowed, and review the interaction against configured compliance parameters.

## When to use this

Use Conversation Intelligence when you want to:

- Record live conversations between sales representatives and healthcare professionals.
- Transcribe recorded conversations for review.
- Generate AI feedback on the discussion and the representative's performance.
- Evaluate whether the conversation aligns with configured compliance parameters.

## Steps

### 1. Access the User Portal
Log in to **web.smartwinnr.com**.

### 2. Start a conversation
From the left-hand menu, click **Conversation Intelligence**. 

On the Home tab, click Start Conversation to begin a new conversation for the first time.
![Start the conversation](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mvqijyhn.png)

If you have recorded conversations before, the Home tab displays your previous conversations, along with their recording dates, duration, scores, and analysis status. To record another conversation, click **Start Conversation** at the bottom of the page.
![Start the conversation](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-record-conversation-intelligence-mvujlgzu.png)
### 3. Select the conversation details
On the setup page, review the conversation type. Select the language from the **Language** dropdown. This language is used for consent and analysis. Click **Continue to Consent** when you are ready.

![select the language and click continue to consent](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-record-conversation-intelligence-mvuk71cv.png)

### 4. Obtain HCP consent
Show the consent information to the healthcare professional and ask for consent to record the conversation. If they agree, click **I Consent** to continue.

If they decline, platform returns you to the **Conversation Intelligence** home page.

![Obtain HCP Consent](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mvqncei7.png)

### 5. Start recording
In the Record step, click the Microphone button to start recording the conversation.

![Recording the Conversation](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mvqnltlc.png)

Your browser may ask for microphone access. Choose **Allow while visiting the site** or **Allow this time** so SmartWinnr can record audio.
![permission to access the microphone](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mvqo0opf.png)

### 6. Manage the recording
Use **Pause** to stop temporarily. Use **Resume** to continue recording. When the conversation is complete, click **End Recording**.

![microphone access](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-record-conversation-intelligence-mvrycea7.png)A confirmation prompt appears. Click **End** to send the recording for analysis. Click **Keep recording** if you want to continue.

![confirmation pop-up](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mvqoc836.png)
You cannot resume after you end the recording.
### 7. Review the analysis
After you end the conversation, The platform processes the recording and generates feedback.

The Feedback Generation page shows the progress of uploading the recording, understanding the conversation, assessing engagement, generating feedback, and securing source data.

The process usually takes 3 to 5 minutes. When the feedback is ready, click **View Analytics** to review the results, or click **Return to Conversations** to go back.

![Kindly click on view analytics](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-record-conversation-intelligence-mvrxwl0y.png)
## Things to know

:::warning
Once you click **End**, you cannot resume that recording.
:::

:::note
Keep SmartWinnr open while you record so the audio can be captured and processed.
:::
