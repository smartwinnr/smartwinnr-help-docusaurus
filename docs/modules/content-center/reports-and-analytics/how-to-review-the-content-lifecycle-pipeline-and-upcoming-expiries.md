> **At a glance** - The Lifecycle tab shows where your content stands in the content lifecycle. Use it to spot items waiting for review, nearing expiry, or becoming outdated.

The Lifecycle tab in the Content Centre dashboard brings approval activity, lifecycle stages, expiry timing, and content freshness into one view. It helps you monitor content health and identify items that need attention.

## When to use this

Use the Lifecycle tab when you want to:

- Check content that is pending approval.
- Identify content expiring within 30 days.
- Monitor average approval turnaround time.
- See how content is distributed across lifecycle stages.
- Review items waiting for approval and how long they have been waiting.
- Check content approaching expiry.
- Review the age distribution of your content library.
- Identify the oldest content items that may need review.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Lifecycle tab

Log in to the Editor Portal, then go to **Content Management → Content Centre → Content Effectiveness**. Select the **Lifecycle** tab to see the current lifecycle status of your content.

![Open the Lifecycle tab](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwcw7z0.png)

### 2. Select a reporting period

Use the controls in the top-right corner to select the reporting period:

* Select **30D** to view data for the last 30 days.
* Select **90D** to view data for the last 90 days.
![30D and 90D reporting controls](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwcz9yt.png)
* Select the **Calendar** icon to choose a specific date range, then select **Apply**.
![Calendar date range control](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwd0sxr.png)

### 3. Review the summary tiles

The top of the **Lifecycle** tab shows four summary tiles. Use them to quickly spot content that needs attention.

- **Pending Approval** shows how many content items are waiting for review.
- **Expiring in 30 Days** shows how many items are expected to expire soon.
- **Expired (Unpublished)** shows how many items have passed their expiry date and are unpublished.
- **Avg Approval Turnaround** shows the average time between submission and approval.

![Lifecycle summary tiles](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwftjnn.png)

### 4. Review the Lifecycle Pipeline

The **Lifecycle Pipeline** shows where your content items sit in the lifecycle. It includes **Draft**, **Pending Approval**, **Published**, **Expired**, and **Archived**.

Use the counts in each stage to understand how your content is distributed. The pipeline also shows how many items were rejected during the selected period. Rejected items return to **Draft** so you can update them and submit them again.

![Lifecycle Pipeline](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwfy9di.png)

### 5. Review the Pending Review Queue

The **Pending Review Queue** lists items that are waiting for approval. It sorts the oldest items first, so you can follow up on the longest-waiting content.

The queue shows:

- **Content** - the content item's name.
- **Type** - the content type or file type.
- **Version** - the content version.
- **Submitted By** - the person who submitted the content.
- **Waiting** - how long the item has been waiting for approval.

![Pending Review Queue](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwg2l3k.png)

### 6. Review the Expiry Timeline

The **Expiry Timeline** shows published content that is approaching expiry. It groups items into **0–7 days**, **8–30 days**, **31–60 days**, and **61–90 days**.

Use **Expiring Soonest** to focus on the items with the nearest expiry date first.

![Expiry Timeline](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwg6vbz.png)

### 7. Review Content Freshness

The **Content Freshness** section shows how current your content library is based on content age. It groups items into **0–30 days**, **1–3 months**, **3–12 months**, and **Over 1 year**.

It also shows **Added last 30 days**, **Over 1 year old**, and the average library age. Use these metrics to understand how fresh your content library is overall.

### 8. Review Oldest Content
The Oldest Content (Candidates for Review) section lists older content items that may need to be reviewed.
Use this section to identify content that may need:
•	An update
•	A new version
•	A relevance check
•	Continued use
•	Archival, where appropriate
Reviewing older content regularly helps keep the content library current.

![Content Freshness](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muwgf6er.png)

## Things to know

:::note
Expired items are automatically unpublished and hidden from learners.
:::
