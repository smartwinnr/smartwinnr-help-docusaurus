> **At a glance** - The Content Centre Dashboard gives you a high-level view of your content library. Use it to check storage, usage, uploads, organization, and lifecycle status.

The Content Centre Dashboard helps you understand the overall state of your content library. It shows how much content you have, how it is used, and how it is organized across Content Centre.

Use the dashboard when you want a quick health check of your content library or need to spot areas that need attention.

## When to use this

Use the Content Centre Dashboard to:

- Get a quick overview of the content available in your organization.
- Check how much storage the content library is using.
- See how much content is actively used or unused.
- Review upload activity over a selected period.
- Find the most referenced content.
- See which users contribute the most content.
- Understand how content is distributed across folders.

# 1. Overview Tab
The Overview tab provides a snapshot of your Content Centre library, including content volume, storage usage, upload trends, and content references. It helps you understand how your content is organized, used, and contributed to across the selected reporting period.
### 1. Review the Overview tab

The **Overview** tab gives you a summary of your content library and its usage.

At the top of the page, review the summary tiles for **Total Content Items**, **Storage Used**, **Actively Used**, and **Unused Content**. These tiles help you quickly understand the size of the library and how much content is in use.

![Content summary](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzdss3g.png)

### 2. Check content types

Use **Content by Type** to see how many items you have by format, such as **PDF**, **Video**, **Image**, **Audio**, and **SCORM**.

This view helps you understand the mix of assets stored in Content Centre.

![Content by Type](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzdvhpi.png)

### 3. Review upload activity

Use **Upload Trend** to see how many content items were added during the selected period.

The chart breaks activity down by content type, so you can spot periods with higher or lower upload volume.

![Upload Trend](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzencq3.png)

### 4. Find frequently used content

Use **Most Referenced Content** to see the items used most often across modules.


![Most Referenced Content](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzerago.png)
You can also open individual content item details to review **content type**, **status**, **folder**, **number of module references**, **age**, and **added date**. Item details also show **Internal Views**, **External Views**, **Unique Viewers**, and **References**.
![information about individual content item](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzeugft.png)

### 5. See where content is used

Use **References by Module** to see which modules use content from the central repository.

Depending on your setup, this can include **SmartFeed**, **SCORM Course**, and **K-Hub Item**.

![References by Module](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzfjaba.png)

### 6. Review contributors and organization

Use **Top Contributors** to see which users uploaded the most content during the selected period.


![Top Contributors](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzgkjw1.png)
Use **Folder Distribution** to see how content is spread across folders in Content Centre.
![Top Contributors](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzgngav.png)
Use **Top Tags** to see the tags most often associated with content items.
![Top Tags](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-muzgsgix.png)

### 7. Switch between dashboard tabs

Use the dashboard tabs to focus on different parts of your content library:

- **Overview** shows the summary of your content library and usage.
- **Effectiveness** shows how content is viewed and used across folders and business units.
- **All Content** lets you browse content items and download the all-content report.
- **Lifecycle** shows the content lifecycle pipeline, pending reviews, and upcoming expiries.

### 8. Change the date range

Use the controls in the top-right corner to select the reporting period:

* Select **30D** to view data for the last 30 days.
* Select **90D** to view data for the last 90 days.
![Select 30D to view data for the last 30 days.Select 90D to view data for the last 90 days.](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-centre-dashboard-muzias8a.png)
* Select the **Calendar** icon to choose a specific date range, then select **Apply**.
![ custom date range to adjust the period](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-centre-dashboard-muzi80xs.png)
This helps you review content activity for the time frame that matters to you.

## Things to know

* The dashboard data reflects the **selected date range**. Change the date range to compare short-term and long-term activity.

# 2. Effectiveness
The Effectiveness Dashboard helps you analyze how content is viewed and used across your organization. It provides insights into content views, active viewers, folder usage, business-unit adoption, and most- and least-viewed content, helping you identify engagement trends and content that may need review or improvement.

 For more information, refer to [How to Measure Content Effectiveness and Usage](https://help.smartwinnr.com/modules/content-center/reports-and-analytics/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

# 3. All Content
The All Content Dashboard helps you search for content items, review content usage and details, filter results by type, status, or creator, and explore content highlights such as most referenced, most recent, and most popular items. You can also open individual content details and download the All Content report for further analysis.

For more information, refer to [How to Find a Content Item and Download the All Content Report](https://help.smartwinnr.com/modules/content-center/reports-and-analytics/how-to-find-a-content-item-and-download-the-all-content-report?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

# 4.  lifecycle
For more information, refer to [How to Review the Content Lifecycle Pipeline and Upcoming Expiries](https://help.smartwinnr.com/modules/content-center/reports-and-analytics/how-to-review-the-content-lifecycle-pipeline-and-upcoming-expiries?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The Lifecycle Dashboard helps you monitor the content lifecycle, including pending approvals, upcoming expiries, and content freshness. It provides insights into lifecycle stages, approval turnaround time, items awaiting review, and older content that may need updating, versioning, or archiving.
