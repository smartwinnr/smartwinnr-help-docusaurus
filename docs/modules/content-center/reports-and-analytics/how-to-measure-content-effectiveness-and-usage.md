> **At a glance** - The **Effectiveness** tab shows how content is used across your organization. Use it to compare views, viewers, folders, and business units.

The **Effectiveness** tab helps you measure content usage and engagement in one place. It shows summary metrics, folder-level usage, business-unit adoption, and a ranked **Content Consumption** table. You can also open any content item for a deeper breakdown of internal and external views.

## When to use this

Use the **Effectiveness** tab when you want to understand content performance, not just manage the library.

- Find content that gets frequent views.
- Spot content with little or no engagement.
- Compare usage across folders.
- Review adoption across business units.
- Separate internal consumption from external room views.
- Inspect a specific content item in detail.
- Check whether older content is still being viewed.
- Decide which content may need review, promotion, or updates.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Content Centre Dashboard
Go to **Content Management → Content Center → Content Center Dashboard** in the **Editor Portal**. Open the **Effectiveness** tab.

### 2. Review the summary metrics
Start with the header metrics to understand overall content usage. You can see the **Content Library**, **Assets Viewed**, **Internal Views**, **External Views**, and **Active Viewers**.
* **Content Library** – It shows the number of non-archived items.
* **Assets Viewed** – It shows the number of content assets that have been viewed.
* **Internal Views** – It shows views from users within the organization.
* **External Views** – It shows views from users outside the organization.
* **Active Viewers** – It shows the number of users actively viewing content.

### 3. Set the date range
Select the **Start Date** and **End Date**, then click **Apply** to define the period you want to analyze.
![select the date range](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muutafgk.png)

### 4. Filter the data
Use the available filters to narrow the data based on the audience you want to analyze. You can filter by **Business Unit**, **Country**, **Region**, **Job Title**, **Department**, and **Market**, then click **Apply Filters**.
![filter content useage](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muutd4wp.png)

### 5. Compare folder usage
Check **Usage by Content Folder** to see which folders receive the most views. Longer bars show higher usage, which helps you compare engagement across folders and identify low-activity areas.

### 6. Review business-unit adoption
Use **Usage & Adoption by Business Unit** to compare views and adoption across business units. The adoption table shows **Business Unit**, **Active**, **Total**, **Adoption**, and **Views**.
![content efectiveness](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muutj6zy.png)

### 7. Content Consumption

The **Content Consumption** section provides a ranked view of content usage. It includes two tabs:

* **Most Viewed**
* **Least Viewed**

The table combines **internal and external views** to show overall content consumption.

## Most Viewed

![most viewed](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muutzq5b.png)

The **Most Viewed** tab ranks content items with the highest number of views. Use it to identify content receiving the most engagement.

### Content Consumption columns

* **Content** – Shows the name of the content item.
* **Type** – Shows the content format, such as PDF, Video, or Image.
* **Total Views** – Shows the combined number of internal and external views.
* **Internal** – Shows views from internal surfaces such as SmartFeed, K-Hub, and SCORM.
* **External** – Shows views from external rooms.
* **Aging** – Shows how long the content item has been in the system.

The items and values change based on the selected filters and date range.

Select a row to open the full breakdown for the most-viewed content.

![Most viewed content details](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muuu4qvv.png)

The detailed view includes **Content Type, Publication Status, Folder, References, Aging, Internal Views, External Views, Unique Viewers, Internal Views by Surface, External Views by Room, Views by Business Unit, Top Internal Viewers**, and **External Viewers**.

## Least Viewed

![least viewed](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muuu01ua.png)

The **Least Viewed** tab identifies content items with the lowest number of views. Use it to find content with limited engagement that may need further review.

Low views do not necessarily mean that the content is ineffective. Consider the content's purpose, audience, age, and internal or external usage when reviewing these results.

Select a row to open the full breakdown for the least-viewed content.

![Least viewed content details](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-measure-content-effectiveness-and-usage-muuu58u9.png)

The detailed view includes **Content Type, Publication Status, Folder, References, Aging, Internal Views, External Views, Unique Viewers, Internal Views by Surface, External Views by Room, Views by Business Unit, Top Internal Viewers**, and **External Viewers**.

## Tips

* Use **Most Viewed** to identify content with high engagement.
* Use **Least Viewed** to identify content with low engagement.
* Compare **Internal Views** and **External Views** to understand where consumption is coming from.
* Review **Unique Viewers** along with total views to understand how widely the content is being viewed.

## Things to know

* Internal Views and External Views measure different types of content consumption, so review them separately.
* Low views do not always mean poor performance, as some content may serve a specific audience or use case.
* A higher view count does not always indicate broader adoption. Review Adoption and Unique Viewers for additional context.
