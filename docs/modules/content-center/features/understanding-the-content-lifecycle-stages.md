> **At a glance** - The **Content Lifecycle** shows how a content item moves through each stage, while the **lifecycle strip** on the **Content Item** page displays its current stage and the next available action.

The **Content Lifecycle** helps you manage content in a structured flow. Content moves through **Draft**, **In Review**, **Approved**, **Published**, and **Archived**. Each stage has a clear purpose, and the lifecycle strip on the content item page shows where the item is and what you can do next.

## When to use this

Use this article when you want to understand how content moves through review and publishing.

- Learn which stage a content item is in.
- See who takes action at each stage.
- Understand which actions are available next.
- Track content from creation through archival.

## 1. Start in Draft
When you create a content item, it starts in **Draft**. Use this stage to prepare the content before review.

### Manage the Draft

1. On the **Content Item** page, click the **hamburger menu** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Manage Lifecycle**.
![kindly click on manage lifecycle page](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-muf7rlte.png)
3. On the **Manage Lifecycle** page, under **Lifecycle Actions**, click **Submit for Review**.
![kindly click on submit for review](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufffali.png)
4. Select a **reviewer**.
5. Set **Remind the Reviewer**, add the required **Note for Reviewer**, and click **Submit for Review**.
![kindly fill the details and click on submit for review](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufb52q1.png)
### Status History

 section records each change to the content item, including **Change**, **Changed By**, **Changed On**, and **Comment**.

The **content creator or editor** prepares the content and submits it for review.

### Archive Rules

You can set **Archive Rules** to automatically archive published content based on a defined schedule. You can choose to:

* **Never archive automatically**
* Archive after a **set period** from the date it is published
* Archive on a **specific date**
* Set a **reminder** before the content is archived
* Enable **Hold off while it is still in use** to prevent archival while the content is being referenced.

Draft → Submit for Review

## 2. Review the content
After submission, the content moves to **In Review**. The selected reviewer checks the content and decides whether it is ready to move forward.

Reviewer:
The page shows **Approve** and **Request Changes** for the reviewer.
![reviewer reviews the draft![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufbeq41.png)](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufbblnb.png)
The **Reviewer** is responsible for reviewing the content and deciding whether it is ready for approval.

If the reviewer wants to approve the item, they add a **Comment** and click **Approve**.
![kindly write the comment and click on approval](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufc4qkx.png)
If the reviewer wants changes to be made, they can click **Request Changes** and specify what needs to be updated.
![requesting changes](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufc7qo2.png)
 If the reviewer requests changes, the **Rejected** status is recorded in **Status History**, and the item moves back to the **Draft** stage. The content can then be updated and **submitted for review again**.
![the item moves back to the Draft stage.](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufbf8qa.png)
In Review → Approve or Request Changes

## 3. Approve the content
When the reviewer approves the content, it moves to **Approved Stage**. The content is ready to be published and used in supported modules.
![reviewer approved the content](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufbfwkc.png)
The **Reviewer** approves the content, and the **editor or content manager** publishes the approved content.

Approved → Publish or Send Back
## 4. Published
Click **Publish** to publish the content.
![kindly click on publish to publish the content](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufd1pcs.png)
The **Send Back** option allows the approved content to be returned for further changes instead of publishing it. Use **Send Back** when the content needs additional updates before it can be published.

Enter the required **Comment** explaining why the status is changing, and click **Publish**.
![kindly write the comment and click on publish](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufdezjc.png)
The content is now **published**.

![the content is now published](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufd08oi.png)
Only published content items can be attached to modules such as SmartFeed, K-Hub, and SmartPath.

Published → Archive
## 5. Archived

Once published, you can **archive** the content when it is no longer required for active use. Archived content cannot be attached to **new modules**, while any existing module attachments remain intact.

Click **Archive** if the content is no longer in use.
![kindly click on archive](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufe1y8z.png)
Enter a specific **Comment** explaining why the content is being archived, then click **Archive** to archive the content.

![kindly enter the comment and click on archive](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-muffrmvh.png)
Archived → Republish

The archived content can be **republished** when it needs to be made available for use again. Click **Republish** to make the content available again.
![kindly click on republish](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufecmrc.png)

Enter the required **Comment** explaining why the content is being republished, and click **Republish** to make the content available for use again.
![lindly click on republish to make the content available for use again.](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-content-lifecycle-stages-mufej9r9.png)

**Note:** Each lifecycle stage has specific actions and responsibilities. Follow the appropriate stage and action to ensure the content moves through the lifecycle correctly.
