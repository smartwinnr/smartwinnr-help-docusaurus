> **At a glance** – Content may not be available for attachment to a module if it has not been published. The content’s lifecycle stage determines whether it is available to use in modules such as SmartFeed, KHub, or SCORM courses.

Content Centre manages content through different lifecycle stages: Draft, In Review, Approved, Published, and Archived. Content that has not reached the Published stage, or has been archived, may not be available when you try to attach it to a module.

## When to use this

Use this when:

* A content item does not appear while creating or editing a module.

* You cannot find an item in the content selection list.

* You are unsure whether the content’s lifecycle stage affects its availability.

* Previously available content is no longer available for attachment.

## Why content cannot be attached

### 1. The content is still in Draft

Content in the **Draft** stage has not completed the publishing process. Therefore, it may not appear in the list of content available for attachment to a module.

### 2. The content is In Review

Content in the **In Review** stage is awaiting review. It is not yet published and may not be available for attachment.

### 3. The content is Approved but not Published

Approval does not mean the content is already published. Content in the **Approved** stage must complete the publishing process before it becomes available for use.

### 4. The content is Archived

Archived content is no longer available for new attachments where published content is required. If an item was previously available but is now archived, its lifecycle stage may explain why you cannot select it.

### 5. The content is already Published

If the content is in the **Published** stage but still does not appear, the issue may not be related to its lifecycle stage. Check whether you are searching for the correct content item and whether it is available in the module you are setting up.

## Things to know

* The lifecycle strip on the content item page displays its current stage and the next available action.

* Content must be published to be available for attachment where the module requires published content.

* Archived content may need to be republished before it can be used for new attachments.

* If published content is still missing, check the content selection list and the module you are working with.
