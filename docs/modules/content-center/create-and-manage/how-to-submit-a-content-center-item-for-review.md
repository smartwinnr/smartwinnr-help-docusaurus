> **At a glance** - Send a Content Center item to review from **Manage Lifecycle**. The reviewer gets an email right away with the item details and a link to open it.

Open a Content Center item, choose **Manage Lifecycle**, and submit it for review. You can pick a reviewer, add a comment, and send the request in one flow.

The reviewer receives a **Content Submitted for Your Review** email with the item name, version, your note, and a **Review now** button. They can open the item from that email and choose **Approve** or **Reject** in the review banner.

## When to use this

Use this when you want someone to review a Content Center item before it moves forward.

- You need feedback on a new or updated item.
- You want the reviewer notified right away.
- You want to include a note with the review request.
- You want to track review requests from the **My Reviews** tab.

## Before you start

- The content lifecycle must be turned on for your organization.
- Email notifications must be enabled.
- The item must be in a folder the reviewer can access.

## Steps

### 1. Open the item lifecycle
Open the Content Center item you want reviewed, then select **Manage Lifecycle**.

### 2. Start the review request
Choose **Submit for Review**.

### 3. Choose a reviewer
Select a reviewer from the dropdown list, then add a comment.

The list only shows people who can access and edit the item's folder.

### 4. Send the request
Submit the review request.

The reviewer gets the email right away after the request is saved.

## Things to know

:::note
The **My Reviews** tab shows items assigned to you for review.
:::

:::note
The item page can be opened from the review email by the reviewer.
:::

:::warning
If the reviewer list is empty, make sure the item is in a folder they can access.
:::
