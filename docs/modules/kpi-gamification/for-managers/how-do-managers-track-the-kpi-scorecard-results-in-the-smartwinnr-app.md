> **At a glance** - This article outlines how managers can track KPI performance for their teams in their SmartWinnr account.

This section allows managers to monitor the KPI performance of their direct reports and teams. It provides a consolidated view of target achievement, individual KPI performance, and team-level performance.

Managers can use this view to quickly identify how their team members are progressing against their assigned targets and determine areas that may require additional attention or coaching.

![KPI data page](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt41ign9.png)

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to:
- Track the KPI performance of your team members.
- Identify areas where additional coaching may be needed.
- View consolidated KPI data for better decision-making.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your SmartWinnr account
Log in to your SmartWinnr account and switch to **Manager view**. Then, go to the left menu and select **KPI**.

![Go to the left menu > KPI.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt41r3e5.png)

### 2. View KPI scorecards
This opens the KPI data page, where you can see the KPIs of your direct reportees and other reportees in the hierarchy.

![KPI of reportees](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt41ueyg.png)

### 3. Understand User and Manager cards
The KPI scorecard of a reportee who is not a manager differs from the scorecard of a reportee who is a manager. Let's call the scorecard of the manager the **Manager Card** and the scorecard of a non-manager the **User Card**.

**User card**: The scorecard of a reportee who is not a manager (who has only the user role) is called a user card. Here is how it looks:

![User card: The scorecard of a reportee who is not a manager (who has only the user role) is called a user card. Here is how it looks:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt41zch0.png)

The user card shows data for the user against each KPI. Click on the **View All** option to see all the KPIs available under the selected scorecard.

**Manager card**: The scorecard of a reportee who is a manager (who has both user and manager roles) is called a manager card. Here is how it looks:

![Manager card](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt42ovsh.png)

The manager card shows the performance of all the team members reporting to this particular manager.

### 4. View top performers
You can also view the **top performers** in a manager's scorecard. Click on the **View All** option to see how much each team member has scored against each KPI (available in the selected scorecard). You can filter and view the achieved value for each KPI by clicking on the KPI dropdown at the top right corner.

![top performers](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt42smbu.png)

### 5. Drill down into team member scores
In a manager card, you can drill down further to view the scores of team members by clicking on the arrow at the top right corner of the scorecard. When you click the arrow, the first scorecard you see will be the manager's scorecard, followed by all of the reportees' scorecards below that.

![In a manager card, you can drill in further to view the scores of his team members by clicking on the arrow at the top right corner of the s](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt42j599.png)

### 6. Use filters for data analysis
You have multiple filters available on this page to filter the data for KPIs from specific scorecards and specific date ranges.

![KPI scorecard filters](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-managers-track-the-kpi-scorecard-results-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mt42zirt.png)

**Scorecards**: Here you can filter and choose to display data from any specific scorecard. By default, the system displays data from the first scorecard in the list.

**Date Range**: Here you can choose to display the data for a specific frequency or duration of the scorecard.
