> **At a glance** - KPI Reports offer insights into participant performance in KPI gamification, helping you track engagement and effectiveness.

The KPI Reports provide detailed analytics of the participants in the KPI gamification. You can view these reports to analyze performance and engagement metrics effectively.

## When to use this
Use KPI Reports when you need to:
- Assess participant engagement in your KPI gamification initiatives.
- Analyze performance trends over time.
- Identify areas for improvement in your gamification strategy.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to KPI Reports
Go to **EDITOR > PERFORMANCE > KPI Scorecards > Reports**. This will display the KPI Reports page.
