> **At a glance** - Achievements in SmartWinnr are rewards that users receive upon completing specific tasks, helping to motivate and recognize user participation.

An **Achievement** in SmartWinnr is a form of recognition or reward that a user receives after successfully completing a defined task, activity, or achievement criteria. Achievements can be used to recognize user participation, celebrate accomplishments, and motivate users to complete their assigned activities.

## Types of Achievements
SmartWinnr supports two types of achievements:

- **Badges**: Digital rewards that recognize a user's accomplishment or completion of a defined activity or milestone.
- **Certificates**: Formal recognition provided to users upon meeting the criteria defined for the certificate.

Achievements play a crucial role in enhancing user engagement and fostering a sense of accomplishment within the platform.
