> **At a glance** - Competitions in SmartWinnr allow you to engage teams through training challenges, fostering a healthy competitive environment.

A Competition in SmartWinnr is a gamification feature that allows organizations to engage users through friendly competition. It motivates them to complete learning activities and achieve defined performance goals. Competitions can be configured for individual participants or teams and can run for a defined period. You can combine different activities and performance measures within a competition, allowing participants to earn scores based on their engagement and performance.

## When to use this
Use Competitions to:

- Increase engagement with learning activities.
- Encourage participation and completion of assigned tasks.
- Motivate users to improve their performance.
- Create healthy competition among individuals or teams.
- Combine multiple learning and performance activities in a single gamified experience.
- Recognize and showcase high-performing participants and teams.

## What can you include in a Competition?
SmartWinnr allows you to include different types of activities and performance components in a competition, such as:

- **Quizzes**: Test participants' knowledge and award scores based on their performance.
- **Surveys**: Encourage participants to provide feedback or complete required surveys.
- **SmartFeeds**: Include SmartFeed content as part of the competition to encourage users to consume and engage with learning content.
- **Scorecards**: Track participants' performance against defined KPIs and targets.
- **Challenges**: Add competitive challenges between individuals, teams, or other defined participants.
- **Other learning or performance activities**: Combine relevant activities based on the objective of the competition.

This flexibility allows you to create competitions that combine learning, engagement, and performance rather than relying on a single activity.

## Leaderboards
Competition results can be displayed through leaderboards, allowing participants to track their progress and see how they rank against others. You can create leaderboards at different levels, including:

- **Participant leaderboard**: Rank individual participants based on their scores or performance.
- **Team leaderboard**: Rank teams based on their combined or average performance.

Leaderboards help create healthy competition, recognize top performers, and encourage participants to improve their performance throughout the competition.

## Tips
- A competition can be designed around a specific business or learning objective. 
- You can combine quizzes, SmartFeeds, surveys, scorecards, challenges, and other activities to create an engaging experience that best suits your requirements.
