> **At a glance** - KPI gamification enhances employee engagement by turning performance metrics into games, fostering competition, and recognizing achievements.

KPI gamification transforms organizational success metrics into engaging games. Key performance indicators (KPIs) are established at the organizational level and tailored for each business unit, function, and individual employee. By gamifying these KPIs, you can motivate your team to achieve higher targets and effectively communicate corporate goals to employees, aligning them with those objectives.

SmartWinnr offers a comprehensive suite of gamification capabilities that enable various types of gaming, improving employee engagement and performance.

## When to use this
KPI gamification is beneficial in several scenarios:
- To enhance motivation among employees by turning KPIs into competitive games.
- To create a fun and engaging environment that drives performance.
- To recognize achievements through awards and leaderboards.
- To maintain real-time performance tracking without complex spreadsheets.

## Steps

### 1. Engage different roles through gamification
Gamification provides an effective method to measure employee performance and the activities that drive that performance. Instead of relying on weekly email updates or scorecards filled with numbers, gamification turns KPIs into games that drive behavior and motivate employees to achieve their goals.

### 2. Create awards to motivate everyone
With gamification, you can set up multiple award categories to recognize achievements and improvements across various KPIs or employee roles. It allows for tiered awards for individuals and teams, enabling more players to receive recognition. This approach keeps participants engaged and excited throughout the competition by creating multiple ways to win.

### 3. Embrace a competitive spirit
Salespeople are inherently competitive. Gamification leverages this spirit by creating a challenging and gamified work environment. Additionally, millennials enjoy playing games, earning points, and receiving badges, which fosters recognition among peers and seniors.

### 4. Keep the competition alive with updated scores
Leaderboards encourage players to focus on all aspects of the game. Gamification uses points assigned to each KPI, which are earned by individual players and teams, to determine weekly standings and rankings for specific KPIs.

### 5. Increase motivation with leaderboards, badges, and awards
KPI gamification allows players to compete with colleagues in a friendly manner, providing them with bragging rights for their achievements. It utilizes leaderboards, badges, and awards to recognize accomplishments and drive players' motivation to deliver their best results.

### 6. Monitor performance without hassle
KPI gamification enhances performance while making the experience enjoyable, eliminating the burden of managing complex spreadsheets.
