> **At a glance** - Identifying KPIs is essential for driving effective KPI gamification. This article outlines how to select the right KPIs for your organization.

The first step in setting up a KPI gamification is to identify the KPIs that will be used to drive the game. You can select any KPI used in your organization to measure success. Here are some examples:

* Number of calls made
* Number of new signups
* Average call duration
* Percentage of sales quota achieved
* Total revenue

By carefully selecting the KPIs, you can create a gamification strategy that motivates and engages your team effectively.
