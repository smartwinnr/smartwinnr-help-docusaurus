> **At a glance** - KPI definitions help you configure the performance metrics used in SmartWinnr. By defining a KPI’s name, measurement settings, categories, and display properties, you can organize and track the metrics that support your organization’s business goals and KPI gamification strategy.

## What Is a KPI Definition?

A **Key Performance Indicator (KPI)** is a measurable value used to track performance against a business objective. In SmartWinnr, a KPI definition contains the information and settings associated with a performance metric.

Thus, the first step in setting up a KPI gamification is to identify the KPIs that will be used to drive the game. You can select any KPI used in your organization to measure success. Here are some examples:

* Number of calls made
* Number of new signups
* Average call duration
* Percentage of sales quota achieved
* Total revenue

By carefully selecting the KPIs, you can create a gamification strategy that motivates and engages your team effectively.

### How to view existing KPIs defined in your organisation

To view the KPIs configured for your organization:

1. Navigate to **KPI Definitions**

Go to **Admin > KPI Settings > Definitions**

2. View **KPI Definitions**

The KPI Definitions page displays the KPIs available for the selected division. Use the available filters to KPIs defined based on:

- Division
- Metric Type 
- KPI group

![KPI list](/img/helpscout/authored/define-kpis-mv549ht2.png)

Click on a **KPI name** to view its detailed configuration.

### Creating a KPI Definition

If a KPI does not already exist, you can create a new KPI by clicking **Add New KPI**. 

[Click here to learn more on how to create a KPI](https://help.smartwinnr.com/modules/kpi-gamification/create-and-manage/how-to-create-kpi)
