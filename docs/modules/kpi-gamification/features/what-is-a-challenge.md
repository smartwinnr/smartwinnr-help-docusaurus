> **At a glance** - A Challenge in SmartWinnr allows participants to compete against each other in structured competitions, fostering engagement and performance improvement.

Challenge is defined as '_a call to someone to participate in a competitive situation or fight to decide who is superior in terms of ability or strength._' Challenges are a well-established concept in video games, where one player challenges another. This adds a layer of competitiveness and enthusiasm to the game.

SmartWinnr adopts this concept, enabling you to create challenges among individuals, teams, or even managers. 

## When to Use Challenges

Timing is crucial in any gamification strategy. There are various scenarios when introducing challenges can be particularly effective:

* **Re-engage Participants:** Challenges play a vital role when enthusiasm in a game begins to wane. If the game has been ongoing for some time and participants are losing interest, introducing challenges can reignite their engagement.
  
* **Maintain Engagement During Slower Periods:** Challenges are especially popular during the first half of the month. Sales representatives often strive to maximize their sales during the last week of the month or quarter. Challenges can help keep them focused during the slower parts of the month or quarter.
  
* **Motivate Individual Participants:** Use individual challenges to encourage competition among participants. For instance, when a new person joins the team, you can pit them against another new employee from a different team.
  
* **Engage Larger Teams:** Team challenges are effective for motivating larger groups. For example, during a new product launch, team challenges can stimulate participation and performance.
  
* **Drive Performance Across Managers or Territories:** Manager challenges can be employed to boost sales in specific regions or territories. This creates competition among managers, encouraging them to focus on performance metrics relevant to their areas.

## Understanding Challenges in SmartWinnr

A Challenge in SmartWinnr is a gamification feature that allows participants to compete against each other in a structured, friendly competition. Participants can be paired as individuals, teams, or managers, depending on the objective of the challenge.

Challenges help create a sense of competition and encourage participants to stay engaged, improve their performance, and work toward defined goals. SmartWinnr supports different types of challenges, allowing you to choose the format that best fits your engagement or business requirements.

## Tips for Implementing Challenges

* **Choose the Right Challenge Type:** Select the challenge type based on the participants you want to compare and the business objective you aim to achieve.
* **Monitor Engagement Levels:** Keep an eye on participation and enthusiasm levels to determine the best times to introduce challenges.
* **Leverage Challenges for Team Building:** Use challenges as a tool for team building and fostering collaboration among team members.

By effectively utilizing challenges, you can enhance engagement, boost performance, and create a more dynamic and competitive environment within your organization.
