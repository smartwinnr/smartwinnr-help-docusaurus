> **At a glance** - View your KPI scorecard to track your performance against assigned KPIs over various timeframes.

A Scorecard is a collection of KPIs being tracked. Scorecards can be Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or for a custom date range. You can monitor how the scorecard changes as you complete your activities. Follow these steps to view a KPI Scorecard:

## When to use this
Use the KPI scorecard to:
- Track your performance against assigned KPIs.
- Review your progress over different timeframes.
- Compare your actual achievements with the targets set for you.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your SmartWinnr account
Navigate to **Dashboard > Performance** tab. Alternatively, go to **Left Menu > Scorecards** under the Performance section.

![Left menu > scorecard](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-the-kpi-scorecard-mt3vz1ao.png)

### 2. View the scorecard
You will see a scorecard with a bar graph that shows your performance in each KPI assigned to you. You can choose to view the monthly or weekly scorecard by selecting the respective tabs. The frequency tabs available in your account depend on the scorecards assigned to you in SmartWinnr.

![You will see a scorecard with a bar graph that shows your performance in a particular KPI. You can choose to view the monthly scorecard](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-the-kpi-scorecard-mt3xgpuw.png)

### 3. Understand the scorecard values
The visual bar helps you quickly understand whether you are below, meeting, or exceeding the expected target for each KPI.

- **Target**: This is the target set for you to achieve against each KPI.

![KPI target](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-the-kpi-scorecard-mt3yu7ge.png)

- **Actual**: This blue bar shows your current achievement so far.

![Actual for the KPI](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-the-kpi-scorecard-mt3yyfsm.png)

Note: The KPIs displayed on the Scorecard depend on the KPIs assigned to you in SmartWinnr.

### 4. Review the Actual and Target section
The Actual and Target section provides a detailed view of your KPI performance for different months/weeks or the specific frequency of the KPI.

Each KPI is displayed along with its actual achievement and target value in the format: **Actual / Target**.

![Actual and target section](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-the-kpi-scorecard-mt3z05yg.png)

For example:
- Average Handle Time: 12/15
- Knowledge Score: 68/100

This format allows you to easily compare what you have achieved against the target defined for each KPI.

You can use the navigation arrows to view the actual and target values for different months.
