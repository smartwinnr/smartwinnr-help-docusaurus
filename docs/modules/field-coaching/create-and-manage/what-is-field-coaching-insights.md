> **At a glance** - Coaching Insights helps you review field coaching data in one place. Use filters to narrow the results, then explore competency history, identify gaps, and review coaching performance charts.

Coaching Insights shows field coaching data in a single view. You can filter the results by coaching name, manager, business unit, and date range. You can also review competency history, identify gaps, and see the most coached competency groups and competencies.

## When to use this
Use Coaching Insights when you want to review coaching activity across teams or time periods.

- Check coaching data for a specific manager.
- Focus on one business unit.
- Review performance within a date range.
- Compare competency trends across coaching groups.

## Steps

### 1. Open Coaching Insights
Go to **COACHING > Field Coaching > Coaching Insights**.

![steps to go coaching insights](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mumbvxq4.png)

### 2. Filter the coaching data
Use the available filters to narrow the results. You can filter by **Coaching Name**, **Manager**, **Business Unit**, and **Date Range**.

![filter part](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mumc2j3p.png)

### 3. Review competency history
Open **Competency History** to see ratings for competencies in the selected competency group. Use **Competency Group** and **Frequency** to shape the chart.

![competecy history](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mumcb4f3.png)

### 4. Choose a frequency
Select one of the available frequencies: **Daily**, **Weekly**, **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Annually**. The chart updates to match your selection.

### 5. Download the chart
Download the graph as a report or image when you need to share or save the results.

### 6. Identify gaps
Open **Identify Gap** to review gaps by **Competency Group** and **View by User Tag**.

![identify gap](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mumcnnlq.png)

### 7. Review coaching performance
**Most Coached Competency Groups** and **Most Coached Competencies** show the most coached competency groups in the selected filters and the most coached competencies in the selected filters.

![Most Coached Competency Groups and Most Coached Competencies i](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mumcwv6w.png)

## Things to know

:::note
The charts reflect the filters you select. Change the filters to focus on a different manager, business unit, or date range.
:::

:::note
You can use the chart details to compare coaching activity across topics and competency groups.
:::
