> **At a glance** - Only users with ADMIN roles can create field coaching templates.

Field coaching templates allow you to standardize coaching sessions and ensure consistency across your organization. If you have the necessary ADMIN privilege, you can create these templates through the Admin section of your SmartWinnr dashboard.

## When to use this
Use field coaching templates to:
- Standardize coaching practices across your team.
- Ensure all coaches follow the same guidelines.
- Streamline the coaching process for new hires.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Admin section
Open your SmartWinnr dashboard and locate the **Admin** section in the left menu.

### 2. Access Field Coaching Settings
Under the **Admin** section, find and click on the **Field Coaching Settings** option.

### 3. Create a new template
Click on the **Templates** link to start creating a new field coaching template.

### 4. Fill in the template details
Provide all necessary information for your coaching template, including title, objectives, and any specific guidelines.

### 5. Save the template
Once you have filled in all the required fields, click the **Save** button to create your field coaching template.

## Tips
- Review existing templates to ensure consistency before creating a new one.
- Use clear and concise language in your templates for better understanding.
- Regularly update templates based on feedback from coaches and participants.
