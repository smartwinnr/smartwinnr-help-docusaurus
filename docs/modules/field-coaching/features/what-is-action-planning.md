Action Planning helps reps build quarterly plans from past performance data and track them through review. It gives managers visibility into each plan, along with comments, inputs, and status updates.

Use it when you want a structured planning process for a quarter or year. You can organize the work into stages, review progress over time, and keep reps and managers aligned.

## When to use this

Use Action Planning when you want to:

- Help reps plan the next quarter using prior sales or visit data.
- Guide planning through defined stages and sections.
- Let managers review plans and add feedback.
- Track plan progress across users, teams, or periods.
- View planning activity in the Annual Planner and reports.

## Before you start

Your organization needs an Action Planning process configured.

## How Action Planning Works

Action Planning can be configured as a multi-step process, allowing organizations to define the stages a rep needs to complete as part of their quarterly planning.

A typical process can include:

**Review Previous Performance → Create Current Quarter Plan → Manager Review → Rep Complete Actions and update in the plan → Periodic Reviews**

The information and steps included in the plan depend on how the Action Planning process is configured.

Once a rep creates their plan, the respective manager can track its progress and participate in the review process.

Managers can:
- Track the status of an Action Plan.
- Review the rep's plan.
- Add comments and inputs.
- Provide feedback during the planning or review stages.
- Update the Action Plan status as the rep progresses.

This creates a continuous interaction between the rep and manager throughout the planning period.

## Things to know

:::note
The stages, sections, and approval flow depend on how your organization configures Action Planning.
:::

:::note
Managers can review plans, add comments, and update status as reps move through the process.
:::

:::note
The Annual Planner gives you a broader view of planning activity across the year.
:::
