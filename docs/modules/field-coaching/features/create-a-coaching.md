> **At a glance** - Field Coaching gives you a structured way to observe performance, capture feedback, and track improvement over time.

Field Coaching in SmartWinnr helps you structure and evaluate coaching interactions between managers and their team members. You can use it when a manager needs to observe an employee’s performance, provide feedback, assess specific skills or behaviors, and track improvement over time.

A Field Coaching form gives you a consistent framework for these interactions. Instead of relying only on informal feedback, you can define what the manager should observe, capture responses, and use a scoring model to measure performance.

## When to use this

Use Field Coaching when coaching needs to happen regularly and follow a consistent structure. For example, you can use it to:

- Observe an employee during a customer or field interaction.
- Assess specific skills, behaviors, or competencies.
- Identify areas where an employee needs improvement.
- Provide structured feedback after an interaction.
- Track coaching outcomes and performance over time.
- Ensure managers follow a common coaching framework across teams.

## Who is involved in Field Coaching?

Different users can be involved at different stages of the Field Coaching lifecycle.

### Editor

The Editor is responsible for designing and creating the coaching. This includes defining the coaching structure, sections, questions, and scoring approach based on the organization's requirements.

### Manager/Coach

The manager or coach uses the created coaching with their team members. They complete the coaching form based on their observations and provide the required assessment or feedback.

### Learner/Coachee

Depending on the coaching setup, the employee being coached participates in the coaching interaction and can receive feedback based on the assessment.

The exact roles involved can vary depending on how the organization uses Field Coaching and how the coaching is assigned.
