> **At a glance** - Quizzes can automatically reassign based on score thresholds. This article explains how it works and what learners should do.

Quizzes may automatically reassign if your score falls below the set pass threshold. This feature ensures learners can retake the quiz to achieve a passing score. If you see a completed quiz reappear in your list, it indicates that your score did not meet the required threshold.

## When to use this
You may encounter automatic reassignment in the following scenarios:
- You completed a quiz but scored below the pass threshold.
- You want to understand why a quiz has reappeared in your list.
- You need guidance on retaking the quiz.

## Steps

### 1. Understand Automatic Reassignment
If a quiz returns to your list, it means your score was below the established pass threshold. This is a normal part of the learning process, allowing you to improve your understanding.

### 2. Know the Attempt Limits
Be aware that there may be limits on how many times you can attempt the quiz. Check your organization’s policies regarding quiz retakes.

### 3. Decide When to Retake the Quiz
You can choose to retake the quiz immediately or later. Consider reviewing the material before attempting again to increase your chances of passing.

For detailed instructions on how to retake a quiz, refer to the [Retake Quiz article](#).
