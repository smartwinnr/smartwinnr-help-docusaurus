> **At a glance** - Use the Quizzes section in the Manager view to monitor your team's quiz progress and completion status.

The Quizzes section allows managers to track the quiz participation and completion status of their team members. This helps you identify who has completed their assignments and who may need follow-up support.

## When to use this

Use the quiz performance view to:

- Monitor quiz progress across your team.
- Identify users who have completed their quizzes.
- Recognize team members who may need reminders or additional support.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to SmartWinnr

Access the SmartWinnr Mobile App or Web App using your login credentials.

### 2. Switch to Manager view

Change your view from User to Manager.

### 3. Select Quizzes

From the left navigation menu, click on **Quizzes**.
![Image shows the quizzes](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-quiz-performance-for-your-team-ms8qh2zc.png)

### 4. Review quiz performance

The Quiz Performance page will display the quiz-wise status of users in your team. You can see whether each user has:

- **Completed** – The user has completed the quiz.
- **In Progress** – The user has started the quiz but has not yet completed it.
- **Not Started** – The user has not yet attempted the quiz.
![This images shows the status of users](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-quiz-performance-for-your-team-ms8qi5so.png)

This overview helps you quickly identify learners who have completed their assignments and those who may need reminders or additional support.
