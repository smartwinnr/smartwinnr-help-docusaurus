> **At a glance** - This article explains how to track the status and scores of quizzes assigned to your team, providing detailed steps for effective management.

You can easily find the quiz status of your team for each quiz assigned. This allows you to monitor progress and scores, ensuring that your team stays on track.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to:
- Check the completion status of quizzes assigned to your team.
- Monitor individual scores for team members.
- Filter quiz results based on completion status.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Quizzes Section
In the left menu, click on **Quiz**, then select the **Quizzes** tab. 

This will display a list of quizzes assigned to your team. Active quizzes appear at the top, along with the remaining active days for each quiz. Select the desired quiz from the list.

![List of quizzes assigned to your team](/img/helpscout/authored/finding-status-and-scores-for-quizzes-assigned-to-your-team-ms8lg8tz.png)

### 2. View Completion Status
Once you select a quiz, the page will open showing the completion status for each team member for that particular quiz. On the right-hand side, you will see the completion status for each member as "Not Started", "Pending", or "Completed". You can also find the scores obtained by team members who have already completed the quiz.

![Completion status for each team member](/img/helpscout/authored/finding-status-and-scores-for-quizzes-assigned-to-your-team-ms8li1tj.png)

### 3. Filter Completion Status
To filter the results by various completion statuses, click on the down-arrow at the top-right corner. This will provide you with filter options such as "All", "Not Started", "In Progress", and "Completed".

![Filter options for completion status](/img/helpscout/authored/finding-status-and-scores-for-quizzes-assigned-to-your-team-ms8ljgaw.png)
