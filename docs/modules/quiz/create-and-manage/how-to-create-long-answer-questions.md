> **At a glance** - This article explains how to create 'Long Answer' questions in the SmartWinnr platform, detailing each step involved in the process.

Long answer questions are generally open-ended questions that require participants to construct a response. These questions allow for a more detailed and thoughtful reply.

## When to use this

Use 'Long Answer' questions when you want participants to provide comprehensive responses. This question type is suitable for assessments that require critical thinking or elaboration on a topic.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Question Bank

Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > Questions and Quizzes > Question Bank**.

![Here is how you can create a 'Long answer' question.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-1.jpg)

### 2. Add a New Question

Click on the **Add Question** button at the **top right corner**.

![](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-2.jpg)

### 3. Select Question Type

On the **Create new Question** form, select the **Question Type** as '**Long Answer**'.

![](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-3.jpg)

### 4. Enter Your Question

Enter your question in the **Question** field.

![](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-4.png)

### 5. Specify Maximum Points

Specify the maximum points that can be awarded for the question in the **Maximum Allowed Points** field. This setting allows you to define the highest number of points that can be assigned to the question.

![](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-numeric-type-questions-5.jpg)

### 6. Add Correct Answer Feedback (Optional)

Use **Correct Answer Feedback** if you want to add additional information to a question.

![Use ‘Correct Answer Feedback’ if you want to add additional information to a question. (Optional)](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-missing-words-question-8.png)

### 7. Include Reviewer Notes

You can add any additional feedback on the question or answer options.

![You can add any additional feedback on the question or answer options.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-missing-words-question-9.png)

### 8. Assign Categories

Every question must be assigned at least **one category**. Categories are predefined by your administrator. You may select multiple categories if applicable.

![Every question must be assigned at least one category. Categories are predefined by your administrator.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-a-drag-and-drop-into-image-question-7.jpg)

### 9. Copying Text from Word Documents

Microsoft Word adds certain hidden characters and markups to every document. If you directly copy text from a Word document, these hidden artifacts can create a problem, especially on mobile devices. To avoid this issue, if you are copying any question from a Word document, please follow these steps:

- Copy the text from the Word Document into Notepad. This removes all hidden markups.
- Then copy the text from Notepad into the SmartWinnr **Question** box.

### 10. Add Various Elements to the Question

You can have various elements like Simple Text, Videos, Images, Links, or Audio for the question.

**Text**
If it's just a text question, then you can simply write it in the box.

**Videos**
You can pose a question through a video as well. Choose videos of products, product demonstrations, customer feedback, customer stories, and ask questions based on that. Click on the video icon. You can add your own videos to the question or videos from YouTube, Box, and Brainshark.

[Learn how to add different videos to a question](/modules/quiz/features/how-to-add-a-video-to-a-question)

**Images**
Images or pictures can be sent with a question by clicking on the image icon. Then click on the **Choose File** button to select the required files from your computer.

[Learn how to add an image to the question](/modules/quiz/features/how-to-add-an-image-to-the-question)

**Links**
You can also insert a link with the question by clicking on the links icon.

[Learn how to embed a link into the question](/modules/quiz/features/how-to-embed-a-link-into-the-question)

**Audio**
You can also send an audio file with the question by clicking on the Audio icon.

[Learn how to embed an audio file into the question](/modules/quiz/features/how-to-add-an-audio-file-to-a-question)

### 11. Change Question Language

You can change the language of the question by clicking on the **Change Language** button at the top right corner of this section. It will open a popup.

![](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-mr5z4p4m.png)

Choose the language to which you want to change and then click on the **Save** button.

### 12. Save the New Question

Once you have filled in the required details of the question, click on the **Save New Question** button to save the question.
