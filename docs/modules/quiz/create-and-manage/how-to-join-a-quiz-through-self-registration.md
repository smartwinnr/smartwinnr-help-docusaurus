> **At a glance** - Learn how to join quizzes that allow self-registration and how to find them.

Self-registration lets editors make a quiz available for learners to join without assigning it individually. Once self-registration is enabled, learners can access and register for the quiz directly through the available quiz link.

## When to use this

Use self-registration when you want learners to access and join a quiz without waiting for an individual assignment.

- You want to make a quiz available for direct learner access.
- You do not want to assign the quiz individually to learners.
- You want to share a quiz link with learners.
- You want learners to access the quiz after logging in through a shared link.

## Steps

### 1. Find quizzes open for self-registration
Go to **LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE** > **View all quizzes** to see the list of coachings created.

![sheetshot](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-join-a-quiz-through-self-registration-mtr50o87.png)

Open the quiz that you want to enable self-registration for, then select the checkbox called **Allow self registration**.

![allow self registration](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtmusy1v.png)

Then click **Save**.

### 2. Open the quiz link
After saving, open the quiz. You will see a small box with **Quiz Link**. Click it to copy the link so you can share it with learners.

If the editor shares a link with a learner, the learner can join the quiz without waiting for an assignment. The link takes the learner directly to that quiz.

If the learner is not logged in to SmartWinnr, the login page appears first. After learners log in, SmartWinnr takes them to the quiz.

![where we can copy quiz link](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtmv1cdt.png)

## Tips

- Enable self-registration only for quizzes that you want learners to access directly.
- Ensure that the quiz is active and available before sharing the link.
- Share the quiz link only with the intended learners or audience.
- Review the quiz settings before enabling self-registration.
