> **At a glance** - Knowledge categories help you organize and classify quiz questions effectively, ensuring better tracking and reporting.

**Knowledge categories** provide a structured way to organize and classify your organization’s critical information within the SmartWinnr platform. They allow you to break down content into meaningful topic areas that align with your business goals, training objectives, and performance indicators.

## When to use this
Use knowledge categories when you want to:
- Organize quiz questions by relevant topics.
- Enhance tracking and reporting of quiz performance.
- Align training content with business objectives.

## Examples of How Categories Can Be Used
Categories may represent a wide range of business-relevant topics, such as:

* **Products, Solutions, or Services** - e.g., specific product lines or service offerings.
* **Product Attributes** - including features, advantages, benefits, and pricing details.
* **Organizational Knowledge** - such as industry context, company information, policies, and competitive landscape.

For example, a question could be categorized under both a product (e.g., “Stents”) and a product attribute (e.g., “Features”), enabling deeper tracking and analysis.

## How SmartWinnr Uses Categories
Every piece of learning content—including questions, SmartFeeds, and coaching modules, can be linked to knowledge category. Multiple categories can be applied when content spans several topic areas. All analytics and performance measurements in SmartWinnr are based on these category tags, enabling focused reporting and targeted interventions.

**It is mandatory to have atlease one category for each question that gets created in SmartWinnr**

## Selecting Categories
In SmartWinnr, every question requires at least one category. To select categories for questions, follow these steps:

### 1. Navigate to Questions and Quizzes
Go to **Questions and Quizzes** → **Question Bank**.

### 2. Add a New Question
Choose **Add New Question**.

### 3. Select Categories
Select one or more categories that most accurately define the content of the question. You can select multiple tags from same category for the question if needed.
