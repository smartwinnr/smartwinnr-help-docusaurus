> **At a glance** - Open **Quizzes** to find a live event quiz after your trainer starts it. You can complete and submit the quiz, then check your score in the competition leaderboard.

Use a live event quiz when your trainer is running a quiz session for a competition. The quiz appears in your quiz list after the trainer starts it from their editor account. Once you begin, you can finish and submit your attempt even if the trainer closes the quiz later.

## When to use this

Use this flow when you want to join a quiz that is tied to a live event or competition.

- You are ready to take a quiz your trainer has started.
- You want to submit your answers during the live session.
- You want to check your score in the competition leaderboard.

## Steps

### 1. Open **Quizzes**
Go to the left menu and select **Quizzes**.

![Take the quiz from left menu](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttsgzr3.png)

### 2. Find the live event quiz
Look through the quiz list for the event quiz. It appears after your trainer starts it.

![Empty quiz list](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttsj5gm.png)

### 3. Start the quiz
Once the editor starts the quiz, it will be visible in the quiz list page. Select the quiz from the list and begin playing it like any other quiz.

![Quiz list page after starting the event quiz by trainer](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttskl8b.png)

### 4. Complete the quiz
Answer the questions and continue through the quiz until you are ready to submit.

![Play the quiz](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttsmi2o.png)

### 5. Submit your attempt
Select **Submit** when you finish the quiz.

![Submit the quiz](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttsmwgr.png)

### 6. Check your score
Open the competition leaderboard and select **View** to see your score.

![Check score in the leaderboard](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mttsyja6.png)

## Things to know

:::note
If you start the quiz before your trainer closes it, you can finish and submit your attempt.
:::

:::note
After the trainer closes the quiz, new users cannot start it.
:::

:::note
Event quizzes are part of competitions, so you can check your score in the leaderboard.
:::
