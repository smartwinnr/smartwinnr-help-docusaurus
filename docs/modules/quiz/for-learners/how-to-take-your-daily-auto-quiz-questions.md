> **At a glance** - Your Auto Quiz appears on a recurring schedule. Open it, answer the questions for that day, and return later for the next set. Repeated questions help you review over time.

Auto Quiz gives you a short set of questions on a schedule. You use it to review knowledge in small, regular sessions. Questions can repeat across days, so you can reinforce what you learned.

## When to use this

Use this when you want to complete your scheduled quiz for the day.

- You see a recurring quiz in your learner experience.
- You want to answer today’s assigned questions.
- You want to understand why some questions appear again.
- You want to track your progress across multiple days.

## Steps

### 1. Open your Auto Quiz
Go to the area where your assigned quizzes appear and open the Auto Quiz for today. The quiz shows the questions scheduled for your current session.

### 2. Answer each question
Read each question and choose or enter your answer. Move through the set until you finish all questions for the day.

### 3. Submit the set
After you answer the scheduled questions, submit the quiz. Your completion is tracked for that day, and you can return for the next scheduled set later.

### 4. Review repeated questions
If you see a question again, answer it again. Repeating questions is part of the schedule and helps reinforce learning over time.

## Things to know

:::note
Auto Quiz questions can appear more than once across days. That is expected.
:::

:::note
Your progress is tracked across days, so each completed set contributes to your overall completion.
:::
