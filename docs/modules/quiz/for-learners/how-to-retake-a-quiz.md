> **At a glance** - This article explains how to retake a quiz, manage attempt limits, and respond to reassignment prompts.

You can retake a quiz when you do not achieve the passing score. Understanding the retake process and attempt limits helps you navigate quizzes more effectively.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to:
- Retake a quiz after scoring below the pass threshold.
- Understand the reassignment prompt after quiz completion.
- Know what to do when you exhaust your allowed attempts.

## Steps

### Retake a completed quiz
If you have completed a quiz but did not pass, a **popup** option will appear. You can choose to take the quiz now or later based on your preference. After scoring below the pass threshold, you will see a reassignment prompt. This prompt allows you to take the quiz again either now or later. Choose your preferred option to proceed.

![clickopt](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-retake-a-quiz-mtmkyj69.png)

### Taking a reassigned quiz
You can take the reassigned quiz immediately or at a later time. If you choose to take it later, you can access it from your quiz list when you are ready.

### Know your attempt limits
Be aware of the maximum number of attempts allowed for each quiz. If you exhaust all attempts, you will see a message indicating that no further attempts are available.

![prompt](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-retake-a-quiz-mtml8d69.png)

## Things to know
:::warning
If you exhaust all allowed attempts, you cannot retake the quiz until an admin resets it.
:::
