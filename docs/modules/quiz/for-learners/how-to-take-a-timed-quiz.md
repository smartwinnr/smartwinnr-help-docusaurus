> **At a glance** - This article explains how to take a timed quiz, manage your time effectively, and view your results.

To take a timed quiz, follow the steps below. You will see a timer that counts down the total time allowed for the quiz, which is 10 minutes in this case. You can manage your time for each question according to your preference.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to complete a timed quiz. This can apply in various scenarios, such as:
- Completing a training module.
- Participating in a skills assessment.
- Engaging in a competitive quiz environment.

## Steps

### 1. Start the quiz. You can see the time allotted for this quiz if 10 Min.
Begin by taking the required quiz. 

![Starting page of the quiz where the users can see the timer](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtlcq252.jpg)

### 2. Manage your time
You will see the timer displayed on the top right corner of the screen. The timer shows the total time for the quiz. You can allocate time as you prefer for each question. For example, you may take 30 seconds for one question and 90 seconds for another.

![Timer showing in the page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtldf6gr.jpg)

### 3. Submit the quiz
Once you have completed the quiz, click on the **Submit Quiz** button to submit your answers.

![Submit Quiz](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtldjgte.png)

### 4. Automatic closure of the quiz
If you do not complete the quiz within the allotted time, it will automatically close, and your answers will be submitted.

### 5. View your results
After submitting the quiz, you will see the quiz submission page. This page will display your score and the time taken to complete the quiz.

![Score and time taken by the user](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtldlu19.png)
