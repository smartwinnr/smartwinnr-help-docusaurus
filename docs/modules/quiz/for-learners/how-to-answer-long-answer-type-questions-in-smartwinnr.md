> **At a glance** - This article explains how to effectively answer long answer type questions in SmartWinnr.

SmartWinnr includes descriptive-type questions where you will need to write your answer in one line or paragraph. These questions require thoughtful responses and the ability to articulate your understanding clearly. Follow the steps below to answer such long-answer type questions in SmartWinnr effectively.

## When to use this

Use this guide when you encounter long answer questions in quizzes. This will help you provide detailed and comprehensive responses effectively. Long answer questions often assess your understanding of concepts, so it's important to express your thoughts clearly and concisely.

## Steps

### 1. Start the quiz

Click on the assigned quiz to begin. This will take you to the quiz page where you can view the instructions related to the quiz.

![You can follow the below steps to answer such Long - answer type Questions in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrcc55hf.png)

### 2. Begin answering questions

Click the **START QUIZ** button to start answering the questions in the quiz. Take a moment to read the instructions carefully before proceeding to ensure you understand the requirements for each question.

![start quiz page](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrntaz4a.png)

### 3. Locate long answer questions

When you encounter a long answer question in your quiz, it will be displayed in a specific format. You will see a textbox where you can enter your detailed answer. This textbox is designed to accommodate longer responses, allowing you to articulate your thoughts thoroughly.

![long answer question sample](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrntbonz.png)

While writing your long answer, you can also utilize the **Text Formatting features** to enhance your response.

- Use the **Bold**, **Italics**, and **Underline** text features to emphasize keywords and important points in your answer. This can help draw attention to critical elements of your response.
- **H1** and **H2** are heading font features that convert your text into headings, making it easier for evaluators to follow your response and understand the structure of your answer.
- The _Tx_ feature can reduce the text size for less critical information, allowing you to prioritize key points while still including additional context.
- You can use the list option to create an ordered list or an unordered list (which will display as bullet points) to organize your thoughts clearly. This can improve readability and help evaluators quickly grasp your main ideas.
- There is also a feature to align your text to the left or right based on your requirements, allowing for better presentation of your answer and enhancing the overall clarity of your response.

![Long answer question filled response](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrntclpi.png)

### 4. Submit the quiz

Once you have answered all the questions in the quiz, click the **SUBMIT QUIZ** button to submit your responses. This action will finalize your answers and send them for evaluation.

### 5. View the leaderboard

After submitting the quiz, you will be redirected to the quiz leaderboard page. If all the questions in your quiz were long answer questions, the leaderboard will show zero scores until the evaluator reviews your answers. This allows evaluators to assess the quality of your responses before assigning scores, ensuring a fair evaluation process.

You can find the number of your responses pending evaluation at the top of the leaderboard.

![Quiz leaderboard](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrntdgmy.png)

### 6. Check your score and feedback

Once the evaluation is complete, your score will be visible on the leaderboard. This score reflects your performance based on the evaluator's assessment of your answers.
