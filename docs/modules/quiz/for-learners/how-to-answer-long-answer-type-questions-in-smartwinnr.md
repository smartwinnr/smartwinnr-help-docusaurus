> **At a glance** - This article explains how to effectively answer long answer type questions in SmartWinnr.

SmartWinnr includes descriptive-type questions where you will need to write your answer in one line or paragraph. These questions require thoughtful responses and the ability to articulate your understanding clearly. Follow the steps below to answer such long-answer type questions in SmartWinnr effectively.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you encounter long answer questions in quizzes. This will help you provide detailed and comprehensive responses effectively. Long answer questions often assess your understanding of concepts, so it's important to express your thoughts clearly and concisely.

## Steps

### 1. Start the quiz
Click on the assigned quiz to begin.
![You can follow the below steps to answer such Long - answer type Questions in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-mrcc55hf.png)

### 2. Begin answering questions
Click the **START QUIZ** button to start answering the questions in the quiz. Take a moment to read the instructions carefully before proceeding.

![](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-2.png)

### 3. Locate long answer questions
When you encounter a long answer question in your quiz, it will be displayed in a specific format. You will see a textbox where you can enter your detailed answer.

![](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-create-long-answer-questions-5.png)

While writing your long answer, you can also utilize the **Text Formatting features** to enhance your response. 

- Use the **Bold**, **Italics**, and **Underline** text features to emphasize keywords and important points in your answer.
- **H1** and **H2** are heading font features that convert your text into headings, making it easier for evaluators to follow your response.
- The _Tx_ feature can reduce the text size for less critical information.
- You can use the list option to create an ordered list or an unordered list (which will display as bullet points) to organize your thoughts clearly.
- There is also a feature to align your text to the left or right based on your requirements, allowing for better presentation of your answer.

### 4. Submit the quiz
Once you have answered all the questions in the quiz, click the **SUBMIT QUIZ** button to submit your responses.


### 5. View the leaderboard
After submitting the quiz, you will be redirected to the quiz leaderboard page. If all the questions in your quiz were long answer questions, the leaderboard will show zero scores until the evaluator reviews your answers. This allows evaluators to assess the quality of your responses before assigning scores.

![](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-4.png)

### 6. Check your score and feedback
Once the evaluation is complete, your score will be visible on the leaderboard.

- To check the leaderboard, select the quiz from the **Completed section tab** and choose the quiz.

![How to view the Quiz score and Feedback?](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-5.png)

- By selecting the quiz, you can view the score leaderboard, which provides insights into how you performed compared to others.

![](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-6.png)

- You can also check the evaluator's feedback by clicking on the **Review** icon at the top right corner. This feedback is crucial for understanding your strengths and areas for improvement.

![](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-7.png)

- This will redirect you to a page where you can access your answers and feedback provided by the evaluator.

![](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-answer-long-answer-type-questions-in-smartwinnr-8.png)

- **User Response:** This is the answer submitted by you during the quiz, showcasing your understanding of the topic.
- **Evaluator Feedback:** This feedback includes the correct answer for the long answer question provided before the quiz started, helping you learn the expected response.
- **Additional Feedback:** This feedback is based on your response after the quiz is completed, providing insights into how you can improve in future quizzes.
