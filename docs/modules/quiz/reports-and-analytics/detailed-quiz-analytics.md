> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr offers detailed real-time analytics for quizzes, allowing you to evaluate participant performance effectively.

SmartWinnr provides detailed real-time quiz analytics that help you gain clear insights into the performance of participants in the quizzes.

## When to use this

Use detailed quiz analytics to:

- Evaluate participant performance.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses in quiz questions.
- Track overall participation and completion rates.
- Analyze results by different user segments.

## Before you start

Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access quiz analytics.

## Steps

### 1. Access Quiz Analytics

To get the detailed analytics of a specific quiz, go to **Left Menu → Editor → Questions and Quizzes → View all Quizzes**. Click on the **analytics icon** next to the quiz for which you would like to see the analytics.

![To get the detailed analytics of a specific quiz, go to Left Menu → Editor → Questions and Quizzes → View all Quizzes. Click on the analytics icon next to the quiz.](/img/helpscout/editors/detailed-quiz-analytics-1.png)

### 2. Set Analytics View

By default, all analytics displayed are grouped by **SubDivisions** (typically countries or states, depending on your system configuration).

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41w94f.png)

If you want to view the analytics based on **Territory**, **User Types**, or any other **User Tags** present in your organization, you can set your preference. Go to **Profile → Settings** and click on **Edit** on **Analytics Grouping**.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt412zkh.png)

Select the appropriate grouping and refresh the page to see the new grouping in the quiz analytics.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt414ct2.png)

### 3. Analyze Participation

You can view detailed analytics on participation. **Overall Participation** shows the distribution of how many people have completed the quiz versus how many have not. **Participation by Country** shows the distribution by different countries.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41czf5.png)

For detailed information on who has completed and not completed the quiz, click on the **Details** button. This list shows the details of every individual to whom the quiz had been assigned, their score, and status.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41fyr5.png)

### 4. Review Performance for All

The performance metrics are shown by categories. Select a category to see the distribution. The values in the chart are based on percentage scores, helping to identify areas of strength and areas for improvement.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41ie5p.png)

#### Performance by Category by Country

Here, the performance metrics are further broken down by country. This helps to identify strengths and improvements by geography.

![Here, the performance metrics are further broken down by Country. This helps to identify the areas of strengths and improvements by geography.](/img/helpscout/editors/detailed-quiz-analytics-8.png)

If you want to see further details by different Territories, click on the **View Details** button.

![If you want to see further details by different Territories, click on the ‘View Details’ button.](/img/helpscout/editors/detailed-quiz-analytics-9.png)

### 5. Analyze Questions in Quiz

This section shows the analytics of each question in the quiz. These analytics help quiz masters and editors understand how participants are performing on each question. The percentage indicates how many participants answered the question correctly.

Separate analytics are available for multiple-choice questions. To view the analytics of a multiple-choice question, click on the analytics icon on the right-hand side of the question.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41m2d5.png)

It will open a pop-up where you can see:

- The question.
- The correct option to the question.
- A graph showing all the options of the question and analytics of the number of responses each option has received.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41oihx.png)

You can also save this graph as an image or change the color of the graph. To do so, click on the hamburger icon at the top right corner of the pop-up. It will give you two options: **Save as Image** and **Change Color**. Click on the **Save as Image** option to save this graph as an image. Click on the **Change Color** option to change the color of the graph. Clicking on **Change Color** opens the following pop-up:

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41pgnh.png)

Here, enter the hex code of the color of your choice and click on the **Apply** button. This will change the color of the graph successfully.

### 6. View Leaderboard

Every quiz has a real-time leaderboard that shows the relative ranking of quiz takers based on their scores. The leaderboard can be filtered by country and territory.

![Every quiz has a real-time leaderboard that shows the relative ranking of quiz takers. The ranking is based on their score on that quiz.](/img/helpscout/editors/detailed-quiz-analytics-13.png)

Clicking on the **download icon** against the leaderboard will download the quiz leaderboard in Excel format.

#### View User’s Dashboard

The quiz master or editor has access to details of an individual’s performance. Click on the **View Analytics** button next to each user in the leaderboard to find individual performance details.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/detailed-quiz-analytics-mt41rknb.png)

### 7. Download Quiz Results

You can download the quiz results into an Excel sheet for further use. Go to **Questions and Quizzes** in the Left Menu → **View all quizzes** → Click on the **View Analytics** button of a quiz → Click on the **Download Quiz Results** button at the top right corner. This will download the entire quiz report in Excel format.

![Go to Questions and Quizzes in Left Menu → View all quizzes → Click on the 'View Analytics' button of a quiz → Click on the button ‘Download Quiz Results’ at the top right corner.](/img/helpscout/editors/detailed-quiz-analytics-15.png)

The Excel file will contain the following sheets:

- **Quiz Details** – A high-level overview of the quiz.
- **Participants Report** – A list of all the participants to whom the quiz had been assigned, along with Quiz Completion Status, Quiz Taken date, Percentage Score, etc.
- **Participants Wise Question Analytics Reports** – This report provides data on every participant and how they answered each question in the quiz.
- **Quiz Questions Details Reports** – This report lists the questions that have been added to this quiz.
