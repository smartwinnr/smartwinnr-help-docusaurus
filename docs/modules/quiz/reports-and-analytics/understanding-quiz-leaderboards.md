> **At a glance** - This article covers how quiz scores affect leaderboards, including visibility options and learner rankings.

Quiz leaderboards display how learners perform on quizzes, allowing for competition and motivation. You can configure leaderboards during quiz creation, manage visibility for different audiences, and control how quiz results feed into global and division leaderboards.

## When to use this
Use quiz leaderboards to:
- Encourage friendly competition among learners.
- Track learner performance across quizzes.
- Manage visibility based on audience needs.
- Motivate learners through rankings.

## Before you start
Your organization should have the **quiz module** enabled to utilize leaderboards.

## Steps

### 1. Configure leaderboard settings during quiz creation
When creating a quiz, you can enable the option to add results to the per-quiz leaderboard. This setting allows you to track scores specifically for that quiz.

![setting](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-quiz-leaderboards-mtmsltf2.png)

You have three options:
- **Add to all leaderboards**: Display quiz results in both quiz-specific and competition leaderboards to maximize engagement and visibility.
- **Add to only quiz leaderboard**: Show results exclusively in the quiz-specific leaderboard, excluding them from competition rankings.
- **Do not add to any leaderboard**: Keep quiz results private by excluding them from all leaderboards. This is ideal for assessments and examinations where score privacy is required.

### 2. View learner rankings
Learners can see their rank on the leaderboard, which helps them understand their performance relative to their peers. This visibility can enhance motivation and engagement.

![moilwview](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-quiz-leaderboards-mtmt5kg7.png)

## Tips
- Regularly review leaderboard settings to ensure they align with your organizational goals.
- Encourage learners to check their rankings frequently to maintain motivation.
