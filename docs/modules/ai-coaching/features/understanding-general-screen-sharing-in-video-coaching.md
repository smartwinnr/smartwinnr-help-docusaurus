> **At a glance** - Use the General Screen Sharing feature to enhance your coaching sessions by sharing your screen and recording the discussion.

The **General Screen Sharing** feature enables users to share their screen during coaching sessions. This allows for effective product demonstrations, application walkthroughs, and discussions around documents, all of which are recorded for later review.

## When to use this
Use the General Screen Sharing feature when you need to:
- Conduct product demonstrations, application walkthroughs, presentations, or document discussions during a live coaching session.
- Walk through documents or presentations with participants.
- Record the coaching session for future reference.

## Before you start
To enable screen sharing in video coaching, navigate to **Editor > Coaching > View Coaching**. The list of coaching sessions you have access to will be displayed.

![List of coaching sessions](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2yn7md.png)

Open the coaching session that you need to enable screen sharing for, go to **Settings**, and enable the checkbox.

![Enable screen sharing in settings](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2ypxbz.png)

## How Screen Sharing Works in Coachings

### Start the coaching session
Begin by initiating your coaching session as you normally would. Select the language by clicking on the **CHOOSE LANGUAGE** button.

![Start the coaching session](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2vx1h8.png)

After that, select the appropriate option you want. We have **Video Role Play** and **Audio Only Role Play**; choose according to your needs. Each option's difference is mentioned there itself.

![Select role play option](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2w128x.png)

Then click on **Start With Video/Audio** to begin. After that, click on **START ROLE PLAY**.

![Start role play](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2w36g9.png)

A pop-up will appear prompting you to enable camera and microphone permissions. Select camera and mic as per your need and click on **Proceed**.

![Enable camera and mic permissions](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2w6pkd.png)

The following interface will appear, where you can see the AI avatar and the screen share option. From there, you can share your screen.

![Interface with AI avatar and screen share option](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2xssrt.png)

After clicking on the **Share** button, the following screen will show. You can choose to share **Entire Tab**, **Window**, or **Entire Screen** (recommended).

![Select what to share](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2xvigr.png)

You can interact with the AI while sharing your screen.

![Interact with AI while sharing screen](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-general-screen-sharing-in-video-coaching-ms2xxszf.png)

After completing the interaction, you can stop sharing and end the role play. The recorded session will be saved, and the score will be generated based on the interaction.

## Best Practices
- Share the **Entire Screen** if you need to switch between multiple applications.
- Close unnecessary applications to avoid sharing sensitive information.
- Ensure the required document or application is open before starting screen sharing.
- Verify that microphone permissions are enabled for proper audio recording.
- End screen sharing once the demonstration is complete.

## Things to know
:::note
Screen sharing is only available after the coaching session has started. The coaching document remains accessible within the session while screen sharing.
:::

Everything shown on the shared screen during the session is captured in the final recording, which can be reviewed later by authorized users.
