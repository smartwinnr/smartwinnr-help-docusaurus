> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr offers five coaching types to enhance communication, selling, presentation, and customer-interaction skills through various practice formats.

SmartWinnr provides different coaching types to help organizations assess and improve communication, selling, presentation, and customer-interaction skills. Depending on the coaching objective, learners can practice through recorded responses, audio submissions, AI-powered conversations, live interactions, or presentation-based activities.

The five coaching types available in SmartWinnr are:

- Video Coaching
- Audio Coaching
- Conversational AI Role Play
- Live Coaching
- Presentation Coaching

Each coaching type provides a different way to practice and evaluate skills.

## When to use this

Choose the appropriate coaching type based on the skill you want the learner to practice or assess:

- Use **Video Coaching** to assess overall delivery, including visible communication.
- Use **Audio Coaching** when the focus is on spoken communication.
- Use **Conversational AI Role Play** for dynamic, two-way customer conversations.
- Use **Live Coaching** when a manager or coach needs to observe and coach performance in a live or field setting.
- Use **Presentation Coaching** to improve structured presentation and delivery skills.

## Understand More on each Coaching types:

### 1. Video Coaching

**Video Coaching** allows learners to record a video response to an assigned coaching scenario and submit it for evaluation. It is useful when the learner's verbal and non-verbal communication needs to be assessed. Coaches or managers can review the submitted video, evaluate the learner against defined competencies, and provide feedback.

How Video Coaching works:
- A trainer or editor assigns a coaching scenario to the learner.
- The learner reviews the scenario and records a video response.
- The learner submits the video through SmartWinnr.
- The coach or manager reviews the submission.
- The submission is evaluated based on the defined competencies or criteria.
- Feedback and scores can be provided to help the learner improve.

When to use Video Coaching:
- Practicing sales pitches and product messaging.
- Handling customer objections.
- Practicing new product introductions.
- Evaluating communication and presentation skills.
- Reinforcing sales scripts and key messages.
- Comparing progress across multiple attempts.

SmartWinnr's video coaching workflow supports practice, assessment, feedback, and sharing of strong examples for peer learning.

### 2. Audio Coaching

**Audio Coaching** allows learners to submit an audio response to an assigned coaching scenario. This coaching type focuses primarily on the learner's spoken communication without requiring video.

How Audio Coaching works:
- A coaching scenario is assigned to the learner.
- The learner records an audio response.
- The response is submitted for evaluation.
- The coach or manager reviews and evaluates the response based on the defined criteria.
- Feedback can be provided to help the learner improve.

When to use Audio Coaching:
- Verbal communication.
- Message delivery.
- Tone and clarity.
- Product or sales messaging.
- Objection handling.
- Practicing conversations where visual assessment is not required.

### 3. Conversational AI Role Play

**Conversational AI Role Play** allows learners to practice a realistic, two-way conversation with an AI-powered customer or other defined persona. Unlike a recorded response, the conversation is interactive. The AI can respond to what the learner says, ask questions, raise objections, and adapt the conversation based on the learner's responses.

SmartWinnr's AI Roleplay platform is designed for realistic conversations with personas such as physicians, pharmacists, and other healthcare decision-makers. Conversations can be evaluated for areas such as tone, empathy, objection handling, clarity, and compliance.

How Conversational AI Role Play works:
- The learner starts an assigned AI role-play scenario.
- The AI takes the role of the defined customer/persona.
- The learner interacts with the AI through a two-way conversation.
- The AI responds and may ask questions or raise objections.
- The conversation is evaluated against predefined criteria.
- The learner receives feedback and performance insights.

When to use Conversational AI Role Play:
- Customer or HCP conversations.
- Product discussions.
- Discovery conversations.
- Objection handling.
- Difficult or unexpected questions.
- Compliance-sensitive conversations.

AI role play provides an opportunity for learners to practice repeatedly without depending on a manager being available for every practice session.

### 4. Live Coaching

**Live Coaching** is designed for coaching interactions that happen directly between a learner and a coach or manager. It can be used to capture observations from real-world coaching activities and provide structured feedback and follow-up actions.

For example, a manager may conduct a joint field visit with a sales representative, observe the interaction, record their observations, and provide feedback.

SmartWinnr's Live coaching workflow supports shared coaching forms, weighted criteria, manager notes, observations, feedback, and follow-up assignments.

How Live Coaching works:
- A coaching activity is planned or assigned.
- The coach and learner participate in the live coaching activity.
- The coach records observations using the relevant coaching form.
- The learner's performance is evaluated against defined criteria.
- Feedback and next steps are captured.
- Additional learning or practice activities can be assigned based on the coaching outcome.

When to use Live Coaching:
- Joint field visits.
- On-the-job coaching.
- In-person performance observation.
- Real customer interaction assessment.
- Providing immediate coaching feedback.
- Identifying follow-up learning or practice needs.

### 5. Presentation Coaching

**Presentation Coaching** focuses on helping learners improve their ability to deliver structured presentations. It can be used when learners need to practice presenting a product, solution, proposal, or other business content clearly and effectively.

How Presentation Coaching works:
- A learner prepares and delivers a presentation based on the assigned coaching activity.
- The presentation can then be evaluated against the defined coaching criteria, allowing the learner to understand what they did well and where improvement is needed.

When to use Presentation Coaching:
- Product presentations.
- Sales presentations.
- Product launches.
- Customer-facing presentations.
- Training or educational presentations.
- Practicing presentation structure and delivery.
- Improving confidence and communication.
