> **At a glance** - Access your coaching feedback easily through your user account and download it if available.

To view your coaching feedback, log into your user account and navigate to the coaching section. Here, you can check any coaching submissions that include feedback.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to:

- Review feedback on your submitted coaching videos.
- Understand your performance and areas for improvement.
- Download your feedback for offline reference.

## Steps

### 1. Log in and access coaching

Log into your user account and go to the coaching section. Find the coaching submission that includes video feedback and click on the **View Feedback** button.

![Click on the View Feedback button](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms4bpgb8.png)

### 2. Review your feedback

Once you click on **View Feedback**, you will see the feedback displayed below your submitted video. This includes overall feedback and scores.

![Overall Feedback with Score](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms4bwxzh.png)

Detailed feedback on Objections, objectives etc.

![Detailed feedback on Objections, Objectives etc](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms4by3rh.png)

### 3. Download your feedback

If the coaching feedback download option is enabled, click on the **Download Feedback** button. This will provide you with a PDF document containing your feedback.

![Download Feedback as a document](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms4c00vp.png)

## Tips

- Check regularly for feedback to stay updated on your performance.
- Use the PDF feedback document for detailed insights and to track your progress over time.
