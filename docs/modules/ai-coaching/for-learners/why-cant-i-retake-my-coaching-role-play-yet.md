> **At a glance** - Understand the reasons you might not see the retake option for your coaching role play.

You may not see the retake option for your coaching role play due to two main reasons. First, if you have already completed the maximum number of attempts allowed for the scenario. Second, if your current attempt is still being processed for feedback.

## When to use this
This article is helpful when you are unable to retake a coaching role play. You might encounter this situation if:
- You have reached the limit of allowed attempts for the role play.
- Your current attempt is still under evaluation and feedback generation.

## Steps

### 1. Check your attempts
If you have completed the maximum number of attempts for the coaching scenario, the **retake** option will not be available. 

![attempt exhausted](/img/helpscout/authored/why-cant-i-retake-my-coaching-role-play-yet-mtn8y7a9.png)

### 2. Wait for feedback
If your current attempt is still processing, you will need to wait until the evaluation is complete. You can only make the next attempt after receiving feedback on your current attempt.

![Evalution still processing](/img/helpscout/authored/why-cant-i-retake-my-coaching-role-play-yet-mtn90xvd.png)

If you try to retake the scenario before the AI feedback is generated, it will show you a pop-up as shown below:

![If you try to retake the scenario before the AI feedback is generated, it will show you a pop-up like this](/img/helpscout/authored/why-cant-i-retake-my-coaching-role-play-yet-mtn92shj.png)

## Tips
- Review the feedback from your previous attempt to identify areas for improvement.
- Ensure you check the status of your current attempt before trying to retake the role play.
