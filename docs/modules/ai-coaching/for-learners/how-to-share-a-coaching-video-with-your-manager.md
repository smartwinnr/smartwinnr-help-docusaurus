> **At a glance** - You can easily share your coaching video with your manager using the SmartWinnr platform.

In SmartWinnr, you can share your coaching video with your manager for review, feedback, and discussion. This helps facilitate better communication and support throughout your learning journey.

SmartWinnr allows you to choose which attempt you want to make available to your manager for review. This means you can make multiple attempts and practice the scenario before sharing your response with your manager.

Once you are satisfied with your performance, you can select your best attempt and share it with your manager for review and feedback.

This gives you the flexibility to:

- Practice a scenario multiple times before sharing it.
- Review your attempts and select the one you are most confident about.
- Share your selected attempt with your manager for feedback and discussion.
- Use your manager's feedback to further improve your performance.

Note: This feature will be available only if your trainer has enabled this feature for the scenario. If the feature is not enabled, then all your attempts will be available by default to your manager.

## Steps to share specific attempt to the manager

1. Goto the Coaching > Select the scenario for which you want to share an attempt with your manager.

2. Select the attempt you want to share and click on the **SHARE WITH THE MANAGER** button.

![Click on the button "SHARE WITH THE MANAGER"](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-share-a-coaching-video-with-your-manager-mto2az66.png)

3. A confirmation message will appear. Click Confirm to share the attempt. Once shared, the attempt will be marked with the Shared with Manager tag. Your manager can then view the shared attempt from their SmartWinnr account.

![shared with manager](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-share-a-coaching-video-with-your-manager-mto2c3td.png)

Note: For scenarios where the **'Share with Manager'** option is enabled, your manager will not be able to view your submissions until you share an attempt with them. Until then, your manager will see a message indicating that no attempt has been shared for review.

![Manager view - shared with manager option](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-share-a-coaching-video-with-your-manager-mto2dg96.png)
