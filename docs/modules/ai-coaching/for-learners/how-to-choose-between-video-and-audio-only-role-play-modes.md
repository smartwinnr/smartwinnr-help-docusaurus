> **At a glance** - When you start an assigned AI Role Play, you can choose Video Role Play or Audio-Only Role Play. Pick the mode that fits your practice goal before the session begins. This option is available only if your trainer has enabled a choice of practice mode for the assigned scenario.


When you start an assigned AI Role Play, SmartWinnr asks how you want to practice. You can choose a video experience or a voice-only experience before the role play begins. The score and feedback stay the same in both modes.

## When to use this

Use this when your trainer has enabled a choice of practice mode for the assigned scenario.

- Choose Video Role Play for face-to-face practice.
- Choose Audio-Only Role Play for private, voice-focused practice.
- Choose Audio-Only Role Play when you want to start quickly.
- Choose Audio-Only Role Play when a slower network makes video less practical.

## Steps

### 1. Open the assigned role play
Open your assigned AI Role Play to begin.

![When you start the role play, it will ask you on how you would like to practice - audio mode or video mode](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwoog7w.png)

### 2. Review the practice options
On the **How would you like to practice?** screen, review **Video Role Play** and **Audio-Only Role Play**.

### 3. Choose your mode
Select **Start with Video** for a camera-based practice session, or **Start with Audio** for voice-only practice.

### 4. Complete the role play
Continue the AI Role Play in the mode you selected. You cannot change the mode after the session starts.

## Things to know

:::note
Your score and feedback are the same in both modes. The difference is only how you practice.
:::

:::caution
Choose your mode before you begin. You cannot switch between video and audio during the role play.
:::
