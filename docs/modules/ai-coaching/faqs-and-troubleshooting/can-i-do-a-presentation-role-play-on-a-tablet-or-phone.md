> **At a glance** - Presentation role plays in SmartWinnr require a desktop browser due to screen sharing needs.

Presentation role plays in SmartWinnr are designed for desktop use only. This requirement is due to the need for screen sharing, which is not supported on mobile devices like phones and tablets.

## When to use this
Use this information when you need to complete a presentation role play in SmartWinnr. The following scenarios apply:
- You are preparing for a presentation role play.
- You are unsure about device compatibility for SmartWinnr features.
- You want to know if you can use your phone or tablet for role plays.

## Before you start
To complete a presentation role play, ensure you have:
- A desktop or laptop computer.
- A supported desktop browser that allows screen sharing.

## Steps

### 1. Use a desktop or laptop
Open SmartWinnr in a desktop browser to access presentation role plays.

### 2. Ensure screen sharing is enabled
Make sure your browser settings allow screen sharing.

### 3. Start the presentation role play
Launch the role play and share your screen when prompted.

## Tips
- For other role play modes, you can use the SmartWinnr mobile app on your phone or tablet.
- Always check your browser settings before starting a presentation role play to avoid interruptions.

## Things to know
:::caution
Presentation-mode role plays cannot be completed on phones or tablets due to the screen sharing requirement.
:::
