> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr retains role-play recordings and AI-generated feedback for a configurable period, ensuring secure access and storage.

SmartWinnr records each role-play attempt, generating video/audio recordings and AI-based feedback. These records are securely stored and have a default retention period of 180 days, which can be customized based on client needs.

## When to use this
This information is useful when you want to understand how role-play recordings are managed within SmartWinnr. Consider this when:
- You need to review past role-play sessions for training purposes.
- You want to configure access for managers to view role-play recordings.
- You are responsible for setting retention policies for your organization.
- You need to ensure compliance with data retention regulations.

## Steps

### 1. Understand What Records Are Retained
SmartWinnr retains the following for each role-play attempt:
- **Video and audio recording**: A complete recording of the user's role-play session.
- **AI-generated feedback**: Feedback based on the user's submission.

### 2. Know the Retention Period
The default retention period for these recordings is 180 days. This period can be adjusted according to your organization's requirements.

### 3. Learn About Access Control
By default, role-play recordings and feedback are accessible only to the individual user. However, you can configure access for the user's line manager based on your organizational needs.

### 4. Review What Happens After Deletion
Once the retention period expires:
- The video/audio recording is automatically deleted.
- The AI-generated feedback remains available for reference and can support ongoing learning and development.

## Tips
- Regularly review your organization's retention settings to ensure they meet your compliance requirements.
- Encourage users to utilize the AI-generated feedback even after the original recordings have been deleted.
- Configure access settings to allow managers to support their team's learning effectively.

## Things to know
:::caution
Deletion of video and audio recordings does not affect the availability of the associated AI-generated feedback.
:::
