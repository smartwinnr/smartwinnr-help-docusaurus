> **At a glance** - Use these steps when an AI coaching session freezes, buffers, or the AI voice sounds distorted. Check your connection, browser, and device settings to restore a smooth session.

If your AI coaching session freezes, buffers, or the AI voice sounds distorted, check your connection, browser, and device settings. These steps help you restore a smooth session and continue without interruption.

## When to use this

Use these steps when the video or AI conversation stops responding during a coaching session.

- The session freezes or buffers.
- The AI voice repeats questions or sounds unclear.
- Video or audio does not play correctly.
- You want to confirm whether the issue is your browser, network, or device.

## Steps

### 1. Check your internet connection

A stable connection helps the video and AI conversation run smoothly. If you use Wi-Fi, move closer to the router or switch to a wired connection if possible.

Close other apps or tabs that use a lot of bandwidth. Avoid switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data during the session.

### 2. Check your browser

Use an up-to-date supported browser. Close extra tabs and applications, then refresh the page and start the session again.

If the issue continues, clear your browser cache and cookies, then sign in again. SmartWinnr works best in **Chrome browser**.

### 3. Check your audio and video settings

If the AI voice sounds distorted or repeats words, check your microphone, speaker, and headphone settings. Make sure the correct devices are selected.

Disconnect and reconnect your headphones or other audio devices. Confirm that your browser permission are enabled to use your microphone and camera.

### 4. Close background applications

Close apps that use your camera, microphone, or system resources. Also close unused browser tabs.

If your browser becomes unresponsive, restart it before you try the session again.

### 6. Contact SmartWinnr Support

If the issue continues, contact SmartWinnr Support at support@smartwinnr.com with these details:

- Your name and registered email address
- The coaching scenario name
- A short description of the issue
- Your browser name and version if possible
- Screenshots or a screen recording, if available

Providing these details helps our Support team investigate the issue faster.

## Things to know

:::note
If the session becomes unresponsive, a refresh and restart often resolves temporary connection or browser issues.
:::

:::caution
Before starting a new attempt, check whether your previous attempt was saved.
:::
