> **At a glance** - A role-play can show **Reassigned** or **Not Submitted** even after a learner has completed it. These statuses usually mean the latest attempt was retaken, reassigned, or not uploaded successfully.

Use this article to understand what each status means and what to check next when a learner says they completed an AI role-play, but the status does not show **Completed**.

## When to use this

Use this when a learner says they completed an AI role-play, but the status does not show **Completed**.

- The learner selected **Retake** after finishing a scenario.
- A manager or trainer reassigned the scenario.
- The learner recorded a response, but the upload did not finish.
- The submission was interrupted by a network or browser issue.

## Steps

### 1. Check why it shows **Reassigned**

A role-play shows **Reassigned** when the scenario has been assigned again for another attempt.

This can happen if the learner selected **Retake** after completing the first attempt. It can also happen if a manager or editor (trainer) reassigned the scenario to the learner.

When the learner submits the next response successfully, the status changes back to **Completed**.

### 2. Check why it shows **Not Submitted**

A role-play can show **Not Submitted** when the response was not successfully uploaded.

This can happen if the learner recorded a response but the upload did not finish. It can also happen if the session ended before submission completed.

Ask the learner to check whether another attempt is available and submit again if possible.

### 3. Decide whether the attempt can be restored

Whether a previous attempt can be restored depends on what happened during the session.

If the scenario was intentionally reassigned, the learner may need to complete the new attempt. The status changes to **Completed** after the response uploads successfully.

If the learner completed the role-play but the response is missing, contact SmartWinnr Support. They can investigate whether the submission was uploaded and whether the attempt can be recovered.

### 4. Gather details for Support

If the status still does not match the completed attempt, share these details with SmartWinnr Support:

- Learner name and registered email address
- AI role-play scenario name
- Approximate date and time of the attempt
- Current status shown in SmartWinnr
- Whether the learner selected **Retake** or the scenario was reassigned by trainer or manager
- Screenshot or screen recording of the status, if available

These details help SmartWinnr Support team to investigate the submission and determine whether the previous response can be recovered.

## Things to know

:::warning
Restoring an earlier attempt is not guaranteed. SmartWinnr Support must review the submission details first.
:::
