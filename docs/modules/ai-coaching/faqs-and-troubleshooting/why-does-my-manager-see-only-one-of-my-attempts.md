You can make several attempts in an AI Coaching scenario and keep practicing before you share anything. When attempt sharing is enabled, you choose one attempt for your manager to review. Your other attempts stay private.

## When to use this

Use this when you want your manager to review your best response, not every practice attempt.

- You want to practice the scenario more than once.
- You want to review your responses before sharing one.
- You want to change which attempt your manager sees.
- You want to keep practice attempts private unless you share them.

## How does attempt sharing work?

You can make multiple attempts at an AI Coaching scenario to practice and improve your performance.

Once you are satisfied with an attempt, you can choose to share that specific attempt with your manager.

For example:

- You complete Attempt 1 and Attempt 2 for practice.
- You complete Attempt 3 and feel it is your best performance.
- You select Attempt 3 to share with your manager.
- Your manager can see Attempt 3, while Attempts 1 and 2 remain private.

This allows you to practice multiple times before deciding which response you want your manager to review.

## Things to know

- Your manager can see only the attempt you select to share.
- Your other practice attempts remain private unless you share them.
