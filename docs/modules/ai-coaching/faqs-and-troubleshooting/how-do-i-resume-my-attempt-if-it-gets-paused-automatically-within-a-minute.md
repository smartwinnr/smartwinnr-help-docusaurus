> **At a glance** - If your role play attempt pauses automatically, follow these steps to resume it.

Role play attempts should not pause automatically. However, if you experience a pause, it may be due to network connectivity issues or if the conversation ends before reaching the minimum dialogue threshold, typically within the first minute. To resume your attempt, check your internet connection and relaunch the attempt.

## When to use this

Use this guide when:

- Your role play attempt pauses unexpectedly.
- You want to understand why your attempt may have paused.
- You need to troubleshoot network issues affecting your role play.

## Steps

### 1. Check your internet connection

Ensure that your internet connection is stable. A weak or interrupted connection can cause the role play to pause.

### 2. Relaunch your attempt

If the attempt has paused, close the session and restart it to continue your role play.

## Tips

- If you frequently experience pauses, consider using a wired connection for more stability.
- Monitor your network status during the role play to avoid interruptions.
