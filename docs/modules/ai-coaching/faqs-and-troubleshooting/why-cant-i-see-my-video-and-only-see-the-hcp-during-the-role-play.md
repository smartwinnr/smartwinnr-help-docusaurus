> **At a glance** - If you can't see your video during a role play, it may be due to network issues, camera permissions, or firewall restrictions.

During role plays, you may encounter a situation where you only see the Healthcare Professional (HCP) and not your own video. This typically occurs for a few reasons related to network conditions, camera access, or security settings.

## When to use this

This article is useful when you experience issues with your video feed during a role play. Consider this if you:

- Are preparing for a role play and notice your video is not displaying.
- Experience interruptions in video during the session.
- Need to troubleshoot video visibility issues with your team.

## Steps

### 1. Check your network bandwidth

A slow or unstable internet connection may cause the platform to disable your video to ensure a smooth interaction with the HCP.

**Recommendation:**

- Ensure you have a stable, high-speed internet connection.
- Switch to a stronger Wi-Fi network or use a wired connection if possible.

### 2. Verify camera permissions

Your browser needs permission to access your camera. If permissions are not granted, you won’t see your video.

**Recommendation:**

- Allow camera access when prompted by your browser.
- Ensure no other applications are using your camera.
- Refresh the page after granting the required permissions.

### 3. Check for network or firewall restrictions

In some corporate environments, network security policies or firewall settings may block the necessary video connection.

**Recommendation:**

- Contact your IT team to ensure that SmartWinnr and relevant communication service domains are whitelisted on your organization's firewall or proxy.

If you continue to experience issues after following these steps, reach out to your SmartWinnr administrator or support team for further assistance.
