> **At a glance** – Published AI Role-Play scenarios can be assigned to specific groups or meta-tags, such as a new-hire cohort. Unpublished scenarios can only be assigned to demo users, one person at a time.

AI Role-Play scenario assignment allows you to provide targeted practice to selected learners based on their training needs. For example, you can assign an objection-handling scenario to new hires or share a product knowledge scenario with a specific team. The available assignment options depend on whether the scenario is published.

## When to use this

Use this when you want to:

- Assign relevant scenarios to new hires during onboarding.
- Provide practice to a specific group of learners rather than your entire audience.
- Assign scenarios to cohorts organized using meta-tags, such as role or training stage.
- Test an unpublished scenario with individual demo users before assigning it more broadly.

## How does scenario assignment work?

### Published scenarios

Once an AI Role-Play scenario is published, you can assign it to a specific group or a cohort identified by meta-tags. This allows you to target the scenario to the intended learners without assigning it individually to every user.

For example, you can assign an objection-handling scenario to the Blue Team or use a meta-tag to target new hires.

### Unpublished scenarios

Before an AI Role-Play scenario is published, you can assign it only to demo users, one person at a time. This allows you to test the scenario with individual demo users before making it available to a wider audience.

## What options are available for selecting learners?

When assigning users to a scenario, you can use the available filters to find the intended learners. Depending on your configuration, these may include:

- **Business Unit:** Filter users by business unit.
- **Country:** Filter users by country.
- **Group:** Select a group, such as Blue Team, Red Team, or another configured group.
- **Advanced Search:** Find specific users based on the available search criteria.
![assign specific scenarios for a particular group](/img/helpscout/authored/can-you-turn-on-assign-specific-scenarios-for-a-particular-group-or-cohort-mvi4z5ov.png)
You can also select or deselect individual users as needed.

## Things to know

- **Scenario status matters:** Group and meta-tag assignments are available for published scenarios.
- **Demo assignments:** Unpublished scenarios can only be assigned to demo users individually.
- **Group membership:** Ensure the intended learners belong to the appropriate group before assigning the scenario.
- **Meta-tags:** Use meta-tags when your organization identifies cohorts through shared labels, such as new-hire status or training stage.
