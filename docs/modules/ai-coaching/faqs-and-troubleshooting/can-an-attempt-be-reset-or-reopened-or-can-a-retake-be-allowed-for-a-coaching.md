Individual role-play attempts cannot be reset, reopened, or restored after they are completed. If a learner selects **Retake**, the previous attempt stays closed.

To give a learner another chance, you can either increase the scenario’s attempt limit or have an **Editor** or **Manager** reassign the scenario.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to understand what happens after a learner finishes a role-play attempt.

- A learner completed an attempt and wants to try again.
- A learner accidentally selected **Retake**.
- You want to give every assigned learner more attempts.
- You want to give one learner another opportunity without changing the scenario for everyone.

## Steps

### 1. Check whether the attempt can be restored
A completed role-play attempt cannot be reset or reopened. You also cannot undo an accidental **Retake** and restore the earlier attempt.

### 2. Choose how to give another opportunity
Use one of these options, depending on your goal.

Increase the scenario’s attempt limit when you want all assigned learners to have more attempts.

Reassign the scenario through the **Editor** or **Manager** portal when you want to give one learner another opportunity.

### 3. Match the option to your need
Use the scenario’s attempt limit for group-wide changes. Use reassignment for a single learner.

- Allow all assigned learners more attempts: increase the scenario’s attempt limit.
- Give an individual learner another opportunity: reassign the scenario through the **Editor** or **Manager** portal.
- Restore a previously completed attempt: not supported.
- Undo an accidental retake and restore the previous attempt: not supported.

## Things to know

:::warning
Changing the attempt limit affects the entire scenario and applies to all users assigned to it.
:::

:::note
You cannot restore a completed attempt through the platform.
:::
