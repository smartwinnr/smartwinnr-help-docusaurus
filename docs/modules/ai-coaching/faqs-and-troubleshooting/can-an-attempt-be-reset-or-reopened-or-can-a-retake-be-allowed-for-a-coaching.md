> **At a glance** - You cannot reset or reopen a completed role-play attempt. To give a learner another chance, increase the attempt limit or reassign the scenario.

A completed role-play attempt cannot be reset, reopened, or restored. If a learner selects **Retake**, the previous attempt stays closed.

To give a learner another chance, you can either increase the scenario’s attempt limit or have an **Editor** or **Manager** reassign the scenario.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to understand what happens after a learner finishes a role-play attempt.

- A learner completed an attempt and wants to try again.
- A learner accidentally selected **Retake**.
- You want to give every assigned learner more attempts.
- You want to give one learner another opportunity without changing the scenario for everyone.

## Before you start

- You must have access to the specific scenario through your Editor role, or you must be assigned as the Coach for the learner (user) in that scenario to reassign it.

## Steps

A completed role-play attempt cannot be reset or reopened. You also cannot undo an accidental **Retake** and restore the earlier attempt.

## Choose how to give another opportunity
Use one of these options, depending on your goal.

### Option 1: Increase the scenario’s attempt limit
This can be used when you want all assigned learners to have more attempts. 

To increase the number of attempts follow the below steps:

1. Goto Editor Portal > Choose **Video coaching** Under coaching section > goto the scenario where you would like to increase the attempt limit.

![Coaching view page](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwz98eat.png)

2. Click on **Edit** at top right corner and goto section **'Schedule and Attempts'**. 

![coaching edit page](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwz99l0n.png)

3. Here, you can increase the number of attempts under the option **Maximum Attempt Count**. Please note that once the coaching is created, you can only increase the maximum attempt count. Reducing it isn’t allowed.

![Maximum allowed attempt](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwz9euv5.png)

### Option 2: Reassign the scenario to the required users

You can reassign the scenario through the **Editor** portal, if you are the editor who has access to the scenario. If you are a reviewer to the user for the specific scenario, then you can reassign the scenario to the user from **Manager** view.

### To reassign the scenario through the **Editor** portal, follow the below steps:

1. Goto Editor Portal > Choose **Video coaching** Under coaching section > goto the **Analytics** for the scenario where you would like to reassign it for a user.

2. Navigate to the **Coaching Status** section and search for the required user

![Coaching status page](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwz9or2y.png)

3. Click on the user's name and it will redirect you to the user's response video, where you can view each attempt made by the user. 

4. Goto the **Hamburger Menu** and choose **Reassign Coaching** option to reassign the scenario to the user, which will give the user an additonal attempt for the scenario.

![Choose reassign from the hamburger menu](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwza9v8p.png)


### To reassign the scenario through the **Manager** view, follow the below steps:

1. Log in to your SmartWinnr account through the mobile app or web version. If you have both User and Manager roles, switch to the **Manager view**.

2. Navigate to **Left Menu** > **Coaching** and select the required scenario under the **'My reviews'** tab.

![My review tab](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwzaf9mg.png)

3. You will see a list of users whose submitted responses are pending your review for that scenario.

4. Select the relevant user to open their response video for the scenario. Click the **Reassign** option in the top-right corner to reassign the scenario to the user. This will give the learner an additional attempt to complete the scenario.

![Reassign from manager view](/img/helpscout/authored/can-an-attempt-be-reset-or-reopened-or-can-a-retake-be-allowed-for-a-coaching-mwzam929.png)

    Note: You must be assigned as the Coach for the learner in that specific scenario to access it through the Manager view.

### 3. Match the option to your need
Use the scenario’s attempt limit for group-wide changes. Use reassignment for a single learner.

- Allow all assigned learners more attempts: increase the scenario’s attempt limit.
- Give an individual learner another opportunity: reassign the scenario through the **Editor** or **Manager** view.

## Things to know

:::warning
Changing the attempt limit affects the entire scenario and applies to all users assigned to it.
:::
