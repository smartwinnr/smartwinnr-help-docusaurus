> **At a glance** - Use these settings to control how many times learners can retake a role play and what feedback they see after submission. You can also choose who can see submitted videos and AI analytics.

Use these settings to shape the learner experience for a coaching activity. You can limit retake attempts, hide scores, and control visibility for video and AI analytics by audience. You can also show transcript, competency rating, and pace details in learner feedback and coaching analytics.

## When to use this

Use these settings when you need to tailor role play feedback for different audiences.

- Limit how many times a learner can submit the same coaching activity.
- Hide scores from learners or other audiences.
- Show submitted videos only to selected audiences.
- Control who can see AI analytics.
- Review competency, transcript, and pace information in coaching results.

## Before you start

You need access to the coaching activity you created.

## Steps

### 1. Open the coaching settings
Go to the coaching activity you created. From there, use **Schedule and Attempts** to view **Maximum Attempt Count** and enter the value you need.

![allowed retake attempts](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtshzgin.png)

### 2. Set the maximum attempt count
In **Maximum Attempt Count**, enter the number of retakes you want to allow. For example, if you set it to 10, learners can attempt the role play up to 10 times.

If you later enter a lower number, SmartWinnr keeps the current value. You can increase the count after saving.

### 3. Control score visibility
In **Visibility**, use **Disable Scoring** to hide AI scores from coaching pages and coaching reports. Clear the checkbox to show scores again.

![show or hide scores](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtsi7u1w.png)

### 4. Choose who sees AI analytics
Use the AI analytics visibility setting to decide which audiences can view analytics. You can grant access to editors, managers, and users by selecting the relevant checkboxes.

![per-audience AI analytics visibility](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtsidv59.png)

### 5. Show or hide submitted video
Use the video visibility setting to control who can see the submitted video. You can enable or disable visibility for editors, managers, and users.

If you do not want a specific audience to see the video, clear that audience's checkbox.

![per-audience video visibility](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtsj7wu7.png)

### 6. Review competency and pace details
Use the competency rating graph to review competency results in graph form.

![competency rating graph](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtsio3ln.png)

You can also view pace details in the analytics tab.

![pace](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtsiuacz.png)
