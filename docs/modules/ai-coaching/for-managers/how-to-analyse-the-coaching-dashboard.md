> **At a glance** - The Coaching Dashboard helps you review participation, performance, competency development, and skill improvement in one place.

Use the Coaching Dashboard to understand how coaching is performing across learners, groups, and time periods. You can compare attempts, review skill trends, and use AI-generated insights to spot areas that need attention.

## When to use this

Use the Coaching Dashboard when you want to review coaching outcomes and decide where to focus next.

- Monitor participation, completion, and session activity.
- Compare scores across attempts to see whether performance is improving.
- Identify skill gaps across groups, regions, or competencies.
- Review submission patterns over a selected date range.
- Use AI insights, key takeaways, and improvement areas to guide coaching plans.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Coaching Dashboard

Go to **Editor > Coaching > Video Coaching > Coaching Dashboard**.

![Navigate to the Coaching Dashboard](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv69qw7i.png)

### 2. Set your date range and filters

Use the controls at the top of the dashboard to narrow the data you want to review.

Choose **30D**, **90D**, or the calendar option to set a custom date range.

![the date range](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6che9i.png)

![calendar option to choose a custom date range](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6ci5uw.png)

Click **Filters** to open the filter panel. From there, you can filter by business unit, country, coaching type, specific coaching, group, user type, and metatags.

Click **Apply Filters** to refresh the dashboard with your selections. Click **Clear All** to remove them.

![Filters](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6csyla.png)

Review the applied filters shown below the dashboard heading before you analyze the results.

### 3. Review the Overview Analysis tab

Use **Overview Analysis** to get a high-level view of coaching activity, learner performance, and skill development.

Review the summary metrics for active learners, average attempts, training hours saved, and average session duration.

![summary metrics](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6hroiw.png)

Check **Score Over Attempts** to compare learner scores across attempts. Use the menu on the chart to download it as a PNG or JPEG.

![Score Over Attempts](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6htkj7.png)

Review **Skill Performance Improvement** to compare first-attempt and latest-attempt performance across skills.

![Skill Performance Improvement](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6hwt6z.png)

Use **Performance Trends** and **Key Focus Areas** to understand overall coaching effectiveness and the skills that need attention.

![Performance Trends, Key Focus Areas](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6hwt9p.png)

Review **Skills Development** to compare current skill levels with target levels. Use the chart menu to download the graph as a PNG or JPEG.

![Skills Development](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6hwtcv.png)

Check **AI Insights** to review AI-generated observations about learning patterns, skill development, and improvement opportunities.

![AI Insights](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv6hwtgu.png)

### 4. Review competency insights

Use **Competency & Skills Insights** to study competency development in more detail.

Review **Competency Growth Over Time** to track how skills progress across weeks. Select a group from the drop-down to focus on it, and click a skill in the legend to highlight its graph. Use the chart menu to download the view as a PNG or JPEG.

![Competency Growth Over Time](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv7wsscx.png)

Review **Competency Performance by Attempt** to compare performance across successive attempts.

![Competency Performance by Attempt](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv7xpprb.png)

Use **Competency Trends Analysis** to review identified trends and areas that need more focus.

![Competency Trends Analysis](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv7xsxla.png)

Check **Learning Path Recommendations** for suggested learning paths that support structured skill development.

![Learning Path Recommendations](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv7xwq68.png)

Use **Competency Assessment Heatmap** to compare competency scores across user groups and skill groups. Change the displayed groups with the drop-down menus, then download the chart from the menu if needed.

![Competency Assessment Heatmap](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv7yj08k.png)

Review **Soft Skills Assessment** to see scores, performance labels, and recommendations for skills such as fluency, clarity, pace, and gestures.

![Soft Skills Assessment](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv81ewdk.png)

### 5. Review participation insights

Use **Participation Insights** to understand who is participating, how often they complete coaching, and how they submit attempts.

Review the summary metrics for total participants, total completed, average score, and average attempts.

![Participation Insights tab](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv81lcsg.png)

Check **Submission Trends Over Time** to see how submission activity changes across the selected period.

Review the **Submissions** table for attempt-level details such as user, coaching title, attempt, score, best score, total time, date, and available business details.

![Review the Submissions table](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv820k6x.png)

Use **Sort by** to organize records, **Filter** to narrow the list, and **Report** to access reporting options. Use the pagination controls to move through the records.

![Submissions](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv822yyj.png)

Review **Key Takeaways** for positive trends and coaching outcomes.

![Key Takeaways](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv8248li.png)

Review **Areas of Improvement** to identify development areas that need more attention.

![Areas of Improvement](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mv8248ni.png)

### 6. Compare the results

Use all three tabs together to decide what to do next.

Compare participation and completion metrics with performance and competency trends. Review individual submissions to understand learner-level engagement. Use AI insights, key takeaways, and improvement areas to identify coaching priorities.

Revisit the dashboard with different date ranges to track progress over time.

## Things to know

:::note
Dashboard metrics depend on the date range and filters you select.
:::

:::note
Use the tab that matches your goal: overall performance, competency development, or participation.
:::

:::note
Review individual scores alongside attempt history and group trends for a fuller view of coaching effectiveness.
:::

:::note
Treat AI-generated insights and recommendations as inputs when planning coaching improvements.
:::
