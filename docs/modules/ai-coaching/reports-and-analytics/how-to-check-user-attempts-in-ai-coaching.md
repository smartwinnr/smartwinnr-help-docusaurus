> **At a glance** - Managers can easily track user engagement by checking how many users have submitted their video scenarios.

Managers can view how many users have attempted the scenarios in AI Coaching. This feature helps you monitor user participation and engagement effectively.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to:
- Track the progress of users in AI Coaching scenarios.
- Identify users who have not yet submitted their videos.
- Ensure accountability and encourage participation in coaching sessions.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to Manager View
Access the Manager View of your SmartWinnr account.

### 2. Click on **My Coaching**
Navigate to the **My Coaching** section to see the list of coaching sessions where you are designated as the coach. 

![How to check the coaching from Manager view](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrujrq0x.png)

### 3. Check the Number of Submissions in the Coaching
Click on the "My Review" section and it will list down all the coachings in which this manager set as the coach. Against each coaching name, you will see how many users have completed their video submissions.

![number of submission in the coaching](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-check-user-attempts-in-ai-coaching-mrukjgq7.jpg)

In the above example, out of 86 users assigned, 2 users have submitted the coaching video. The remaining 84 users are yet to submit the video.

## Tips
- Regularly check user submissions to identify any patterns in participation.
- Encourage users who have not submitted their videos to complete their scenarios for better learning outcomes.
