> **At a glance** - The Participation Insights tab provides a comprehensive view of learner engagement, activity trends, and AI-generated performance insights.

The Participation Insights tab offers a high-level overview of participation, activity trends, and AI-generated insights. It focuses on learner participation, completion rates, attempts, scores, and submission activity.

![Participation Insights Tab](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msy6cab2.png)

## When to use this
Use the Participation Insights tab to:

- Monitor learner engagement over time.
- Identify trends in participation and activity.
- Analyze performance insights generated by AI.
- Adjust coaching strategies based on data-driven insights.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Participation Insights Tab
Navigate to the **Coaching Dashboard** and select the **Participation Insights** tab to view the relevant metrics.

### 2. Adjust the Time Range
At the top of the dashboard, you can see the current time range displayed. The default setting is "Last 90 days," but you can adjust this using the filter buttons.

**To change the time range:**

1. Click one of the preset buttons (30d or 90d) in the upper-right corner.
2. Or click the calendar icon to select a custom date range.

![Date range filter](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msyezv3v.png)

All metrics on the dashboard update based on the selected time period.

### 3. Review Participation Summary Metrics
The top section provides a quick summary of learner participation during the selected period.

- **Total Participants**: Shows the total number of unique users who have been assigned coaching during the selected period.
- **Total Completed**: Displays the number of participants who have successfully completed the assigned coaching activity.
- **Average Score**: Indicates the average score achieved across all coaching attempts during the selected period.
- **Average Attempts**: Reflects the average number of coaching attempts made per user.

![Summary Metrics](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msy76fwk.png)


### 4. Analyze Submission Trends Over Time
This chart shows the total number of roleplay attempts submitted each week during the selected time period. The graph displays the number of submissions during each period, allowing Trainers to identify changes in participation and activity over time.

![All metrics on the dashboard update based on the selected time period.](/img/helpscout/editors/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-1.png)

**What it shows:**

- Weekly submission volume across all learners.
- Trends in practice activity over time.
- Spikes or drops in engagement.

**How to read it:**

- Hover over any data point to see the exact number of submissions for that week.
- An upward trend indicates growing engagement with the coaching program.
- A declining trend may signal that learners need reminders or additional motivation.
- Irregular patterns might reflect seasonal factors, training cycles, or campaign launches.

**Why this matters:** Use this chart to identify when your team is most active and when participation drops off. This helps you time coaching initiatives, send reminders, or launch new campaigns when engagement is highest.

### 5. Review Submissions
The Submissions section provides detailed, user-level information about individual coaching attempts. This section allows Trainers to move from the high-level participation metrics to the actual submissions made by individual learners.

![Submissions](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msyeq4hd.png)

### 6. Examine Key Takeaways
This section provides auto-generated narrative insights about overall performance and trends. It highlights positive trends and important patterns that Trainers may want to be aware of.

![Key Takeaways](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msyer05f.png)

**What it includes:**

- Quarter-over-quarter improvement percentages.
- Observations about program effectiveness.
- Trends in participation consistency.
- Skill development highlights.

**Key details:**

- All content is auto-generated by SmartWinnr AI.
- Updates automatically as new data becomes available.
- Provides context for the numbers you see in the charts.

**Example insights you might see:**

- "Sustained improvement across all competencies with an overall score increase of 28%."
- "Participation consistency improved by 45% through personalized learning paths."
- "Skill development in trust building and call closing shows strongest progress."

Use these insights to quickly understand what's working and where to focus your efforts.

### 7. Identify Areas of Improvement
This section highlights specific areas where your coaching program can be strengthened. It identifies areas where additional coaching or intervention may be beneficial and helps pinpoint broader development opportunities based on the available coaching data.

![Areas of Improvement](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-the-participation-insights-tab-on-the-ai-coaching-dashboard-msyeshde.png)

**What it includes:**

- Strategic recommendations for skill development.
- Focus areas for different performance levels.
- Suggestions for comprehensive skill enhancement.

**Key details:**

- Auto-generated by SmartWinnr AI.
- Updates dynamically based on current performance data.
- Tailored to your organization's specific needs.

**Example recommendations you might see:**

- "Strategic improvement: Advanced competency development for mid-level performers."
- "Focus: Comprehensive skill enhancement across all competency domains."

Use these recommendations to prioritize your coaching strategy and allocate resources where they'll have the most impact.

## Tips and best practices

- **Review the dashboard weekly** to stay on top of participation trends and catch potential issues early.
- **Compare time periods** by switching between 30-day and 90-day views to see both short-term and long-term trends.
- **Focus on improvement rates** rather than absolute scores. A learner who starts at 40% and reaches 70% is making excellent progress.
- **Use the heatmap strategically** to identify which groups need targeted support and which can serve as mentors for others.
- **Pay attention to soft skills** as much as hard skills. Strong communication abilities are essential for sales success.
- **Monitor the gap** between logged-in users and actively participating users. A large gap suggests barriers to completion.
- **Use AI-generated insights** to uncover patterns you might not notice in the raw data alone.
- **Celebrate wins** when you see strong upward trends in competency charts.
- **Act on Learning Path Recommendations** to create structured development programs.
