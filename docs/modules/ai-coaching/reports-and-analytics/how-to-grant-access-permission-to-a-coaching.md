> **At a glance** - Grant access to specific editors for a coaching program in SmartWinnr to enhance collaboration and management.

To grant access to a coaching program, you will follow a series of steps in your SmartWinnr Editor account. This allows selected editors to view and manage the coaching from their respective accounts.

## When to use this

Use this process when you need to:

- Collaborate with other editors on a specific coaching program.
- Manage access permissions effectively within your team.
- Ensure that only authorized editors can view certain coaching content.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your SmartWinnr Editor account

Start by logging in to your SmartWinnr Editor account with your credentials.

### 2. Select the Coaching

Navigate to the **Coaching** section and select the coaching program from the coaching list page.

![Selecting the Coaching](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrumssnt.png)

### 3. Go to the Coaching Settings

Go to the coaching settings page and click on the **Hamburger menu** in the top right corner. Select the **Access Permission** option.

![Select Access Permission](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-grant-access-permission-to-a-coaching-mrumwjf8.png)

### 4. Select the Editors

You will be directed to a page displaying the details of other editors who can access this coaching program.

![Select the Editors](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-grant-access-permission-to-a-coaching-mrumwqer.png)

### 5. Select Editors and Save changes

Choose the required editors and click on the **Save** button to apply the changes. The selected editors will now have access to this coaching and can view it in their **Other Coaching** section.
