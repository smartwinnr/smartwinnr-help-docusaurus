This feature allows editors, trainers, and admins to view video coaching submissions from users in real time. You can monitor who has submitted videos, review their AI-generated scores and feedback, and track which submissions have been reviewed by coaches or managers.

> **At a glance** - This article explains how to access and view submissions as they happen, including detailed steps for monitoring user activity and evaluating submissions.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you need to:

* Monitor submission activity across your coaching assignments.
* Check the progress of individual users.
* Review AI evaluations and scores for submitted videos.
* Identify which submissions still need coach review.
* Track completion rates for coaching assignments.

## Before you start

Ensure you have the appropriate access rights as an editor to view submissions in real time.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Coaching submissions list

1. From the left navigation menu, click **Video Coaching** under the **COACHING** section.
2. Select **View Coaching** from the submenu.
3. You'll see a list of all coachings you have created, with the following information:
    * **Coaching Title**: The name of the coaching assignment.
    * **Response Submitted**: Shows completion percentage and submission count (e.g., "2/8" means 2 users submitted out of 8 assigned).
    * **Review Done**: Shows review progress (e.g., "1/2" means 1 submission reviewed by the coach out of 2 total submissions).
    * **Status**: Indicates whether the coaching is Active, Closed, or Ends in a specific timeframe.
    * **Analytics**: Icon to view detailed analytics.

![Coaching submissions list](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-view-submissions-as-they-happen-1.png)

### 2. View detailed analytics for a coaching assignment

4. In the **Analytics** column, click the **analytics icon** next to the coaching assignment you want to review.
5. The Coaching Analytics page opens, showing:
    * Completion status charts, including video submission and coach review progress.
    * Participation breakdown by location or team.
    * Average AI scores for submissions.
    * A detailed table listing all participants and their submission statuses.

### 3. View an individual user's submission

6. Goto the'Coaching Status' table available in the Analytics page, find the user whose submission you want to view.
7. Click on the **Participant Name** (displayed as a blue link). Or search for the user you would like to view the submission status and response video.

![Individual user's submission details](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-view-submissions-as-they-happen-2.png)

8. This will open the individual submission page, displaying:
    * **Coach name**: The assigned coach or manager who will review this submission.
    * **Submission date and time**: When the user submitted their video.
    * **Video player**: Watch the submitted video directly within the interface.
    * **Attempts dropdown**: If the user submitted multiple times, select which attempt to view. To view each response video and analytics for each attempt, select the attempt number from the dropdown list.

![attempt count](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-submissions-as-they-happen-mtie4t79.png)

### 4. Review submission details using the tabs

Once you're viewing an individual submission, use the tabs at the top to see different aspects of the evaluation:

* **Scores**: View the overall AI score, response duration, strengths, and improvement areas.
* **Features**: Check if the user covered required product features or keywords, and view any banned words detected during the submission.
* **Analytics**: See speaking pace (words per minute), score trends across attempts, and progress on individual objectives.
* **Evaluation Criteria**: Review detailed scoring for each competency or knowledge area the AI evaluated.
* **Compliance and Fact Check**: See compliance violations, factual errors, and suggestions for balanced messaging.
* **Soft Skill**: Access detailed performance metrics under this section.

![Detailed performance metrics](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-view-submissions-as-they-happen-mssvk73t.png)

## What happens next

After viewing submissions:

* You can navigate back to the Coaching Analytics page to view other submissions.
* Coaches or managers can use this information to provide additional feedback or coaching.
* You can track which submissions still need human review in the "Review Done" column.

## Tips

* The submission count format "2/8" tells you exactly how many users out of your total assigned have completed the coaching.
* Use the **Attempts** dropdown to compare a user's improvement across multiple submissions.
* The AI provides both strengths and improvement areas automatically, which can guide coaching conversations.
* Filter options at the top of the Coaching page (Date Range, Business Unit, Coaching Type, Status) help you narrow down to specific coaching assignments.

## Things to know

* Only submitted videos appear in the Analytics view. Users who haven't submitted yet won't show in the participant table.
* The review count reflects reviews completed by coaches or managers, not the AI evaluation (which happens automatically).
* Scores and feedback are generated by AI immediately upon submission, so you can view them right away without waiting for human review.
