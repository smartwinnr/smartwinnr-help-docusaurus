> **At a glance** - The SmartWinnr AI Coaching Dashboard provides comprehensive insights into team performance and improvement in AI roleplay practice. Use it to monitor participation, track skill development, and make data-driven coaching decisions.

The **AI Coaching Dashboard** offers a complete view of your team's performance in AI roleplay practice. It displays participation trends, skill improvement over time, and areas where learners may need additional support. This dashboard is designed for trainers and editors to monitor coaching activity, track skill development, and identify training opportunities across your organization.

## When to use this
Use the AI Coaching Dashboard to:

* Monitor how many team members are actively practicing their skills.
* Track learning progress and score improvements across multiple attempts.
* Identify which skills are improving and which need more focus.
* Measure the time savings from using AI coaching instead of traditional roleplay methods.
* Review AI-generated insights about learner behavior and performance trends.
* Compare performance across different teams, regions, or organizational groups.
* Make data-driven decisions about where to focus your coaching efforts.

## Before you start
Ensure you have access to the AI Coaching Dashboard and the necessary permissions to view team performance data.

## Navigating the dashboard tabs
The AI Coaching Dashboard consists of three main tabs at the top, each providing different insights:

### 1. Overview Analysis
The **Overview Analysis** tab shows high-level participation metrics and engagement trends. This is where you can quickly assess overall team activity and identify patterns in participation.

![Overview Analysis tab showing high-level participation metrics and engagement trends](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-track-improvement-mssnfnnu.png)

### 2. Competency & Skills Insights
The **Competency & Skills Insights** tab displays detailed skill development and performance by region or group. This allows you to drill down into specific competencies and see how different segments of your team are performing.

![Competency & Skills Insights tab displaying detailed skill development](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-track-improvement-mssnfv2m.png)

### 3. Participation Insights
The **Participation Insights** tab provides metrics on session duration and engagement patterns. Use this information to understand how long team members are spending on practice sessions and how engaged they are during those sessions.

![Participation Insights tab providing metrics on session duration and engagement patterns](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-track-improvement-mssng2vf.png)

Click each tab to view different aspects of your coachings performance. This structured approach helps you gather insights effectively and make informed decisions.

![Click each tab to view different aspects of your coaching program's performance](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-track-improvement-1.png)
