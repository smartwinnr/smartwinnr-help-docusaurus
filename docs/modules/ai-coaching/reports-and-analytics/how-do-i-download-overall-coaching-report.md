> **At a glance** - The Overall Coaching Report provides a comprehensive summary of all coaching activities across users, allowing you to download and analyze coaching data efficiently.

The Overall Coaching Report summarizes all coaching activities across all users. This report is useful for tracking performance and understanding coaching effectiveness.

## When to use this
Use the Overall Coaching Report when you need to:

- Analyze coaching activities across all users.
- Monitor performance and effectiveness of coaching sessions.
- Generate reports for administrative reviews or presentations.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to the platform
Access your SmartWinnr account using your credentials.

### 2. Click the **Editor** dropdown menu
Locate the **Editor** dropdown menu in the top navigation bar.

### 3. Select **Coaching**
From the dropdown, choose the **Coaching** option.

### 4. Click **View Coachings**
Navigate to the **View Coachings** section to access coaching data.

### 5. Click the **Hamburger menu**
Find and click the **Hamburger menu** icon, typically located in the upper left corner.

### 6. Select **Overall Coaching Summary Report**
From the menu options, choose **Overall Coaching Summary Report**.

![Select option ](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-download-overall-coaching-report-mt2jzufz.png)

### 7. Apply the desired filters
Select the required Meta Tags and Date Range (if applicable).

Select the “Include All Coaching Tabs” checkbox to download reports for coachings under both “My Coaching” and “Other Coaching.”

To download reports for demo users, select the “Include Demo Users” checkbox. Click the **Download** button to download the report.

![select the specifications](/img/helpscout/authored/how-do-i-download-overall-coaching-report-mt2kp8kl.png)

### 8. Go to **Reports → Generated Reports**
Navigate to the **Reports** section and select **Generated Reports**.

### 9. Locate your report
Find your report in the list and click the **download icon** to save it to your computer.

![Locate your report and click the download icon to save it to your computer](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-download-overall-coaching-report-3.png)
