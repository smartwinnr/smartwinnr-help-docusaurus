> **At a glance** - This article explains how to download the Attempt-wise Coaching Report, which details all attempts for a specific coaching session.

The Attempt-wise Coaching Report provides insights into all attempts made for a particular coaching session. You can download this report to analyze performance and engagement.

## When to use this
Use the Attempt-wise Coaching Report when you want to:
- Review individual attempts for a specific coaching session.
- Analyze performance trends over time.
- Identify areas for improvement in coaching effectiveness.

## Before you start
Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access coaching analytics.

## Steps

### 1. Go to **Editor → Coaching → View Coachings**
Navigate to the coaching section within the editor.

### 2. Scroll to the coaching you want to report on
Locate the specific coaching session you wish to analyze.

### 3. Click **View Analytics Icon**
Select the analytics icon associated with the coaching.

![Click View Analytics Icon](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-download-attempt-wise-coaching-report-1.png)

### 4. The coaching analytics page will open
You will see detailed analytics for the selected coaching.

### 5. Click the **Hamburger menu** in the top right corner
Access additional options by clicking the hamburger menu.

### 6. Select **Attempt-wise Overall Coaching Report**
Choose the Attempt-wise Overall Coaching Report from the menu.

![Select Attempt-wise Overall Coaching Report](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-download-attempt-wise-coaching-report-2.png)

### 7. Go to **Reports → Generated Reports**
Navigate to the reports section to find your generated reports.

### 8. Locate your report and click the **download icon** to save it to your computer
Find the report you generated and click the download icon to save it.

![Locate your report and click the download icon to save it to your computer](/img/helpscout/editors/how-do-i-download-attempt-wise-coaching-report-3.png)

## Tips
- Regularly check for new reports to stay updated on coaching performance.

## Things to know
- The report will include all attempts made by participants, providing a comprehensive overview of engagement.
- If you encounter issues downloading the report, check your internet connection or permissions.
