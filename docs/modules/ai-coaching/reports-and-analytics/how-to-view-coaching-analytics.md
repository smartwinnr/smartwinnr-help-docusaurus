> **At a glance** - The Coaching Analytics page provides insights into coaching assignments, helping you monitor progress and performance.

The Coaching Analytics page in the Editor Portal offers detailed insights into coaching assignments across your organization. You can monitor learner participation, coaching completion, AI evaluation scores, coach reviews, and competency-based performance through interactive dashboards and reports.

## When to use this
Use Coaching Analytics to:
- Track coaching effectiveness and participant engagement.
- Identify learning gaps and areas for improvement.
- Measure the performance of both learners and coaches.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to the Editor Portal
Access your account to begin viewing coaching analytics.

### 2. Navigate to Coaching
Go to **Coaching → Video Coaching → View Coaching** in the menu.

### 3. Locate the coaching scenario
Find the specific coaching scenario you want to analyze.

### 4. Open the Coaching Analytics dashboard
Click the **Analytics** button to view detailed coaching analytics as shown below.
![You can follow the below steps to check analytics button in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-coaching-analytics-mrw0dg5t.png)

### 5. Use filters for specific insights
Apply available filters to narrow down analytics for a specific coaching scenario or user group.

## Coaching Status
The Coaching Status section provides scoring metrics for the selected coaching assignment:
![You can follow the below steps to check scoring metrics analytics in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-view-coaching-analytics-mrw0rx5o.png)
- **AI Average Score**: The AI Average Score represents the evaluation progress and average performance of participants based on AI-generated assessments. It indicates how many participant submissions have been evaluated by the AI out of the total number of participants assigned to the coaching scenario. For example, a value of 7/9 means that the AI has successfully evaluated 7 participant submissions out of the 9 assigned, providing an overview of the AI evaluation coverage and scoring progress.

- **Coach Average Score**: The Coach Average Score reflects the evaluation progress and average performance based on manual assessments conducted by the assigned coach. It shows the number of participant submissions reviewed by the coach compared to the total participants assigned to the coaching scenario. For example, a value of 1/9 indicates that the coach has completed the evaluation for 1 participant submission out of the 9 assigned, allowing administrators to track the coach's review progress.

- **Weighted Average Score**: The Weighted Average Score provides a consolidated performance metric by combining both the AI Evaluation Score and the Coach Evaluation Score. This score offers a balanced and comprehensive view of participant performance by considering evaluations from both AI and human coaches. It helps administrators and coaches assess overall coaching effectiveness using a single, unified performance indicator.
