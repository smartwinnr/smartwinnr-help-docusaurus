> **At a glance** - Enable the setting to allow downloading coaching feedback reports as PDFs, then follow the steps to download the report.

You can download coaching feedback reports in PDF format for learners and managers. This feature requires enabling a setting in the coaching module. Once enabled, users can easily download their feedback reports.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to provide feedback reports to learners and managers. This is particularly useful for:

- Sharing performance evaluations with learners.
- Allowing managers to access feedback for their team members.
- Keeping a record of coaching sessions in PDF format.

## Before you start

Your organization needs to enable the setting that allows downloading AI feedback as PDFs in the coaching module.

## Steps

### 1. Access the coaching list

Navigate to **Coaching → View Coaching** to see the list of created coachings.

![list of coachings page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx240st.png)

### 2. Enable the download setting

Open the coaching you want to modify and click on the **Edit** option. In the coaching settings page, check the box labeled **Allow learners and managers to download AI feedback as PDF**.

![where to enable setting in coaching setting page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx2cws0.png)

### 3. Save your changes

After enabling the setting, click the **Save** button at the bottom of the page.

### How it appears in User/Manager View

In the user and manager view, select the number of attempts. A **Download** option will be available next to the attempts. Click this option to download the feedback report as a PDF. It will show once the user has submitted any response to that coaching.

![how visible in userview](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx3tpgd.png)

The downloaded PDF will include all basic details of the coaching, including coaching details, scores, and evaluation criteria.

## Tips

- Ensure that the setting is enabled for each coaching session where you want to allow downloads.
- Inform learners and managers about the availability of their feedback reports for easy access.
