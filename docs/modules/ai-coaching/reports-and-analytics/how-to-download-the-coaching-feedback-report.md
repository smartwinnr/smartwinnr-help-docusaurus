> **At a glance** - Enable the setting to allow downloading coaching feedback reports as PDFs, then follow the steps to download the report.

You can let learners and managers download coaching feedback reports as PDF files. To make the download available, enable the setting in the coaching module. After that, users can download their feedback reports from the coaching view.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to share feedback reports with learners and managers. It is useful for:

- Sharing performance evaluations with learners.
- Allowing managers to access feedback for their team members.
- Keeping a record of coaching sessions in PDF format.

## Before you start

Your organization needs the setting that allows downloading AI feedback as PDFs enabled in the coaching module.

## Steps

### 1. Access the coaching list

Go to **Coaching → View Coaching** to see the list of created coachings.

![list of coachings page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx240st.png)

### 2. Enable the download setting

Open the coaching you want to modify and click **Edit**. On the coaching settings page, select **Allow learners and managers to download AI feedback as PDF**.

![where to enable setting in coaching setting page](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx2cws0.png)

### 3. Save your changes

After you enable the setting, click **Save** at the bottom of the page.

### 4. Download the feedback report

In the user and manager view, select the number of attempts. A **Download** option appears next to the attempts. Click it to download the feedback report as a PDF. It appears after the user submits any response to that coaching.

![how visible in userview](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mrx3tpgd.png)

The downloaded PDF includes all basic details of the coaching, including coaching details, scores, and evaluation criteria.

## Tips

- Enable the setting for each coaching session where you want to allow downloads.
- Let learners and managers know their feedback reports are available for easy access.
