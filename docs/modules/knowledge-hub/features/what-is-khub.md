> **At a glance** - KHub is a centralized knowledge repository that provides employees with instant access to essential content, enhancing their performance and readiness

KHub is a knowledge/content repository for your employees to help them excel at their jobs. This feature lets you share the right content with your team at the right time so that they are prepared to handle any situation.

Product videos, installation manuals, service manuals, sales decks, training videos - you name it. With KHub, the right content is just a search away for your employees. Upload your documents to SmartWinnr, and your sales reps can access them from the mobile app anytime, anywhere.

## When to use this
Use KHub when you need to provide your team with easy access to important resources. This feature is ideal for:

- Distributing training materials to new hires.
- Sharing product updates and manuals.
- Ensuring sales teams have the latest sales decks and presentations.
- Providing quick access to frequently used documents.

## Multiple Language Support

Are your documents in different languages? Don't worry. SmartWinnr supports documents in multiple languages. You can upload documents in various languages for teams from different countries.

## Intelligent Search

SmartWinnr is powered by a highly intelligent, intuitive, and accurate search. It helps your teams search through a vast number of documents quickly and easily.

## Offline Access

SmartWinnr provides offline access to these documents, but only for documents that you select. This feature ensures that it won’t consume more space on your phone than necessary.
