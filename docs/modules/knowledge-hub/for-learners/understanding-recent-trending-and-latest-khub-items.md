> **At a glance** - This article explains how to access and understand the different categories of items available in the KHub.

Once a document is uploaded in the KHub by the **EDITOR**, it becomes available for all team members to search and access. Follow the steps below to view the folders and items in the KHub.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you want to find specific documents or items in the KHub. This is useful for:

- Accessing trending documents that are popular among team members.
- Finding recently searched items for quick reference.
- Exploring newly added documents for the latest information.
- Reviewing favorite items that you or your colleagues have marked.

## Steps

### 1. Open the KHub
Open the SmartWinnr app and navigate through the left menu bar. Click on **KHub**. This opens the KHub page.

![KHub items list](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-use-khub-in-the-smartwinnr-app-mssz9qn5.png)

### 2. View KHub categories
On the KHub page, you will see all the KHub items and folders. These items are categorized into four sections: **Trending**, **Recent**, **New**, and **Favorites**.

### 3. Understand the Trending category
**Trending**: This section shows all the KHub items that are trending and viewed by most people.

![Trending items](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-use-khub-in-the-smartwinnr-app-msszbv3x.png)

### 4. Check Recent Searches
**Recent Searches**: As the name suggests, this section shows the KHub items that people have recently searched.

![Recently searched items](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-use-khub-in-the-smartwinnr-app-msszb408.png)

### 5. Explore New items
**New**: This section shows the newly added KHub items.

![New items](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-use-khub-in-the-smartwinnr-app-msszcy9c.png)

### 6. Review Favorites
**Favorites**: This section shows items that have been added as favorites.

![Favorite items](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-use-khub-in-the-smartwinnr-app-msszd9y9.png)
