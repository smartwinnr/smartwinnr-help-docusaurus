> **At a glance** - SmartFeeds allow you to share concise information with your teams, making critical updates easily accessible.

SmartFeeds enable you to share short, focused snippets of information with your teams. They are designed to help reinforce important messages regularly without requiring lengthy training sessions. SmartFeeds ensure that important information is easily accessible and available for future reference.

## When to use this
Use SmartFeeds to share information that your team needs to remember and apply consistently in their day-to-day work, such as:

* Updates on the latest changes to products
* Announcements of new product launches
* Information about newly introduced services or offers
* Critical updates or reminders that teams should retain over time

All SmartFeeds remain available in the feed for ongoing reference.

## Supported File Types for SmartFeeds

You can share a variety of content formats through SmartFeeds, including:

* **Videos** (e.g., product demonstrations, training videos)
* ** Audios** (eg: Podcasts on required topics)
* **PDF documents** (e.g., product specification sheets, clinical trial data)
* **PowerPoint presentations** (e.g., product knowledge, features and benefits)
* **Links** to external websites or resources
* **Images or posters**
* **Text Content**

## How Are SmartFeeds Delivered?

SmartFeeds are displayed as **Twitter-style feeds** within the SmartWinnr mobile application and web interface, making them easy to consume and revisit.

## Notifications for SmartFeeds

When a SmartFeed is published, recipients are automatically notified through:

* **Email notifications**
* **Push notifications** on their mobile devices

This ensures timely awareness and engagement with the shared content.

## Tips
* Use SmartFeeds for continuous reinforcement of important updates and reminders.
* Regularly share critical information rather than relying solely on formal training sessions.

## Things to know
SmartFeeds are particularly useful for keeping your team informed and engaged with essential updates. They help ensure that important information is readily available for reference whenever needed.
