SmartFeeds enable you to share short, focused snippets of information with your teams. They are designed to help **reinforce critical messages regularly** without requiring lengthy training sessions. SmartFeeds ensure that important information is easily accessible and available for future reference.

## **What Can Be Shared Through SmartFeeds?**

SmartFeeds are ideal for sharing information that your team needs to **remember and apply consistently** in their day-to-day work, such as:

* Updates on the latest changes to products
* Announcements of new product launches
* Information about newly introduced services or offers
* Any critical updates or reminders that teams should retain over time

All SmartFeeds remain available in the feed for ongoing reference.

## Supported File Types for SmartFeeds

You can share a variety of content formats through SmartFeeds, including:

* **Videos** (e.g., product demonstrations, training videos)
* **PDF documents** (e.g., product specification sheets, clinical trial data)
* **PowerPoint presentations** (e.g., product knowledge, features and benefits)
* **Links** to external websites or resources
* **Images or posters**

## How Are SmartFeeds Delivered?

SmartFeeds are displayed as **Twitter-style feeds** within the SmartWinnr mobile application and web interface, making them easy to consume and revisit.

## Notifications for SmartFeeds

When a SmartFeed is published, recipients are automatically notified through:

* **Email notifications**
* **Push notifications** on their mobile devices

This ensures timely awareness and engagement with the shared content.


What are SmartFeeds?

≈ 1 min read · Updated 2026-01-18

SmartFeeds allow you to share short, focused pieces of information with your teams. They are designed to help reinforce important messages regularly without requiring lengthy training sessions.

SmartFeeds make critical information easily accessible to users and allow them to revisit the shared content whenever needed.

What Can Be Shared Through SmartFeeds?

SmartFeeds are useful for sharing information that users need to remember and apply consistently in their day-to-day work, such as:

Product updates and changes
New product launch announcements
Information about newly introduced services or offers
Important business updates, reminders, or messages
Other content that users may need to refer to regularly

Depending on how the SmartFeed is configured, the content can remain available in the user's feed for future reference.

Supported Content Types

SmartFeeds support multiple content formats, allowing you to share information in the format that best suits your audience.

You can share:

Videos: Product demonstrations, training videos, or other video content
PDF documents: Product information, specification sheets, reference materials, and other documents
PowerPoint presentations: Product knowledge, features and benefits, training materials, and presentations
Links: Links to external websites or resources
Images or posters: Visual announcements, reminders, promotional materials, or other important information
How Are SmartFeeds Delivered?

SmartFeeds are presented as a feed-style experience within the SmartWinnr mobile application and web interface. This makes it easy for users to consume short-form content and revisit previously shared information.

Users can also interact with SmartFeeds, depending on the configuration, by engaging with the content.

Notifications for SmartFeeds

When a SmartFeed is shared, recipients can be notified so that they are aware of the new content.

Notifications can be delivered through:

Email notifications
Push notifications on mobile devices

This helps ensure that users are aware of important content and can access it promptly.

Tip: SmartFeeds are particularly useful for continuous reinforcement. Use them to share important updates and reminders regularly rather than relying only on formal training sessions.
