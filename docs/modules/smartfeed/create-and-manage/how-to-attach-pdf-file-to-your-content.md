> **At a glance** - Upload PDF files to share product specification sheets or other documents in your SmartFeed.

You can upload a PDF if you want to share product specification sheets or more in your SmartFeed in SmartWinnr.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you need to share detailed documents, such as product specifications, with your team or audience through SmartFeed.

## Before you start
Ensure you have the necessary permissions to upload files in SmartFeed.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Upload PDF section
In the **Upload PDF** section in SmartFeed, click on the **Choose File** button.

### 2. Select the file to upload
Select the required files from your computer to upload.

![In the Upload PDF section in SmartFeed, click on the 'Choose File' button and then select the required files from the computer to upload.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-attach-pdf-file-to-your-content-1.png)

### 3. Upload the file
After selecting the file, click on the **Upload** button to save the file.

### 4. View the uploaded PDF
The uploaded PDF will now appear in the SmartFeed for users to access.

![This screenshot shows how the PDF appears in the user view of SmartFeed.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-attach-pdf-file-to-your-content-mshox0v8.jpg)
