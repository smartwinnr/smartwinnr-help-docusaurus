> **At a glance** - Learn how to add images to your SmartFeed or attach them to your Questions in SmartWinnr for enhanced content engagement.

Images or pictures can be sent as content in your SmartFeed or attached to your Questions in SmartWinnr. This feature allows you to enrich your content, making it more engaging and informative for your audience.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to:
- Enhance your SmartFeed content with visual elements.
- Provide additional context or information in your Questions.
- Improve user engagement through the use of images.

## Steps

### 1. Click on the image icon
Click on the image icon to attach an image to your SmartFeed or to the question that you create in SmartWinnr.

![Click on the image icon to attach an image to your SmartFeed or to the question that you create in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-add-a-image-to-your-content-1.png)

### 2. Choose the file
Then click on the **Choose File** button to select the required files from your computer.

![Then click on the 'Choose File' button to select the required files from the computer.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-add-a-image-to-your-content-2.png)

### 3. Save your changes
After uploading the image, click the **Save New SmartFeed** button to save the SmartFeed successfully.
