> **At a glance** - You can easily add audio files to your SmartFeed or questions in SmartWinnr by following a few simple steps.

You can send an audio file as part of your SmartFeed or attach an audio file to a question created in SmartWinnr. This feature allows you to enhance your content and provide additional context or information through audio.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to:
- Enhance your SmartFeed content with audio explanations or instructions.
- Provide audio attachments to questions for better clarity and understanding.
- Engage your audience with multimedia content.

## Steps

### 1. Click on the audio icon
To begin, locate the **audio icon** in your SmartFeed or question creation interface. Clicking this icon allows you to attach an audio file.

![Click on the audio icon to attach the audio file to your SmartFeed or to the question that you create in SmartWinnr.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-add-an-audio-file-to-your-content-1.png)

### 2. Select an audio file
Once you click on the audio icon, a pop-up window will appear. In this window, you can select the audio file you wish to upload.

![Once you click on the audio icon, the following pop-up comes up.](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-add-an-audio-file-to-your-content-2.png)

### 3. Choose the file
Click on the '**Choose File**' button to open your file explorer. Navigate to the location of the audio file on your system and select it.

### 4. Confirm the audio format
Please note that the audio file must be in MP3 format. Ensure your file meets this requirement to avoid any upload issues.

By following these steps, you can successfully add an audio file to your SmartFeed or attach it to your questions in SmartWinnr, enhancing your content and engaging your audience effectively.
