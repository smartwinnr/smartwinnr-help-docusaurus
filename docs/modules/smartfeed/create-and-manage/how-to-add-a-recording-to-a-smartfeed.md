> **At a glance** - This article explains how to add recordings to SmartFeeds in SmartWinnr, allowing you to share audio, video, or screen recordings with your audience.

SmartWinnr allows users with the **EDITOR** role to add **recordings** to SmartFeeds. This enables you to share audio, video, or screen recordings directly with your audience, making communication more engaging and informative.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you want to enhance your SmartFeeds with multimedia content. Adding recordings can help clarify instructions, demonstrate processes, or provide engaging updates.

- To share a detailed walkthrough of a process.
- When you want to deliver announcements in a more personal way.
- To provide quick updates or instructions through audio.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to SmartWinnr
Sign in to the **SmartWinnr Admin Portal** with your credentials.

### 2. Access SmartFeeds
Navigate to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > SmartFeeds > View All SmartFeeds**. The SmartFeeds listing page will open.

### 3. Create a New SmartFeed
Click **Add New SmartFeed**. Select the appropriate **Division** for the SmartFeed. Choose **Recording** as the content type.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-add-a-recording-to-a-smartfeed-mt753dez.png)

### 4. Enter SmartFeed Details
Add a **Title** for the SmartFeed. Choose the type of recording you want to include.

![Enter SmartFeed Details](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-add-a-recording-to-a-smartfeed-1.jpg)

Then select the type of recording you would like to create. SmartWinnr supports the following recording options:

- **Screen Recording**: Allows you to record your screen activity. This is useful for demonstrating workflows or explaining step-by-step processes such as data entry or system navigation.

- **Video Recording**: Enables you to record a video using your camera. This option is ideal for personally explaining processes, sharing announcements, or delivering guided instructions.

- **Audio Recording**: Allows you to record voice-only audio. This is suitable for short explanations, instructions, or quick updates.

After completing the recording, click **Save SmartFeed** to successfully create and publish the recording-based SmartFeed.
