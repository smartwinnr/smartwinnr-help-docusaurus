> **At a glance** - Granting access permissions allows selected editors to manage your SmartFeed easily.

You can provide access permissions to a SmartFeed you created, enabling other editors to view and manage it from their accounts. Follow these steps to grant access.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to collaborate with other editors on a SmartFeed. This is helpful in scenarios such as:

- Working on team projects that require multiple contributors.
- Allowing editors to manage content updates.
- Enhancing collaboration by sharing access to resources.

## Steps

### 1. Log in to your SmartWinnr Editor account

Start by logging in to your SmartWinnr Editor account with your credentials.

### 2. Select the SmartFeed

Navigate to the **SmartFeed** section and select the SmartFeed from the list.

![Select smartfeed](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-provide-smartfeed-access-permissions-msa3ay3e.png)

### 3. Open Access Permissions

Go inside the SmartFeed and click on the **Hamburger menu** in the top right corner. Select the **Access Permission** option.

![Select access permission in hamburger menu to provide access permission](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-msa2zuwf.png)

### 4. Select the Editors

You will be directed to a page displaying the list of other editors.

![select editors](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-provide-smartfeed-access-permissions-msa3csx7.png)

### 5. Choose Editors and Save Changes

Select the required editors and click on the **Save** button to apply the changes. The selected editors will now have access to this SmartFeed from the **Other SmartFeeds** tab.
