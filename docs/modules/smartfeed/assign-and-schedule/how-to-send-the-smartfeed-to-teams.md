> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr enables you to send SmartFeeds to teams efficiently, ensuring that all members receive important updates and information in a single step.

SmartWinnr allows you to send SmartFeeds to **groups or teams**, making it easy to reach a large number of users in a single step.

When you assign SmartFeeds to a group, any **new members added to the group** will automatically receive the active SmartFeeds assigned to that group. This means you do not need to resend these SmartFeeds to new users, simplifying the process of keeping everyone informed. Similarly if you **remove a member from the group**, the SmartFeed sent via group **will be removed** from the specific user's feed.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to efficiently distribute information to multiple users at once. Consider sending SmartFeeds to teams in the following scenarios:

* Launching a new initiative or project that requires team awareness.
* Sharing updates and important announcements with all members of a group.
* Ensuring that new team members receive relevant information without additional effort.

## Steps

### 1. Select the SmartFeed

Choose the SmartFeed you want to share with your teams.

Click the **Send** button located at the top-right corner of the SmartFeed.

![When you assign SmartFeeds to a group, any new members added to the group will automatically receive the active SmartFeeds assigned to that](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-send-the-smartfeed-to-teams-mt47jth9.png)

### 2. Choose recipients

This will take you to the page where you can select whom you want to send the SmartFeed.

When sending a SmartFeed, you can choose recipients using different **selection types**:

* **User**: Select individual users to receive the SmartFeed.
* **Group**: Select users based on pre-defined groups within your organization.
* **Metatag**: Select users based on metatags assigned to them.

![Select recipients using different selection types](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-send-the-smartfeed-to-teams-1.png)

### 3. Filter recipients

Choose the **Selection Type** as **Group**. This will list down the groups you have created or you have access to in SmartWinnr.

Choose the groups to whom you would like to share the SmartFeed.You can also seach with the group name to find the group from the list.

![Advanced filters for recipient selection](/img/helpscout/editors/how-to-send-the-smartfeed-to-teams-2.png)

### 4. Send the SmartFeed

After selecting the groups, click on **Send SmartFeed** at the end of the page to successfully send the SmartFeed to the selected group members.

**Note**: SmartFeed visibility is based on the user's group membership. When a new member is added to a group that has a SmartFeed shared with it, the SmartFeed will automatically appear in the user's feed, provided the SmartFeed has not expired. Similarly, when a user is removed from the group, the SmartFeed will no longer be available in their feed.

### 5. View sent history

You can view the history of users/groups with whom the SmartFeed has been shared, along with the dates it was shared, from the Sent History section available within each SmartFeed.

![sent history](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-send-the-smartfeed-to-teams-mt47tom8.png)
