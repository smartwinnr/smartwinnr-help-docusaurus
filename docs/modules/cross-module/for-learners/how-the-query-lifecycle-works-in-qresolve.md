> **At a glance** - QResolve allows you to raise questions, receive expert answers, and track your query's status through various stages.

QResolve helps you submit questions, receive answers from experts, and monitor the status of your queries until they are resolved. Understanding the query lifecycle is essential to effectively use this feature.

## When to use this
Use QResolve when you need assistance or clarification on a topic. This is particularly useful when:
- You have a specific question that requires expert input.
- You want to track the progress of your inquiries.
- You need to confirm if your question has been addressed.
- You want to reopen a query for further clarification.

## Steps

### 1. Submit your query
Post your question in **QResolve**. Your query will be sent to the relevant expert or SPOC for review.

### 2. Receive an answer
Once an expert or SPOC responds, you will be notified. The query status will change to **Responded**.

### 3. Confirm the response
After reviewing the answer, confirm whether it resolves your question. 

### 4. Mark as resolved
If the answer addresses your question, confirm it. The query will then be marked as **Resolved**.

### 5. Reopen if necessary
If the answer does not resolve your issue, you can reopen the query. Add a new message to explain what is still unclear. The query will be reviewed and responded to again.

## Tips
- Keep track of notifications to stay informed about your query's progress.
- Use clear and specific language when submitting your query for better responses.
- If reopening a query, provide detailed information on what remains unresolved.

## Things to know
Notifications will inform you when:
- Your query receives a response.
- The query is awaiting your confirmation or response.
- The query is resolved.
- A reopened query receives further attention or a response.
