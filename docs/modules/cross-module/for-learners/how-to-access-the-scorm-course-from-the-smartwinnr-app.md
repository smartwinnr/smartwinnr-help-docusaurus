SCORM couses will be created as SmartPaths in SmartWinnr. Thus if a SmartPath with SCORM course in it is assigned to you, you can follow the below steps to access and complete the same.

End users/sales reps can access the SCORM course from their SmartWinnr account by following the below-given steps:

1\. Go to the Left menu > SmartPath.This will give you the list of Smartpaths assigned to you. Choose the SmartPath that you would like to attend now.

Alternative step: Go to th e Dashboard > My Task. This will give you the list of activities assigned to you. Choose the SmartPath that you would like to attend now.

![1\. Go to "Dashboard>> My Task", Here the sales rep will be able to see the SmartPath assigned to them.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-access-the-scorm-course-from-the-smartwinnr-app-mt0ebtim.png)

2\. Open the SmartPath, it will take you to the following screen. Click on the button with an “**Arrow**” to start the course.

![2\. Open the SmartPath, it will take you to the following screen. Click on the button with an “Arrow” to start the course.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-access-the-scorm-course-from-the-smartwinnr-app-mt0edszx.png)

3\. It will take you to the course. Click on the button “**START PLAYING**” to start with the course.

![3\. It will take you to the course. Click on the button “START PLAYING” to move to the course.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-access-the-scorm-course-from-the-smartwinnr-app-mt0efe3k.png)

4\. Click on the button “**START COURSE**”.

![4\. Click on the button “START COURSE”.It will open the course and the sales rep can take it.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-access-the-scorm-course-from-the-smartwinnr-app-mt0eh9xe.png)

5\. It will load the course and you can take it.
