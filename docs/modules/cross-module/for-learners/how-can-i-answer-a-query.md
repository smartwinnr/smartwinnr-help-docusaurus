> **At a glance** - This article outlines the steps to answer a query posted in QResolve using the SmartWinnr app.

To answer a query posted in QResolve, follow these steps to ensure your response is submitted successfully.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to respond to a query in QResolve. This is applicable in scenarios such as:
- You have relevant information to provide in response to a posted question.
- You want to assist a colleague or user who has raised a query.
- You are responsible for managing queries within your team or organization.

## Steps

### 1. Open the SmartWinnr app
Login the SmartWinnr account and navigate to the **left menu bar**. Click on **QResolve**.

![Left Menu > QResolve](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1ee8al.png)

### 2. View the list of questions
You will be directed to a page displaying a list of questions that have been posted. Review the list to find the query you wish to answer.

![Query list](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fi9hp.png)

### 3. Select a question to answer
Click on the question you want to respond to. Once the question is open, locate the pen icon at the top right corner of the page. Click on this icon to initiate your response.

![Answer post](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fnh1g.png)

### 4. Enter your answer
In the "Your Answer" text box, type your response to the query. You also have the option to attach supporting images or any other relevant attachments to enhance your answer.

### 5. Submit your answer
Once you have entered your response, click on the **Submit** button at the bottom of the page to finalize and submit your answer.

![Submit response](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fvl20.png)

Your answer will be submitted successfully for the question you addressed.
