> **At a glance** - QResolve serves as a central repository for questions your teams need to perform their jobs effectively.

QResolve is the central repository of all questions that your teams need to know to do their jobs effectively. It typically captures inquiries that arise in informal settings, such as WhatsApp groups or phone conversations. This tool can be utilized by:

* **Sales Teams** - To ask any questions that their customers are posing, including information requests, objection handling, and clarifications.
* **Services Team** - To inquire about troubleshooting, servicing, and maintenance-related questions.

The questions are answered by your teams themselves.

This offers a quick and easy way to get answers, feedback, and opinions on whatever question you post from the people in your organization.
