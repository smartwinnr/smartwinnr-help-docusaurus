> **At a glance** - This article explains how to follow and vote on queries in QResolve, including saving favorites, voting, commenting, and reopening queries.

QResolve allows you to interact with queries after they are posted. You can save important queries as favorites, vote on questions and answers, comment for clarification, and reopen your query if the response does not resolve your concern.

## When to use this
Use these features when you want to:
- Track important queries you may need later.
- Provide feedback on the usefulness of questions and answers.
- Seek clarification on answers that are unclear.
- Continue a discussion if an answer does not fully resolve your issue.

## Steps

### 1. Save a query as a favorite
Mark a query as a favorite to easily track or refer to it later. This is useful when the query contains important information you may want to revisit.

![Favourite query](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-follow-and-vote-on-queries-mtlk6y30.png)
### 2. Vote on a question or answer
You can like or dislike both questions and answers in QResolve. Use this feature to indicate whether you find a question or answer useful. Your feedback helps highlight valuable content for other users.


### 3. Comment on an answer
Add a comment to an answer when you need clarification or want to provide additional information. For example, if an answer does not fully explain how to resolve your issue, you can comment to ask for further clarification.

### 4. Reopen your query
If the answer does not resolve your concern, you can reopen your query. When doing so, add a new message explaining why the answer was insufficient or what additional information you need. This allows the query to continue through the response process.

## Tips
- Use the favorite feature for queries that are particularly relevant to your work.
- Voting can help surface the most helpful content, so participate actively.
- Don't hesitate to ask for clarification if an answer is unclear; your questions can help improve the quality of responses.
