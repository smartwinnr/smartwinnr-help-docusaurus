> **At a glance** - My Tasks gives you one place to review pending activities across SmartWinnr. You can see due-date information, open a task, and complete it from the same view.

My Tasks helps you keep track of activities that still need your attention. It brings together pending items from multiple modules so you can prioritize what to finish first.

## When to use this

Use My Tasks when you want a single view of work that is waiting on you.

- You want to see pending activities from different modules together.
- You want to check how much time is left for each task.
- You want to open an assigned activity without going to each module separately.
- You want to confirm whether you still have anything pending.

## Steps

### 1. Open My Tasks
If **My Tasks** appears on your dashboard, select it to view your pending activities.

![MyTasks in User view](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-track-your-pending-tasks-with-my-tasks-mtscvb6i.png)

### 2. Review your pending tasks
Look through the list of tasks and check the due-date information for each one. You may see time remaining such as **Ends in 5 days**, **Ends in 1 month**, or **Ends in 1 year**.

### 3. Open a task
Select the task or module you want to complete. SmartWinnr opens the selected activity.

### 4. Complete the activity
Finish the activity within the timeline shown for that task. My Tasks keeps your pending work in one place so you can return to it quickly.

## Things to know

:::note
My Tasks can include pending activities from **Quiz**, **Survey**, **SmartPath**, and **Coaching**.
:::

:::note
Tasks are organized by due date, so the most urgent items are easier to spot.
:::

:::note
If you have no pending activities, My Tasks shows an empty state.
:::
