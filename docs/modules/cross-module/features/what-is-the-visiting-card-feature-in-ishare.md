> **At a glance** - Create and share digital visiting cards to enhance your networking experience.

The **Visiting Card** feature allows you to create and share digital cards, making it easier to network and share contact information. You can customize your card with various templates and styles to suit your preferences.

## When to use this
Use the **Visiting Card** feature when you want to:
- Share your contact information digitally with others.
- Customize your visiting card to reflect your personal or professional brand.
- Utilize tags to organize your contacts effectively.

## Steps

### 1. Access the visiting card creation page
Navigate to the visiting card feature in your SmartWinnr dashboard. Click on the option to create a new visiting card.

### 2. Upload your content
You can upload files in PDF, JPG, or PNG formats. This will be the content of your visiting card.

### 3. Customize your card
On the left side, you will see all available **Custom Templates**. Drag and drop the templates you want to use into the main area.

### 4. Adjust template settings
For each template you add, you can customize the following:
- **Default Value**: Set a placeholder value for the text field.
- **Font Color**: Choose a color that suits your style.
- **Font Size**: Adjust the size of the text.
- **Font Family**: Select a font that matches your preference.

### 5. Save your template
Once you have customized your visiting card, click on the **Save** option to save your template details.

## Tips
- Experiment with different templates to find the one that best represents you.
- Use tags to categorize your contacts for easier management.
- Preview your visiting card before sharing to ensure it looks as intended.
