> **At a glance** - Create and share digital visiting cards to enhance your networking experience.

The **Visiting Card** feature allows you to create and share digital cards, making it easier to network and share contact information. You can customize your card with various templates and styles to suit your preferences.

## When to use this
Use the **Visiting Card** feature when you want to:
- Share your contact information digitally with others.
- Customize your visiting card to reflect your personal or professional brand.
- Utilize tags to organize your contacts effectively.

## Before you start
Ensure you have access to the **Visiting Card** feature in your iShare account.

## Steps

### 1. Create a Visiting Card
To create a visiting card, click on the **Create** button. A new page will display where you can upload PDF, JPG, and PNG files.

![The page to upload files for creating a visiting card](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-the-visiting-card-feature-in-ishare-ms5q0r66.png)

### 2. Add Custom Templates
On the left side, you can see all available custom templates. You can drag and drop the templates according to your preference.

![Available custom templates for visiting cards](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-the-visiting-card-feature-in-ishare-ms5q2ag9.png)

### 3. Customize Your Template
To add any of the custom templates, simply drag and drop it. After dragging and dropping, you will see the following options:

- **Default Value**: This default value will appear in the text field as the default placeholder.
- **Font Color**: Adjust the font color to suit your preferences.
- **Font Size**: Adjust the font size to suit your preferences.
- **Font Family**: Adjust the font family to suit your preference.

![Customization options for visiting card templates](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-the-visiting-card-feature-in-ishare-ms5qahyp.png)

### 4. Save Your Template
After incorporating all the custom templates, it will appear as follows. Click on the **Save** option to save the template details.

The below image shows how it appears from the user's view.

![User view of the created visiting card](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-the-visiting-card-feature-in-ishare-ms5qfpms.png)

## Tips
- Experiment with different templates to find the one that best represents you.
- Use tags to categorize your contacts for easier management.
- Preview your visiting card before sharing to ensure it looks as intended.
