> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr is a mobile-first Sales Productivity platform designed to enhance sales learning, coaching, and track performance.

SmartWinnr is the world's number 1 mobile-first Sales Productivity platform. It serves as a single platform through which you can drive:

* Sales Learning
* Sales Coaching
* Sales Contests

Different organizations use SmartWinnr for various purposes, tailoring its features to meet their specific needs. SmartWinnr is an AI-powered learning and sales enablement platform that helps organizations train, coach, engage, and measure the performance of their customer-facing teams. It brings learning, AI-powered roleplays, coaching, content, gamification, and performance tracking together in a single platform.

SmartWinnr enables teams to go beyond traditional training by combining learning with practice, coaching, engagement, and performance measurement. This allows users to learn new concepts, practice real-world customer conversations, receive feedback, participate in challenges, and track their progress.

## When to use this

SmartWinnr provides a range of modules that can be used individually or together to create a complete learning and performance journey. 

### 1. Learn and Build Knowledge
SmartWinnr provides multiple ways to deliver learning content and reinforce knowledge:

- **SmartPath** – Create structured learning journeys with courses, modules, and activities.
- **SmartFeeds** – Share bite-sized learning content and timely updates to reinforce key concepts.
- **KHub** – Provide users with a centralized repository for accessing training materials and resources.
- **Quizzes and Surveys** – Assess knowledge, reinforce learning, and collect feedback.

### 2. Practice with AI Coaching
With **AI Coaching**, users can practice real-world customer conversations through AI-powered roleplays. Users can interact with AI avatars, practice different scenarios, and receive feedback based on predefined competencies and evaluation criteria. This allows learners to practice repeatedly without having to wait for a trainer or manager to be available.

### 3. Receive Manager and Trainer Coaching
SmartWinnr also supports **Coaching** and **Field Coaching**, allowing managers and trainers to review user performance, provide feedback, and support individual development. Managers can review coaching submissions, provide scores and feedback, and use the information to identify areas where users need additional support.

### 4. Engage Through Gamification
SmartWinnr uses **Competitions**, leaderboards, achievements, and KPI-based gamification to make learning and performance activities more engaging. You can create different types of competitions based on your objectives, including learning-based competitions, performance-based competitions, bracket challenges, and league games.

### 5. Track Learning and Performance
SmartWinnr provides analytics and reports that help trainers, managers, and administrators understand:

- User participation and completion
- Learning and assessment scores
- Coaching performance
- Skill and competency development
- Performance trends
- Competition results
- Areas requiring additional training or coaching

This helps organizations use data to identify gaps and make informed decisions about their learning and development programs.

## How do the different modules work together?

SmartWinnr can be used to create an end-to-end learning and performance journey. 

For example: **Learn → Practice → Get Feedback → Reinforce → Compete → Measure**

A typical learning journey could look like this:

- **SmartPath** introduces the learner to a new product or topic.
- **SmartFeeds** reinforces important concepts through bite-sized content.
- **Quizzes** assess the learner's understanding.
- **AI Coaching** allows the learner to practice applying the knowledge in a realistic conversation.
- **Manager Coaching** provides additional feedback and guidance.
- **Competitions** and **Gamification** encourage continued participation and practice.
- **Reports** and **Analytics** help trainers and managers measure progress and identify areas for improvement.

This connected approach allows organizations to bring learning, practice, coaching, engagement, and performance measurement together rather than managing each activity separately.

## Who can use SmartWinnr?

SmartWinnr can be used by different roles within an organization, with each role having access to features relevant to their responsibilities.

- **Admins** can configure the platform, manage users and settings, create learning and coaching activities, and access organizational reports.
- **Trainers** can create and manage learning programs, coaching activities, assessments, and competitions, and monitor learner progress.
- **Managers** can coach their teams, review performance, provide feedback, and track development.
- **Users/Learners** can access assigned learning activities, complete quizzes and courses, participate in competitions, practice AI roleplays, and track their own progress.

In summary, SmartWinnr brings learning, practice, coaching, gamification, and analytics together in one platform, helping organizations create a more continuous and measurable approach to developing customer-facing teams.
