> **At a glance** - Share YouTube videos directly within the app by creating YouTube content. This allows users to view videos seamlessly.

You can create YouTube content to share video URLs, making it easy for users to access and view videos directly within the app. This feature enhances the content experience by integrating video sharing.

## When to use this
Use the YouTube content feature when you want to:
- Share educational or promotional videos with users.
- Enhance your content offerings with multimedia.
- Provide direct access to video resources.

## Before you start
Ensure you have the necessary permissions to create content in iShare.

## Steps

### 1. Access the iShare Gallery
Go to **EDITOR > LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > iShare > Gallery**, then click on the **Create** button in the left corner and select an item from the Gallery as per the screenshot given.

![Access the iShare Gallery](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-customer-engagement-content-creation-mrzxxjfp.png)

### 2. Select Content Type
For YouTube content, users can conveniently share YouTube video URLs through this content type, allowing users to view the video directly within the app.

### 3. Add Tags
If you want to assign tags to a specific group of people, you can utilize this tagging feature.

### 4. Choose Customer Profile
If you wish to choose a specific customer profile for any of our iShare content, you can do so by utilizing the Customer Profile feature.

### 5. Enter Title
Input the title of the content in the designated field.

### 6. Write Description
Provide a description of that iShare content in the appropriate field.

![Content Creation Page](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-how-to-create-youtube-content-in-ishare-mrzykn3q.png)

### 7. Upload Video
By clicking on the YouTube icon, you will see the following page. To upload a video, paste the video's URL into the **Video URL** field.

![Video Upload Page](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-how-to-create-youtube-content-in-ishare-mrzym259.png)

### 8. Submit Content
After entering the video URL, click the **Submit** button to finalize the creation of the content.

![Submit Content](/img/helpscout/authored/understanding-how-to-create-youtube-content-in-ishare-mrzyn5dp.png)
