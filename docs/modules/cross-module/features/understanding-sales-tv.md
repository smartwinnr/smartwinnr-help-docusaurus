> **At a glance** - Sales TV shows rotating content on a shared screen. Use it to keep teams informed, recognize performance, and display contest progress.

Sales TV is a rotating slideshow for shared screens. You can combine different slide types in one presentation, so important information stays visible to your team throughout the day.

Use Sales TV in sales floors, common areas, or any shared space where people can see updates without opening SmartWinnr on their own devices.

## When to use this

Use Sales TV when you want to keep team information visible in a shared space.

- Show competition results on a wallboard.
- Recognize team members in front of the group.
- Display widgets, videos, or external content.
- Keep contest progress visible during the day.

## What Sales TV can show

Sales TV supports multiple slide types in the same presentation.

A **Leaderboard** slide shows rankings or competition performance. Use it to make progress visible and encourage healthy competition.

A **Shout-out** slide highlights team members and celebrates achievements. Use it to recognize strong performance or individual contributions.

A **Widget** slide displays selected information in a visual format. Use it when you want important data easy to see at a glance.

You can also display videos and external content as part of the same rotating experience.

## How Sales TV works

A Sales TV can include several slides. When you display it on a screen, the slides rotate automatically.

For example, you might show a **Leaderboard**, then a **Shout-out**, then a video, then a **Widget**, and repeat the cycle.

This keeps the screen active and gives your team a steady stream of performance, recognition, and other useful content.
