> **At a glance** - Learn how you can implement the 30/60/90 Learning Journey for your Induction program using the different modules in SmartWinnr.

### What is a 30/60/90 Learning Journey?

A 30/60/90 Learning Journey is a structured learning and enablement framework that guides learners through a progressive development plan over three phases—the first 30 days, the next 30 days (Day 31–60), and the final 30 days (Day 61–90). Instead of overwhelming learners with all the training at once, the journey delivers the right content, activities, coaching, and assessments at the right time, helping learners build knowledge, apply skills, and continuously improve.

Using SmartWinnr, organizations can create a comprehensive 30/60/90 learning journey by combining learning, coaching, knowledge reinforcement, assessments, gamification, and analytics into a single platform.

### Why Implement a 30/60/90 Learning Journey?

A structured learning journey helps organizations:

- Accelerate onboarding for new hires.
- Deliver continuous learning beyond classroom training.
- Reinforce key concepts over time.
- Improve knowledge retention.
- Build practical selling and communication skills.
- Measure learner progress and engagement.
- Increase adoption through gamification and recognition.
- SmartWinnr's 360° Learning Framework

SmartWinnr supports every stage of the learning lifecycle through its integrated learning ecosystem.

Based on the 360° Learning Framework, SmartWinnr combines:

- SmartPaths for structured onboarding and training
- K-Hub for on-demand knowledge access
- SmartFeeds for micro-learning and reinforcement
- Gamified Quizzes for knowledge validation

- Field Coaching for manager-led coaching
- Analytics to measure learner performance and engagement

Together, these modules create a continuous learning experience rather than isolated training events.

### **Implementing a 30/60/90 Learning Journey in SmartWinnr**

### Phase 1: First 30 Days – Learn & Onboard

The objective of the first 30 days is to build a strong knowledge foundation.

Recommended SmartWinnr Modules:

- **SmartPaths**:  Create a structured onboarding journey consisting of:
  - Welcome videos
  - Product training
  - Compliance modules
  - Company policies
  - Sales methodology
  - Certification checkpoints

SmartPaths ensure learners complete content in the required sequence.

- **K-Hub** : Provide learners with a centralized knowledge repository containing:

  - Product brochures
  - Sales decks
  - Competitive information
  - SOPs and Training videos
  - FAQs

This enables learners to quickly find information whenever needed.

- **SmartFeeds**: Send periodic micro-learning content such as:
  - Daily product tips
  - Sales best practices
  - Short videos
  - Important announcements
  - Success stories

SmartFeeds keep learners engaged without overwhelming them.

**Surveys**

Collect learner expectations before training using:

- Pre-training surveys
- Skill assessments
- Readiness questionnaires

This helps identify knowledge gaps before the learning journey begins.

**Quizzes**

Validate learning after each module through:

- Topic-based quizzes
- Certification quizzes
- Knowledge checks

Gamification encourages healthy competition while tracking learner progress.

### Phase 2: Day 31–60 – Practice & Apply

Once foundational knowledge is established, learners begin applying what they have learned.

Recommended SmartWinnr Modules:

- **Video Coaching**: Assess learners through recorded role plays such as:
  - Product detailing
  - Customer conversations
  - Objection handling
  - Sales presentations

Managers can review submissions and provide personalized feedback.

- **AI Role Plays**: Allow learners to practice conversations with AI in a realistic environment. Example scenarios include:

  - Customer discovery
  - Objection handling
  - Product positioning
  - Closing conversations

AI provides immediate feedback, enabling learners to improve continuously.

- **Situational Quizzes**: Create scenario-based quizzes that test application rather than memorization. Examples include:

  - What would you recommend to this customer?
  - Which objection handling technique is most appropriate?
  - What should be your next sales action?
  
- **Field Coaching**: Managers can reinforce learning through field observations and coaching discussions. This helps bridge the gap between digital learning and real-world execution.

### Phase 3: Day 61–90 – Reinforce & Improve

The final phase focuses on long-term knowledge retention, continuous reinforcement, and performance improvement.

Recommended SmartWinnr Modules:

- **SmartFeeds**: Continue reinforcing learning by sending:

  - Weekly key takeaways
  - Best practices
  - Product updates
  - Customer success stories
  - Competitive intelligence

Micro-learning helps prevent knowledge decay.

- **Gamified Quizzes**: Schedule periodic quizzes to reinforce concepts learned during the journey. Organizations can use:

  - Weekly quizzes
  - Monthly challenges
  - Leaderboards
  - Competitions

This keeps learners engaged while encouraging continuous improvement.

- **Certifications** : Award digital certifications upon successful completion of the learning journey. Certifications can serve as milestones that motivate learners and validate their progress.

- **Post-Training Surveys** : Collect learner feedback to evaluate:

  - Learning effectiveness
  - Content quality
  - Confidence levels
  - Improvement opportunities

These insights help optimize future learning journeys.
