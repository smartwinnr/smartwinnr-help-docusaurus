> **At a glance** - Follow these steps to answer queries in SmartWinnr efficiently.

You can respond to queries in SmartWinnr by accessing the **QResolve** feature. This allows you to view and answer questions posted by users, ensuring effective communication and support.

## When to use this
Use this feature when you need to:
- Respond to questions from team members or users.
- Forward queries to the appropriate point of contact (SPOC).
- Update the status of resolved or ongoing queries.

## Steps

### 1. Open the SmartWinnr app
Log in to your SmartWinnr account and navigate to the left menu bar. Click on **QResolve**.

![Left Menu > QResolve](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1ee8al.png)

### 2. View the list of questions
You will be directed to a page displaying a list of questions that have been posted. Review the list to find the query you wish to answer.

![Query list](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fi9hp.png)


### 3. Select a question to answer
Click on the question you want to respond to. Once the question is open, locate the **pen icon** at the top right corner of the page. Click on this icon to initiate your response.

![Answer post](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fnh1g.png)


### 4. Enter your answer
In the **Your Answer** text box, type your response to the query. You also have the option to attach supporting images or any other relevant attachments to enhance your answer.



### 5. Submit your answer
Once you have entered your response, click on the **Submit** button at the bottom of the page to finalize and submit your answer. Your answer will be submitted successfully for the question you addressed.

![Submit response](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-answer-a-query-mt1fvl20.png)


### 6. Forward the query if necessary
If the query is not related to you, click on the **Forward** option in the top right corner. Search for a SPOC user or tag you want to forward to, then click on **Forward**.

### 7. Update query status
For answered queries, the status will fall under **Responded**. Once the user receives answers, they can update the status to **Resolved** from their end.

### 8. Reopen resolved queries if needed
If a query has been resolved by a manager but the issue persists, the user can reopen the query with a new message.

## Tips
- Always review the question thoroughly before responding.
- Use clear and concise language in your answers.
- Attach relevant documents or images to support your response.
