> **At a glance** - Create and customize iShare content types to engage customers with personalized materials.

iShare content types allow you to create a variety of materials, such as greeting cards, visiting cards, and shareable links. You can use custom templates to tailor your content to specific audiences.

## When to use this
Use iShare content types when you need to:
- Create personalized greetings for customers.
- Share engaging content with specific groups.
- Utilize custom templates for various marketing materials.

## Steps

### 1. Access the iShare Content Creation
Navigate to the iShare content creation section in your dashboard. Go to **LEARNING AND KNOWLEDGE > iShare > Gallery**. The following page will open. Click on the **Create** button on the left side and select **Item**.

### 2. Fill in Mandatory Fields
Enter the required information, including:
- **Title**: The title of your content.
- **Description**: A brief description of the iShare content.
- **Tags**: Assign tags to categorize the content for specific groups.
- **Customer Profile**: Select a specific customer profile for your content.

![Fill in mandatory fields]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryobpp0.png)

After filling in all details, click on the **Create** button.

### 3. Upload Files
Upload relevant files in PDF, JPG, or PNG formats.

![Upload relevant files]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryogrxi.png)

### 4. Customize Templates
On the left side, you can find custom templates. Drag and drop them into your workspace to arrange them as desired.

![Customize templates]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryoi59m.png)

### 5. Set Default Values
Define the default value that will appear in the text field as a placeholder. Adjust the following settings as needed:
- **Font Color**: Choose a font color that aligns with your branding.
- **Font Size**: Set the font size for readability.
- **Font Family**: Select a font family that fits your design.

![Set default values]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryoj5ko.png)

### 6. Save Your Content
Click the **Save** option to store your customized iShare content. This is how it appears in the user's view.

![User view of saved content]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryrunod.png)

### 7. Share Your Content
By clicking on the **Share** button, you will see the following page. This Customize Template will help you to customize the template by adding the customer's name to whom you would like to send this greeting card.

![Share your content]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryrvhrc.png)

Then click the **Preview** button to preview the final template. After previewing, you can share it with other users through various apps by clicking on the **Share** option.

![Preview and share]( /img/helpscout/authored/understanding-ishare-content-types-creation-and-customization-mryrx30m.png)

## Tips
- Utilize the tagging feature to effectively group your content for targeted audiences.
- Experiment with different templates to find the most engaging designs.
- Regularly update your content to keep it relevant and appealing to your audience.
