> **At a glance** - iShare content enables users to create and share customized templates for various purposes, enhancing engagement and networking.

iShare is a versatile content-sharing system designed to facilitate the creation and distribution of customized templates for various purposes. It includes several content types, such as iShare, customer engagement content, YouTube links, visiting cards, and iShare links, allowing users to generate a wide range of materials.

## When to use this
Use iShare when you need to create and share customized content for different contexts. It is particularly useful in the following scenarios:

- Creating personalized greetings or birthday cards.
- Presenting policies and terms to stakeholders through engaging visuals.
- Sharing YouTube videos directly within the app for easy access.
- Distributing digital visiting cards for networking purposes.
- Inserting external links for users to access additional web content.

## Steps

### 1. Create iShare content
Select the type of content you want to create, such as a greeting card or a customer engagement image. Use the available templates to customize your content.

### 2. Generate customer engagement content
Create images with descriptions to effectively present policies or terms and conditions. This content can be shared with stakeholders for better understanding.

### 3. Share YouTube links
Use the YouTube content type to share video URLs. Users can view the videos directly within the app, enhancing their experience.

### 4. Create digital visiting cards
Design and share digital visiting cards to facilitate networking and easy contact sharing. This feature simplifies the process of exchanging information.

### 5. Insert iShare links
Add external links to your content, allowing users to access additional resources or information easily.

## Tips
- Explore various templates available in iShare to maximize your content's impact.
- Use engaging visuals in customer engagement content to capture attention effectively.
- Ensure that external links are relevant and lead to valuable resources for your audience.
