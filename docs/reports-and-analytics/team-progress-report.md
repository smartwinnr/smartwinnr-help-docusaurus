> **At a glance** - Download the team progress report as a CSV file to analyze performance metrics for your team.

You can download the team progress report as a CSV file from SmartWinnr. Follow the steps below to download the Team Progress Reports.

## When to use this
Use the Team Progress Report to track and analyze the performance of your team members. This report can help you identify areas for improvement and recognize high achievers.

## Steps

### 1. Navigate to the Team Progress Report
Go to **EDITOR > Reports > Team Progress Report**. Then, click on the **Download Report** button to download the reports.

![Team Progress Report](/img/helpscout/authored/team-progress-report-mt1u118n.png)

### 2. Use Filters to Search for Participants
You can search for a particular participant by using the filters: **Name**, **Email**, **Date Range**, **Country**, **Manager**, **Meta Tag Type**, and **Meta Tags**.

![Search by different values](/img/helpscout/authored/team-progress-report-mt1u2gg1.png)

### 3. View Detailed Analytics for Participants
To view the analytics report of a particular participant, click on the **Analytics** icon next to that participant.

![Check reports of particular user by clicking on analytics button against that user](/img/helpscout/authored/team-progress-report-mt1u4kaz.png)

This takes you to a page where a detailed performance report of the participant is shown. Below is an example report of a participant, which includes:

- The number of quizzes completed by the participant.
- The number of quizzes that are pending.
- The overall net score and percentage score of the participant.
- A bar graph that shows the participant's performance by category. You can filter this report by category and by date range.
- The performance of the participant in each quiz. You can filter these results by date range.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/team-progress-report-mt1u71w0.png)
