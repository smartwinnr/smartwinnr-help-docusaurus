> **At a glance** - This article explains how to find your downloaded reports in SmartWinnr.

Reports are typically downloaded directly to your **Downloads** folder. For larger reports, they will be saved in the **Generated Reports** section under **Reports**. A notification will appear, stating "Reports being generated. Please check under Generated Reports."

## When to use this

Use this guide when you need to locate your downloaded reports, especially if they are large files that may not appear in your usual download location.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Generated Reports Section

Navigate to **Editor → REPORTS → Generated Reports**. This section contains all reports that are larger in size.

![Generated Report Section](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms7tou5c.png)

### 2. Download the Required Report

In the **Generated Reports** section, find the report you need. Click on the down arrow next to the report to download it to your file manager.

![Downloaded Reports](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-ms7tpent.png)
