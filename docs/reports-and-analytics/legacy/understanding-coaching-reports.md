> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr offers four distinct types of coaching reports to help you track user performance effectively.

SmartWinnr provides several coaching reports designed to give you insights into learner performance, skill development, and training effectiveness. Each report serves a specific purpose, allowing you to make informed coaching decisions.

## When to use this

Use these reports to monitor user performance and identify areas for improvement. Consider these scenarios:

- Analyzing specific objectives to see how learners perform.
- Reviewing individual learner analytics to understand their progress.
- Gaining an overall view of performance across teams or business units.
- Generating detailed data based on selected KPIs for deeper insights.

## Steps

### 1. Review Attempt-wise User Objective Report

This report provides performance data for each attempt, allowing you to analyze how users perform on specific objectives.

### 2. Access Learner Report - By Learner

This individual report offers analytics for a specific learner. You can filter by Business Unit, Country, and Group/Team to drill down into performance.

### 3. Check Learner Overall Report

This report presents an overall view of learner performance across a selected Business Unit, Country, or specific team. It shows total counts, average scores, and statistics for quizzes.

### 4. Generate KPI Report

This extensive report allows you to generate various slices of data based on selected KPIs, participant filters, and measurement frequency.

## Tips

- Use filters in the **Learner Report - By Learner** to customize your view based on specific criteria.
- Regularly review the **KPI Report** to track progress and adjust coaching strategies accordingly.
- Download reports periodically to maintain a comprehensive record of user performance.

## Things to know

:::caution
Downloading reports may require specific permissions. Ensure you have the necessary access rights.
:::
