> **At a glance** - The ‘Team Analytics’ section provides quizmasters with insights into the performance of all quizzes, including overall performance, category-wise performance, and leaderboards.

The results of the performance of all quizzes are available to all the quizmasters in the ‘Team Analytics’ section. You can view team analytics by navigating to **EDITOR > Questions and Quizzes > Reports > Team Analytics**.

## When to use this
Use the ‘Team Analytics’ section to:
- Analyze overall quiz performance for your team.
- Review performance by category and country.
- Access leaderboards to compare participant performance.
- Filter results based on specific criteria.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Team Analytics Section
Go to **EDITOR > Questions and Quizzes > Reports > Team Analytics** to view the analytics dashboard.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/team-analytics-mt1tmj2b.png)

### 2. Review Overall Performance
The overall performance, performance by country, and leaderboard reflect the data for your entire division, depending on your access.

### 3. Analyze Category-wise Performance
In this section, you will see a heat map that shows performance by category and by country.

![Category-wise Performance for all Countries: This section gives you a heat map which shows the performance by category and by country.](/img/helpscout/editors/team-analytics-2.png)

### 4. Use Filters for Detailed Analysis
SmartWinnr is flexible and supports slicing and dicing of information. You can click on the **Filter** button to filter the results by Country, Category, User Tag, and Email. Clicking the **Filter** button opens the following popup.

![SmartWinnr is flexible and supports slicing and dicing of information. You can click on the 'Filter' button to filter the results by Country](/img/helpscout/editors/team-analytics-3.png)

### 5. View the Leaderboard
In this section, you will be able to view a quarterly leaderboard of performance. You can see a participant's performance over a period of time by clicking on the **View Analytics** button. You can search for a particular user by using the **Country** filter or by entering your search in the **Search** box.

![Leaderboard: In this section, you will be able to view a quarterly leaderboard of performance. You will be able to see a participant's performance over a period of time by clicking on the 'View Analytics' button.](/img/helpscout/editors/team-analytics-4.png)

### 6. Check the Team Knowledge Profile
This section provides the total score of all the team members. You can filter these performance results of the participants by Category, Tags, and Date Range.

![Team Knowledge Profile: This section gives you the total score of all the team members. You can filter these performance results of the participants by Category, Tags, and Date Range.](/img/helpscout/editors/team-analytics-5.png)

## Tips
- Regularly check the leaderboard to motivate team members.
- Use filters to gain insights into specific areas of performance.
- Encourage team members to improve scores based on analytics feedback.

## Things to know
- The available data may vary based on your access level.
- Ensure you have the necessary permissions to view detailed reports.
