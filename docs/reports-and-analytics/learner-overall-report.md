> **At a glance** - The Learner Overall Report provides insights into learner performance across quizzes, SmartPaths, surveys, and coaching sessions.

An Editor who has access to the Learner Report can view the Report Analytics, which helps track learner progress and performance.

## When to use this
Use the Learner Overall Report to:
- Analyze quiz performance, including total counts and average scores.
- Track SmartPath completion and milestones.
- Review survey participation and coaching effectiveness.
- Monitor resource usage and popular content.

## Before you start
Ensure you have the necessary permissions to access the Learner Report.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Learner Report
Navigate to **Editor → Reports → Learner Report → Overall**.

![GO TO EDITOR>>REPORTS>>LEARNER REPORT>>OVERALL.](/img/helpscout/editors/learner-overall-report-1.jpg)

### 2. Select Business Unit and Filters
Select the Business Unit you want to check in the report. You can also filter by Country, specific teams, or groups. Adjust the Date Range as needed.

![Select the Business Unit that you want to check the report. You can also select from Country and you can also check for a specific team or g](/img/helpscout/editors/learner-overall-report-2.jpg)

### 3. Review Quiz Analytics
View analytics for quizzes, including total count, number of questions, average score, and additional statistics.

### 4. Analyze SmartPaths
For SmartPaths, track the total number, average score, milestones covered, certificates issued, and related statistics.

![Similarly for SmartPaths, we can track total SmartPaths, average score, milestone covered, certificates issued, and statistics about the Sma](/img/helpscout/editors/learner-overall-report-3.jpg)

### 5. Check Survey and Coaching Reports
Scroll down to see the report for Surveys and Survey Templates, including stats on completion, in progress, and not started.

For Coachings, view the total count of coaching sessions, participant count, and completion percentage. You can also track participant skills based on competencies.

![For Coachings, you get the total count of coaching, participants count, and completion percentage. Also, you get the data about the particip](/img/helpscout/editors/learner-overall-report-4.jpg)

### 6. Examine Knowledge Gained
In the Knowledge Gained section, view reports based on selected categories, which consist of KPIs to help track participant progress. You can also change the Frequency to view data over different time frames.

### 7. Review Resource Counts
Under the Resources section, see the count of all videos, images, documents, and SCORM Courses added to SmartWinnr.

![Under the Resources section, you will get the count of all the videos, images, documents, and SCORM Courses that have been added to SmartWin](/img/helpscout/editors/learner-overall-report-5.jpg)

### 8. Analyze Popular SmartPaths and Smartfeeds
In the Most Popular SmartPath section, view analytics for the SmartPaths that have been accessed the most.

![Least viewed and most viewed smartfeeds](/img/helpscout/authored/learner-overall-report-mt2un3u2.png)

In the Most Viewed Smartfeed section, see the most viewed smartfeeds by users. In the Least Viewed Smartfeed section, view the least accessed smartfeeds by users.
