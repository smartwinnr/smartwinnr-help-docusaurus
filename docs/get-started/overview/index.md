> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr is a revenue-enablement platform that combines learning, assessment, coaching, and gamification. This overview helps you understand its modules and where to find relevant documentation.

SmartWinnr is a revenue-enablement platform that integrates learning, assessment, coaching, and gamification in one place. Use this overview to determine which modules your organization has and access the documentation that matches your needs.

## What you can do with SmartWinnr

- **Learn fast** - Access bite-sized SmartFeeds, structured SmartPaths, and knowledge-hub content delivered to learners on both web and mobile platforms.
- **Assess knowledge** - Create quizzes, manage question pools, and review per-learner and per-team analytics.
- **Coach in 1:1** - Review video-coaching submissions, conduct AI role-plays, or capture field-coaching observations against templated competencies.
- **Gamify outcomes** - Use competitions, scorecards, leaderboards, badges, XP, and streaks to motivate learners.
- **Collect feedback** - Utilize surveys and forms with reviewer workflows to gather insights.

## How this documentation is laid out

Every module follows the same structure under [**Modules**](/modules/):

| Sub-folder | Who it's for |
|---|---|
| **For Learners** | What end users do day-to-day |
| **For Managers** | Reviewing reportees and reading team analytics |
| **Create & Manage** | Authoring CRUD for editors and admins |
| **Assign & Schedule** | Distribution to users, groups, and metatags |
| **Features** | Per-feature reference |
| **Reports & Analytics** | Admin-level reports and dashboards |
| **Settings & Permissions** | Module configuration |
| **FAQs & Troubleshooting** | Common questions and error codes |

You will only see the sub-folders that your role and your organization’s privileges allow. A learner browsing the Quiz module sees **For Learners** and **FAQs**; an editor sees the full structure.

## Which modules does your org have?

Module availability is set per organization. If you open a module page and see a "Talk to your admin to enable" upsell instead of the get-started cards, your organization has not licensed that module yet. The documentation is still accessible, but the feature is not live for you.
