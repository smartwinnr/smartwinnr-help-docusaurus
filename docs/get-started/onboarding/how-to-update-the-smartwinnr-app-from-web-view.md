> **At a glance** - Follow these steps to update the SmartWinnr app through the web view interface.

Login to the SmartWinnr web view and click on the **profile picture** at the top. This action will take you to the settings screen where you can check for updates.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to update the SmartWinnr app from the web view. This is particularly useful for ensuring you have the latest features and improvements.

## Steps

### 1. Click on your profile picture
Login to the SmartWinnr web view and click on the **profile picture** at the top. It will take you to the following screen. Click on the **Settings** icon at the top right corner, which is highlighted in red.

![Login to the SmartWinnr web view and click on the profile picture at the top. It will take you to the following screen. Click on Settings icon](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-update-the-smartwinnr-app-from-web-view-1.png)

### 2. Check the version
You will be directed to the settings screen, where you can find the version of the SmartWinnr web view currently in your system.

![You will be directed to the settings screen, where you can find the version of the SmartWinnr web view currently in your system](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-update-the-smartwinnr-app-from-web-view-2.png)

### 3. Review additional information
Here, you can also find additional information related to the app version and any available updates.

![You can also find additional information related to the app version and any available updates](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/how-to-update-the-smartwinnr-app-from-web-view-3.png)
