> **At a glance** - Change your language in the SmartWinnr app by accessing your profile settings and selecting your preferred language.

If multiple languages are enabled for your organization in SmartWinnr, you can change your language from English to any other languages available in SmartWinnr. Follow these steps to change the language for your SmartWinnr app.

## When to use this

Use this feature when you want to switch the app's language to better suit your preferences or needs.

## Steps

### 1. Open the SmartWinnr app

Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the **hamburger icon** at the top left corner of the screen. This will display a left menu bar as shown in the screenshot below:

![Click on the profile](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-language-in-smartwinnr-app-ms75fw3g.jpg)

### 2. Access your profile

Click on your **profile picture** at the top. This will take you to the following screen:

![Click on the Settings](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-language-in-smartwinnr-app-ms75h4te.jpg)

### 3. Open Settings

Click on the **Settings** icon at the top right corner highlighted in red, then go to the option **Change Language**.

![Click on Change Language](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-language-in-smartwinnr-app-ms75iccv.jpg)

### 4. Select your preferred language

You will be taken to a screen where you can select the language from the list of languages available for your organization. After selecting your preferred language, go back by clicking the **\<-** option, and the selected language will be applied to your SmartWinnr account.

![Select the language you want to change to](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-language-in-smartwinnr-app-ms75khwo.png)
