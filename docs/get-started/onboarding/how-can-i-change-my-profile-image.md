## Changing your profile picture after you have logged in

Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the **hamburger** icon at the top left corner of the screen. It will show a left menu bar as shown in the screenshot below:

![Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the hamburger icon at the top left corner of the screen. It will show a left menu bar as shown in the s](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msniylpg.png)

Here, click on the **profile picture** at the top. It will take you to the following screen:

![Here, click on the profile picture at the top. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnizd6m.png)

On this screen, click on the **camera** icon on the profile picture. It will take you to the following screen:

![On this screen, click on the camera icon on the profile picture. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj001t.png)

From here, you can click on the " **Open Camera**" to click a picture and upload it. Or you can choose "**Open Gallery**" option to select a picture from your gallery and upload it.

### Adding the profile picture when you first log in

You will be asked to add a profile picture the very first time you log in to SmartWinnr.

Once you download the app and enter your login credentials to log in, you will see this screen:

![Once you download the app and enter your login credentials to log in, you will see this screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj78hr.png)


Now, click on the **Camera icon** that is marked in the screenshot above. It will take you to the following screen:

![Now, click on the Camera icon. It will give you a pop-up window.

In this pop-up, you can click on the " **Open Camera**" option to click a picture and upload it. Or you can choose "**Open Gallery**" option to select a picture from your gallery and upload it.
