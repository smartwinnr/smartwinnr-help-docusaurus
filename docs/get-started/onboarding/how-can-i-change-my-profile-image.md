> **At a glance** - Learn how to change your profile image in the SmartWinnr app, whether you're updating it after logging in or setting it for the first time.

Changing your profile image in the SmartWinnr app is a straightforward process. You can do this after logging in or when you first set up your account. Follow the steps below to update your profile picture easily.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you want to:
- Change your profile image after logging into the SmartWinnr app.
- Set your profile image for the first time during the initial login.

**Follow this steps when you are updating the profile image after logging into the SmartWinnr app:**

### 1. Open the left menu bar
Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the **hamburger** icon at the top left corner of the screen. This action will display the left menu bar.

![Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the hamburger icon at the top left corner of the screen. It will show a left menu bar as shown in the screenshot below.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msniylpg.png)

### 2. Access your profile
Click on the **profile picture** at the top of the left menu bar. This will take you to your profile settings screen.

![Click on the profile picture at the top. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnizd6m.png)

### 3. Change your profile picture
On the profile settings screen, click on the **camera** icon located on your profile picture. This will direct you to the options for changing your image.

![On this screen, click on the camera icon on the profile picture. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj001t.png)

From here, you can choose to either:
- Click on **Open Camera** to take a new picture and upload it.
- Select **Open Gallery** to choose an existing picture from your gallery and upload it.

### 4. Adding a profile picture during initial login
You will be prompted to add a profile picture the very first time you log in to SmartWinnr. 

After downloading the app and entering your login credentials, you will see a screen prompting you to add a profile picture.

![Once you download the app and enter your login credentials to log in, you will see this screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj78hr.png)

Now, click on the **Camera icon** marked in the screenshot above. This will open a pop-up window.

In this pop-up, you can:
- Click on **Open Camera** to take a new picture and upload it.
- Choose **Open Gallery** to select a picture from your gallery and upload it.

![In this pop-up, you can click on the "Open Camera" option to click a picture and upload it. Or you can choose "Open Gallery" option to select a picture from your gallery and upload it.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj78hr.png)
