> **At a glance** - Follow these steps to easily check the version of your SmartWinnr app or web view.

To check the version of your SmartWinnr app, you will navigate through the hamburger menu to access your profile settings. The app version information is located within these settings.

## When to use this
Use this guide when you need to:
- Verify the version of your SmartWinnr app for troubleshooting.
- Ensure your app is updated to the latest version.
- Check compatibility with specific features or updates.

## Steps

### 1. Open the SmartWinnr app
Login to the SmartWinnr account. Click on the **hamburger** icon located at the top left corner of the screen. This action will display the left menu bar.

![Open the SmartWinnr app and click on the hamburger icon at the top left corner of the screen. It will show a left menu bar as shown in the screenshot below.](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msniylpg.png)

### 2. Access your profile settings
Click on your **profile picture** at the top of the left menu bar. This will take you to your profile settings screen.

![Click on the profile picture at the top. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnizd6m.png)

### 3. Navigate to settings
On the profile settings screen, click on the **settings** icon located at the top right corner. This will direct you to the settings page in SmartWinnr.

![On this screen, click on the settings icon on the profile picture. It will take you to the following screen:](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-change-my-profile-image-msnj001t.png)

### 4. Find the app version
On the settings page, locate the option labeled **App version**. Here, you will find the version of the SmartWinnr app installed on your system.

![Go to the option App version. It will take you to the following screen where you can find the version of the SmartWinnr app installed in you](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-check-the-app-version-web-view-version-msoc86ft.png)
