> **At a glance** - This article explains how to log in to SmartWinnr using your organization's SAML-based Single Sign-On (SSO) credentials.

Accessing SmartWinnr through SSO allows you to use your organization-provided credentials without needing a separate SmartWinnr password. Follow these steps to log in seamlessly.

## When to use this
Use this process when your organization implements SAML-based SSO. This is common for organizations that prioritize security and ease of access.

- You are an employee at an organization that uses SAML for authentication.
- You prefer to access SmartWinnr without managing multiple passwords.
- You encounter issues with traditional login methods.

## Steps

### 1. Open SmartWinnr
Download and open the SmartWinnr app on your iOS or Android device. Alternatively, access SmartWinnr through the PWA using your browser.

### 2. Enter your SmartWinnr username
On the SmartWinnr login screen, enter your official work email address in the **Username** field. Click **Login**.

### 3. Go to your organization's SSO login page
SmartWinnr will redirect you to your organization's SSO login page if your organization uses SAML (SSO). For instance, if your organization uses Microsoft as its identity provider, you will be redirected to the Microsoft login page.

### 4. Enter your organization credentials
Input your organization-provided SSO credentials. If required, complete any additional authentication steps, such as multi-factor authentication (MFA).

### 5. Complete authentication
Your organization's identity provider will verify your credentials. You do not need to enter a separate SmartWinnr password when using SSO.

### 6. Access SmartWinnr
After successful authentication, you will be redirected to SmartWinnr. You will arrive at the SmartWinnr home screen, where you can access the dashboard and other features available to you.

## Tips
- Ensure you have the latest version of the SmartWinnr app installed for the best experience.
- If you encounter issues, check with your organization's IT/SSO administrator for assistance.
- Keep your organization credentials secure and do not share them.

## Things to know
:::warning
If you are unable to log in or encounter an error on the SSO login page, contact your organization's IT/SSO administrator or the SmartWinnr Support Team for assistance.
:::
