> **At a glance** - Use your app lock PIN to sign in to SmartWinnr. If you forget it, you can reset it and create a new one.

Use your SmartWinnr username and password to sign in first. Then enter your PIN to access your account. If you forget your PIN, you can reset it and create a new one. You can also change your PIN after you sign in.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to sign in to SmartWinnr with your app lock PIN or update it later.

- You are signing in for the first time with your account credentials.
- You forgot your PIN and need to create a new one.
- You want to change your existing PIN after logging in.

## Steps

### 1. Sign in with your account
Enter your username and password to log in to SmartWinnr.

![Add username password to login](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwtprye.png)

### 2. Enter your PIN
Type your PIN to complete sign-in.

![Type the pin to login](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwtro6p.png)

### 3. Reset a forgotten PIN
If you see **Incorrect PIN, try again**, select **FORGOT PIN?** to start a reset.

![Forgot Pin reset link](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwtvsoc.png)

### 4. Create a new PIN
Enter your employee code and date of joining, then add your new PIN and select **RESET PIN**.

![Reset the pin](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwtxkf9.png)

### 5. Sign in with the new PIN
Use your new PIN to log in to your account.

![login with the new pin](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwu0fnr.png)

### 6. Open your settings
After you sign in, open your profile and go to **Settings**.

![Settings](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwu4ae6.png)

### 7. Select Change PIN
In **Settings**, select **Change PIN**.

![Change PIN](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwu5gpm.png)

### 8. Update your PIN
Enter your old PIN and your new PIN, then select **CHANGE PIN**.

![Change the pin](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mtwu7wj8.png)

## Things to know

:::note
After you change your PIN, you see a confirmation message at the bottom of the page.
:::
