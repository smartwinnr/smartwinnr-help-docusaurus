> **At a glance** - This article explains how to find your username and password for SmartWinnr after account creation.

Once your account gets created in SmartWinnr, you will receive a welcome email or SMS to your registered email address or mobile number.

If your organization registered your account with your official email address, you will receive the welcome email there. If registered with your mobile number, you will receive the welcome SMS to that number.

## When to use this
Use this information when you need to access your SmartWinnr account for the first time or if you have lost your login credentials.

- You are a new user and need to activate your account.
- You have lost the welcome email or SMS containing your login credentials.
- You need to reset your password for account access.

## Activate your Account with the Activation Link

If the welcome email contains the activation link, it looks like this:

![If the welcome email contains the activation link, it looks like this:](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/what-is-my-username-and-password-1.png)

Click on the button **Activate Account**. It will open the following page in your browser (use Chrome or Firefox).

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-my-username-and-password-mszsxh4b.png)

Then click on the link **Click here to reset your Password**. This will send a link to your email. Click on the **Reset Password** button.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-my-username-and-password-mszsztou.png)

You will be redirected to this page where you can reset your password.

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-my-username-and-password-mszt1rbm.png)

Enter your new password and click on **Set Password**. Remember this password - you will need it to log in to the app later on. Your username is your company email or mobile number to which you received the welcome email or SMS.

### Activate your Account with the System Generated Password

If the welcome email has the system-generated password, it looks like this, with your username and password provided in the email:

![If the welcome email has the system generated password, it looks like this with your username and password provided in the email:](/img/helpscout/managers-and-team-members/what-is-my-username-and-password-3.png)

If the welcome SMS has the system-generated password, it looks like this, with your username and password provided in the SMS:

![describe this screenshot](/img/helpscout/authored/what-is-my-username-and-password-mszy9e86.jpg)

You should use the username and password provided in the welcome email or SMS as your login credentials to access your account in SmartWinnr.

You can use the same username and password to log in to your account from both the SmartWinnr Mobile App and the SmartWinnr web view (web.smartwinnr.com).

## What if I lost the email/SMS with the login credentials or activation link?

If you have lost the email or SMS with the login credentials or activation link, you can either write to your system administrator or contact [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com) to have the activation email resent to you.
