> **At a glance** - Use a one-time password (OTP) to log in to SmartWinnr securely without a permanent password.

You can log in to SmartWinnr using a one-time password (OTP) when OTP-based authentication is enabled for your organization. The OTP verifies your identity without requiring a permanent SmartWinnr password.

## When to use this
Use this method when:
- You prefer not to use a permanent password.
- Your organization has enabled OTP-based authentication.
- You need to access SmartWinnr securely from your mobile device or browser.

## Steps

### 1. Open SmartWinnr
Open the SmartWinnr app on your iOS or Android device, or access SmartWinnr through the PWA using your browser.

### 2. Enter your phone number
On the SmartWinnr login screen, enter your registered phone number in the **Username** field. Then, click **Login**.

### 3. Enter the OTP
An OTP will be sent to your registered phone number. Enter the OTP in the verification field.

### 4. Verify the OTP
Click **Submit** or **Verify** to authenticate your account. If the OTP is valid, you will be successfully logged in.

### 5. Access SmartWinnr
After successful authentication, you will be directed to the SmartWinnr home screen, where you can access your dashboard and available features.

## Tips
- Ensure your phone number is registered with your SmartWinnr account.
- If you do not receive the OTP or if it has expired, use the option to request a new OTP.
- For any issues, contact your local IT team or SmartWinnr support.
