> **At a glance** - Log in to SmartWinnr from the app or web version, then enter your username and password or SSO credentials.

Open the installed SmartWinnr app on your iOS or Android device to log in. You can also use the SmartWinnr Web Version in a supported browser.

The login flow asks for your username first. Then you enter your password, or you are redirected to your organization's SSO page if your organization uses SAML authentication.

## When to use this

Use this when you need to access your SmartWinnr account from a mobile device or web browser.

- You want to sign in from the SmartWinnr app on iOS or Android.
- You want to sign in from the SmartWinnr Web Version.
- You need to use your company email or mobile number as your username.
- Your organization uses SAML and sends you to an SSO login page.

## Steps

### 1. Open SmartWinnr
Open the installed app on your iOS or Android device. Look for the SmartWinnr icon, which is displayed below.

![SmartWinnr app icon](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75dsy2.png)

You can also access your SmartWinnr account through the SmartWinnr Web Version using any supported web browser.

**SmartWinnr Web Version**: https://web.smartwinnr.com/login

For the best experience, use **Google Chrome**, as SmartWinnr is optimized for the Chrome browser.

### 2. Enter your username
When the login screen appears, enter your username. You can use your company email or mobile number, then click **Next**.

![Login screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms74zazi.png)

If you are unsure of your username, contact us at [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com). Include your organization name and official email ID in your message.

If you see "user does not exist," your SmartWinnr account has not yet been created by your system administrator. Contact [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com) and provide your organization name and official email ID.

### 3. Enter your password
After you click **Next**, enter your password, then click **Sign In**.

![Password entry screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75gyo2.png)

The app remembers your password unless you install a new version of the app or uninstall and reinstall it.

If you have forgotten your password, [click here to learn how to retrieve your forgotten password](/get-started/onboarding/what-if-i-ve-forgotten-my-password).

### 4. Use SSO when required
If your organization uses SAML SSO to access SmartWinnr, you are redirected to your organization's SSO login page after you enter your SmartWinnr username. Enter your organization-provided SSO credentials to authenticate and access your SmartWinnr account.

You do not need to enter a separate SmartWinnr password when you log in through SSO. Authentication is handled by your organization's identity provider.

If you cannot log in or see an error on the SSO login page, contact your organization's IT or SSO administrator or the SmartWinnr Support Team for assistance.

### 5. Confirm your home screen
After you log in successfully, you are directed to the home screen of the SmartWinnr app. This screen includes a dashboard, as shown below.

![SmartWinnr home screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75ggog.png)

If you have a system-generated password for your account, change it for security purposes. [Learn how to change the password for your SmartWinnr account](/get-started/onboarding/how-can-i-change-my-password-for-smartwinnr).

## Things to know

If you experience issues such as the app crashing, taking longer than usual to load, or displaying a blank screen or a “Page Not Found” error on your phone, tablet, or PC, please wait for a few minutes and try logging out and logging back in.

If the issue persists, you may also try uninstalling and reinstalling the app before logging in again. In some cases, these issues may be caused by temporary server outages or connectivity problems.

If you are still unable to access the app after trying these troubleshooting steps, please contact Customer Support. Our support team will coordinate with the core team to investigate the issue, provide the necessary troubleshooting assistance, and help resolve it as soon as possible.

:::note
If you use SSO, you log in with your organization's credentials instead of a separate SmartWinnr password.
:::
