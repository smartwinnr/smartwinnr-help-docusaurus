> **At a glance** - This article explains how to log in to your SmartWinnr account on your iOS or Android device.

To log in to the SmartWinnr app, start by opening the installed app on your iOS or Android device. Look for the SmartWinnr icon, which is displayed below:

![SmartWinnr app icon](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75dsy2.png)

You can also access your SmartWinnr account through the SmartWinnr Web Version using any supported web browser.

**SmartWinnr Web Version**: https://web.smartwinnr.com/login

For the best experience, we recommend using **Google Chrome**, as SmartWinnr is optimized for the Chrome browser.

Once you open the app/ web version, you will be greeted with the login screen. Enter your username, which can be your company email or mobile number, and click the **Next** button.

![Login screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms74zazi.png)

If you are unsure of your username, please contact us at [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com). Make sure to include your organization name and official email ID in your message.

If you encounter a message stating "user does not exist," this indicates that your SmartWinnr account has not yet been created by your system administrator. In this case, please reach out to [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com) and provide your organization name and official email ID.

After clicking **Next**, you will see a field for entering your password. Type in your password and then click the **Sign In** button.

![Password entry screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75gyo2.png)

The app will remember your password unless you install a new version of the app or uninstall and reinstall it.

If you have forgotten your password, [click here to learn how to retrieve your forgotten password](/get-started/onboarding/what-if-i-ve-forgotten-my-password).

**Note: If your organization uses SAML (SSO) authentication to access SmartWinnr, you will be redirected to your organization's SSO login page after entering your SmartWinnr username. You will then need to enter your organization-provided SSO credentials to authenticate and access your SmartWinnr account.**

You do not need to enter a separate SmartWinnr password when logging in through SSO. The authentication will be handled by your organization's identity provider.

If you are unable to log in or encounter an error on the SSO login page, please contact your organization's IT/SSO administrator or the SmartWinnr Support Team for assistance.

Upon successfully logging in, you will be directed to the home screen of the SmartWinnr app. This screen features a dashboard, as illustrated below:

![SmartWinnr home screen](/img/helpscout/authored/how-can-i-login-to-smartwinnr-app-ms75ggog.png)

If you have a system-generated password for your account, it is advisable to change it for security purposes. [Learn how to change the password for your SmartWinnr account](/get-started/onboarding/how-can-i-change-my-password-for-smartwinnr).
