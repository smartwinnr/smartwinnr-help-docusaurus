> **At a glance** - You can access SmartWinnr on various devices, including PCs, laptops, and mobile devices. 

You can open SmartWinnr from a PC or laptop, as well as through iOS and Android mobile apps. Use the same username and password to log in to both the web portal and the mobile app.

## When to use this
This article is useful when you want to access SmartWinnr on different devices. You can choose to use either a web browser or a mobile app based on your preference.

- Access SmartWinnr from a PC or laptop.
- Download the SmartWinnr mobile app on your smartphone.
- Use your existing login credentials across platforms.

## Steps

### 1. Access SmartWinnr from PC or Laptop
To access SmartWinnr from your laptop or desktop, click on this link: [https://web.smartwinnr.com/](https://web.smartwinnr.com/).

### 2. Access SmartWinnr from iOS/Android Mobile App
To download the SmartWinnr mobile app on your device, use the following links:

- **Android:** [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quizprompt.app&hl=en](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.quizprompt.app&hl=en)
  
- **iOS:** [https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/smartwinnr/id1129087019?mt=8](https://itunes.apple.com/in/app/smartwinnr/id1129087019?mt=8)

## Tips
- Ensure you have a stable internet connection when accessing SmartWinnr.
- Keep your app updated to access the latest features and improvements.
- If you encounter any issues, check your login credentials or contact SmartWinnr support.

## Things to know
You can use the same username and password to log in to both the web portal and the mobile app.
