> **At a glance** - Use **Notifications** to open assigned content quickly. Some items open directly from the notification, while others open from the left menu.

Use **Notifications** to see activity assignments in one place. From there, you can open supported items like **Smartfeed**, **Quiz**, **Survey**, and **View Coaching**. Some **SmartPaths** and **Field coaching** notifications only point you to the assignment, so you open them from the left menu instead.

## When to use this

Use this flow when you receive an assignment and want to start it right away.

- You want to open a **Smartfeed** from a notification.
- You want to launch a **Quiz** or **Survey** from the notification list.
- You want to start a coaching activity from a notification.
- You received a **SmartPath** or **Field coaching** assignment that does not open directly.

## Steps

### 1. Open **Notifications**
Go to the left menu and select **Notifications**. This shows the list of assigned activities.

![Take notifications from left menu](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2nreqf.png)

### 2. Review your assignments
Look through the notification list to find the activity you want to open.

![List of notifications](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2nub4d.png)

### 3. Open the assigned content
Select **View Smartfeed** to open the Smartfeed page.

![Viewing Smartfeed](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2nwhbo.png)

The Smartfeed opens on its own page.

![Actual Smartfeed](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2nxhk6.png)

### 4. Start the activity
Select **TAKE QUIZ** to open the quiz.

![Click on TAKE QUIZ button to start quiz](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2o8vur.png)

The quiz opens, and you can begin it.

![Actual quiz](/img/helpscout/authored/authored-mu2ocyhi.png)

You can use the same flow for **TAKE SURVEY** and **VIEW COACHING** when notifications from those activities appear.

Once you have taken the activity, you cannot take it again. It shows the pop-up below.

![Quiz has been played laready](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-open-content-from-a-notification-mu3pzv27.png)

Once the activity expires, you cannot take it from the notification. It shows the error below.

![Quiz is not available](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-open-content-from-a-notification-mu3q3i3c.png)

Activities like **SmartPaths** and **Field coaching** notifications do not include a button that opens the activity directly. Open those activities from the left menu and submit them there.

![Smartpath notifications](/img/helpscout/authored/how-to-open-content-from-a-notification-mu3q625p.png)
