> **At a glance** - User roles in SmartWinnr define what each person can access and perform within the platform. Each role is designed for a specific responsibility, and access can be further limited by team or content permissions.

User roles in SmartWinnr ensure that each person has access only to the features they need. Whether someone is a learner, manager, editor, or administrator, their role determines the screens they can view and the actions they can perform. Access can also be restricted to specific teams, business units, or content.

## When to use this
Use this when you want to:
- Decide which role to assign to a new user.
- Understand what each role can access in SmartWinnr.
- Find out why one user can see a feature that another user cannot.
- Learn how team access works together with user roles to control what users can see and access in SmartWinnr.
- Change or update a user's role in the platform.


### 1. User (Learner) Role

The User (Learner) role is the standard role assigned to employees or learners who use SmartWinnr to complete learning, coaching, assessments, and other assigned activities.

Users primarily access SmartWinnr through the Learner/User view, where they can view and complete the learning activities assigned to them. The features available to a user may vary depending on the modules and activities enabled for their organization.

### What users can do

With the User (Learner) role, you can:

* Complete assigned learning activities.

* Take quizzes and assessments, and view your results.

* Access SmartFeeds shared with you.

* Complete SmartPaths and their learning modules.

* Participate in coaching activities and submit coaching responses, including video, audio, or screen recordings where applicable.

* Participate in assigned competitions and challenges.

* View your performance, progress, and learning history for applicable activities.

* Communicate with other users through the available chat and collaboration features.

This role is designed for learners who participate in training and track their own learning progress within SmartWinnr.


### 2. Understand the Manager Role
The **Manager** role is designed for users who oversee the performance and development of employees or end users. Managers can access their role through **web.smartwinnr.com**.

Managers can:
- View and track the learning progress and performance of their assigned team members.
- Coach team members by providing scores, feedback, and guidance through **Coaching** and **Field Coaching**.
- Review coaching submissions and identify areas where users need additional support.
- Access reports and performance insights to monitor team progress and development.
- Assign and manage learning activities for their team.

This role helps managers monitor team performance, provide continuous feedback, and support employee development within their assigned teams.

### 3. Understand the Editor Role
The **Editor** role is designed for users who create and manage learning content and activities. Editors can build, update, and organize training content without managing users or platform settings.

Editors can:
- Create and manage quizzes, surveys, **SmartFeeds**, coaching activities, forms, competitions, and learning programs.
- Review reports and analytics related to the content they manage.
- Collaborate with other editors by giving them access to specific content or tasks they create.
- Update and maintain learning content to keep it accurate and relevant.

**Editor Access Permissions:** Editors can grant access to specific tasks they create—such as quizzes, surveys, **SmartFeeds**, competitions, coaching activities, and forms—to other editors. This access permission is available only between editors.

### 4. Understand the Admin Role
The **Admin** role provides the highest level of access and is responsible for managing the platform, users, content, and organizational settings.

Admins can:
- Configure platform settings based on organizational requirements.
- Create, update, and delete user accounts.
- Assign and manage user roles, teams, and business units.
- Create and manage learning activities, coaching programs, quizzes, surveys, **SmartFeeds**, forms, and competitions.
- Access organizational reports and analytics to monitor usage, performance, and learner progress.

This role is responsible for managing the overall setup and administration of SmartWinnr and ensuring users have the appropriate access and resources they need.

### 5. How Roles Work with Team Scope
In SmartWinnr, roles and team scope work together to control user access. A role defines the features and actions a user can perform, while team scope defines which teams, users, or content that user can access.

For example, two users may both have the **Manager** role, but one is assigned to the **Sales** team and the other to the **Marketing** team. Each manager can view reports, coaching, and learner progress only for their assigned team.

**Key point:** Users with the same role can have different access based on the teams or business units assigned to them.
