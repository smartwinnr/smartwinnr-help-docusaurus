> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr provides different report families to help you track learner activity, content engagement, coaching progress, assessments, rewards, and user administration. Instead of choosing from a long list of individual reports, select the report family based on the business question you want to answer. Each report focuses on a specific area, making it easier to find the right insights.

## When to use this
Use these reports to select the most appropriate report for your reporting and analytics needs. Consider these scenarios:
* Tracking quiz participation, scores, submissions, and proctored assessment results.
* Monitoring user login history, platform activity, and engagement levels.
* Reviewing coaching attempts, completion status, and learner progress.
* Measuring SmartPath assignments, batches, segments, attendance, and completion.
* Analyzing survey responses and form submissions.
* Viewing engagement with SmartFeeds, videos, documents, and Knowledge Hub content.
* Tracking KPI achievement, points earned, rewards, and leaderboard performance.
* Exporting user lists or reviewing audit and administrative activities.
* Accessing completed report downloads from the Generated Reports queue.



### 1. SmartFeeds Reports
SmartFeeds reports provide insights into the performance of SmartFeed content and user engagement across the platform. You can download different reports to analyze SmartFeed activity, viewing trends, comments, and user-level engagement.

**Available SmartFeeds Reports**
- **SmartFeed Detailed Report**: Downloads detailed information for every SmartFeed created in SmartWinnr, including content details and engagement metrics.
- **Report by User**: Downloads SmartFeed activity for individual users, including the SmartFeeds they have viewed and their engagement details.
- **Report by View**: Shows the viewing activity of SmartFeed content, including the number of views and related user activity for each SmartFeed.
- **Report by SmartFeed Comments**: Downloads all comments made by users on SmartFeeds, along with the associated SmartFeed and user details.

### 2. Question and Quiz Reports
Question and quiz reports provide detailed insights into quiz participation, performance, learner responses, and team analytics. These reports help administrators and managers evaluate quiz effectiveness and learner progress.

**Available Question and Quiz Reports**
- **Quiz Combined Report**: Downloads all quiz-related information in a single report, including participant details, quiz details, and question details.
- **Quiz Results Report(Sheets in Quiz Report)**:

\- **Participants**:Participants Report: The Participants Report provides a detailed summary of each user's quiz participation, completion status, attempts, scores, time taken, and organizational details.

\- **Participant Answer Analysis Report**: The Participant Answer Analysis Report provides a detailed breakdown of each participant's responses, including selected answers, correct answers, scores, and time taken for every question in a quiz.

\- **Quiz Details Report**: The Quiz Details Report provides an overview of a quiz, including its title, description, type, schedule, total number of questions, and timer settings.

\- **Quiz Questions Details Report**: The Quiz Questions Details Report provides the complete list of quiz questions along with their answer options, correct answers, and associated categories such as department, level, and product knowledge.

- **Quiz Question Analytics Report**: The Question Analytics Report provides performance insights for each quiz question, including question type, average score, average time taken, and the number of correct and incorrect responses.

- **Leaderboard Report**: The Leaderboard Report displays participant rankings for a quiz based on their score, time taken, and other user and organizational details.

- **Team Analytics Report**: Shows category-wise performance, country-wise performance, user score distribution, leaderboard, and team knowledge analysis.
- **Team Progress Report**: Tracks quiz participation, completion status, and scores for each participant.
- **Quiz Comparison Report**: Compares participant performance across multiple quizzes over a selected time period.

### 3. Surveys Reports
Use the Surveys report family to analyze survey participation and responses. Choose the report based on whether you need an overall summary of survey results or detailed responses from individual users.

**Available Survey Reports**
- **Aggregate Report**: You want a consolidated summary of survey results, including each question, response options, and the percentage of responses for every answer.
- **User Wise Report**: You want detailed survey responses for each assigned user, including user details, completion status, survey completion date, and answers submitted.

### 4. SmartPath Reports
Use the SmartPath report family to track learner progress, completion, attendance, and evaluation across SmartPath batches and segments. Choose the report based on the level of detail you need.

**Available SmartPath Reports**
- **User Wise Report**: You want to monitor the progress of individual learners, including completion status, progress percentage, scores, and time spent in a SmartPath.
- **Overview Report**: You want a summary of SmartPath performance, including modules, segments, participation percentages, and associated batch details.
- **Evaluation & Feedback Report**: You want quiz evaluation results, coaching scores and feedback, survey responses, and trainer or manager feedback collected within a SmartPath.
- **Segment Wise Report**: You want learner progress and scores for each SmartPath segment to identify where learners are progressing or getting stuck.
- **Attendance Report**: You want attendance details for Learning Sessions within a SmartPath batch.
- **Attendance Summary Report**: You want a combined attendance summary across multiple SmartPath Learning Session batches.

- **Learner Progress Report**: The Learner Progress Report provides each learner's SmartPath completion status, progress, score, time spent, last activity, and associated organizational details for the selected period.

### 5. Knowledge Hub (KHub) Reports
Use the Knowledge Hub (KHub) report family to track content usage and user engagement within the Knowledge Hub. Choose the report based on whether you need user-level activity or content view analytics.

**Available KHub Reports**
- **KHub Items Details Report**: provides details about KHub items and folders, including their creators, creation dates, paths, and available content types such as PDFs, images, and videos.
It also indicates whether each KHub item is **archived**, helping track and manage the available content.

- **Report by User**: You want to monitor an individual user's KHub activity, including the content they have accessed and their engagement details.
- **Report by View**: You want to analyze the viewing activity of KHub content, including the number of views and user engagement for each item.

### 6. QResolve Report
QResolve Reports provide a consolidated view of queries raised in SmartWinnr, capturing details such as query information, initiator and user attributes, category, tags, status, response details, response time, and feedback. This report helps monitor query handling and track the progress of requests from initiation to response.

**Available QResolve Reports**
- **Query Report:** provides details about raised queries, including the initiator, query category and tags, timestamps, responses, response time, and query status.
It helps track the complete query lifecycle, from initiation and categorization to response, resolution, and feedback such as upvotes or downvotes.

- **QResolve Tag Report:** The QueryResolve Tag Report provides an overview of tags, categories, access settings, user groups, and escalation details, helping admins manage and review query-related configurations.

### 8. iShare Report
iShare is a SmartWinnr feature used to create and manage shareable content such as templates and personalized messages. It helps users organize content, track its status, and publish it for sharing with the intended audience.

**Available iShare Reports**

- **logs Report:** provides details of shareable content sent to users, including the content type, template variables, app version, platform, user status, and relevant organizational attributes. It helps track how personalized SmartFeed content is shared across users and devices.

- **Item Details Report:** provides information about iShare items, including their title, type, publication status, folder, customer profile, creator, and creation dates. It helps track the availability and organization of iShare content within SmartWinnr.

### 9. Content Center Report

**Available Content Center Report**

- **Report:** provides details of content available in SmartWinnr, including the content title, type, status, folder, thumbnail and content URLs, and creation or modification details. It helps track and manage published images and videos stored in the Content Center.

### 10. Video Coaching Reports
Video Coaching Reports in SmartWinnr provide valuable insights into the performance of learners during video coaching assignments. Here’s what you need to know:

- **Purpose**: These reports help you analyze how learners are performing in specific video coaching scenarios, allowing you to evaluate their skills and effectiveness.
- **Insights Provided**: 
  - Performance metrics for each learner's video submission.
  - Evaluation based on criteria such as keyword usage, talk time, and overall structure.
  - Identification of strengths and areas for improvement for each learner.

**Available Video Coaching Reports**

- **Coaching Completion Report:** shows the status of coaching activities assigned to users, including coach and coachee details, coaching title, assigned date, review date, and completion status. It helps track whether coaching has been completed and reviewed.

- **Completion Report as CSV:** Downloads a CSV file with the overall completion status of coaching activities for each user.

- **Completion Report as CSV (ClickHouse):** Downloads the same completion report from the ClickHouse analytics database for faster processing of large datasets.

- **Attempt-wise Report as CSV:** provides details of every coaching attempt made by a coachee, including the coaching title, assigned and submission dates, response status, scores, time spent, attempt number, and reviewer status. It helps track each coaching attempt and monitor user performance throughout the coaching process.

- **Attempt-Wise CSV Report (ClickHouse):** Downloads the attempt-wise report from the ClickHouse analytics database for faster export of large reports.

- **Coaching Completion Report(ClickHouse):** shows the completion status of assigned coaching activities, including coach and coachee details, coaching title, assigned date, submission date, review date, and current status. It helps track which coaching activities have been completed and reviewed.

- **Attempt Wise Combined Objective Report:** provides a consolidated view of each coaching attempt along with the scores achieved for individual objectives. It helps analyze a user’s performance across different coaching objectives within each attempt.



- **Overall Coaching Summary Report:** Provides a comprehensive summary of all coaching activities across users.

- **Coaching Feedback Report:** Details feedback provided to learners after coaching sessions.

- **All Coaching Report:**  It provides details of coaching programs, including the coaching title, category, creator, start and end dates, source, and current status. It helps track and manage coaching activities across the organization.

- **All Coaching User Assignment Report:** Lists all assignments given to users, along with their status.
- **Overall Coaching Report:** Provides an overall view of coaching programs, including the coaching title, category, division, creator, start and end dates, source, and coaching status.
- **Coaching Comparison Report:** Compares performance across different coaching scenarios or learners.

### 11. Field Coaching Reports
Field Coaching Reports in SmartWinnr provide insights into coaching performance, learner progress, training effectiveness, and assessment outcomes through different reports such as Overall Coaching, Coaching-wise, Field Coaching Completion, and Overall Assessment Reports.

**Available Field Coaching Reports**
- **Count Report**: This report provides the count of coaching sessions created under each manager. Use this report when you want to quickly assess the number of coaching sessions assigned to each manager.
- **Combined Report**: This report gives a comprehensive view of all coaching sessions for all users in a CSV format. Opt for the Combined Report when you need a detailed overview of coaching activities across multiple users.

### 13. Forms Report
Forms in SmartWinnr are tools for collecting information and tracking performance, with two main types: Acknowledgement Forms for authenticating documents and KPI Value Submit Forms for submitting KPIs.

**Available Forms Reports**
- **Download Report:** The Forms Download Report provides a detailed record of user submissions for a form. It helps administrators review the responses submitted by users and track submission details.

**The report can include:**
The report includes user responses and comments, uploaded files or videos, submitted user details such as name, email, and identifier, submission date and month, last modified date and user, and approval status with approver feedback when applicable.

- **purpose**: The report can be downloaded to review, track, and analyze form submissions and their approval-related details.

### 14. Competitions Report
- **Competition Reports:**  SmartWinnr provide insights into participant performance and engagement during competitions. They help track **competition results, leaderboard rankings, and activity participation** to evaluate performance and identify high-performing participants or teams.

**Available Competitions Report**

- **The Leaderboard Report:** shows participant rankings based on their performance in quizzes or learning activities. It includes user details, reporting manager information, rank, score, quiz score, and organization attributes such as Business Unit, Country, Region, Team, and Designation.

### 15. Learner Reports
Learner Reports provide insights into individual and overall learner performance, helping managers track progress and identify improvement areas.

**Available Learner Reports**
- **Overall Report:** A consolidated view of learner performance across selected Business Units or Teams, including total learners, average scores, and quiz statistics.

- **By Learner:** Detailed performance analytics for a specific learner with filters such as Business Unit, Country, and Team.

- **Users Login Dashboard:** 

-**First Login Report:** provides user login and profile activity details, helping admins monitor platform access and user engagement.

The report includes: user name, email, user status, reporting manager details, organization identifier, role, initial login date, last login date, profile update status, and organization attributes such as Business Unit, Country, Region, Territory, Team, and Designation.


-**All Login Report By User:** provides a complete overview of user login activity, device usage, and app engagement across the SmartWinnr platform.

The report includes: user details, login count, last login date and time, device type, OS version, app version, app status (foreground/background), reporting manager details, user status, and organization attributes such as Business Unit, Country, Department, Region, Team, and Designation.


**All Login Report By Date** section shows the number of user logins recorded for each day. It helps track daily user engagement and identify changes in login activity.

### 16. Readiness Index Report
**Available Readiness Index Report**
-  **Readiness Index Report:** provides an overview of each learner’s readiness by combining knowledge, skill, engagement, and learning progress into a single score. It helps managers monitor learner performance and identify areas that require improvement.

**The report includes**: user details, knowledge score, skill score, engagement score, readiness index, readiness rating, proficiency level, modules completed, active modules, reporting period, and organization attributes such as Business Unit, Country, Region, Team, and Territory.

### 17. Groups Reports
Groups Reports help admins get a quick view of the groups available in SmartWinnr and understand how users are organized within them.

**Available Groups Reports**
- **Groups Report:** The Groups Report provides an overview of all groups created in SmartWinnr and their member details. It helps admins manage groups, track ownership, and monitor group size and creation information.

**The report includes:** group name, group description, number of members, group owner, group type, group creation date, and created by.

* **Groups Report With Members:** shows the members of each group along with their user and organizational details. It helps admins view group membership, user status, reporting managers, roles, and other assigned attributes.

### 18. Audit Log Report

Audit Logs in SmartWinnr provide a record of activities and events performed within the system. They help admins monitor user actions, track system activity, and review the history of important events.

**Available Audit Log Report**
* **Audit Log Report:** The Audit Log Report provides detailed information about changes made to content in SmartWinnr. It helps admins identify who made the change, what content was affected, and what action was performed.

**The report includes:** date of change, user details, content identifier and title, action performed, folder path, object type, format, shareable status, and version information.

### 19. Manage Contacts Report

The **Contacts Reports** provide an overview of contacts available in SmartWinnr, helping admins review contact information, ownership, contact types, and assigned organizational or territory details.

**Available Manage Contacts Report**
* **Contacts Report:** provides an overview of contacts and their assigned organizational details. It helps admins view contact ownership, contact types, divisions, and associated territories.

**The report includes:** Contact Name, Identifier, Division, Owner, Contact Type, Territory List, and Territory Labels.

### 20.Reports Under Admin

* **Initial Login Report:** This report shows when users first and last logged in to SmartWinnr, along with their basic account and organizational details.

* **Users View Logs Report:** shows user access and engagement with SmartFeeds, including the SmartFeed name, total time spent, and the dates of first and last access.

### 21. Scratchcard Report
Scratchcard Reports provide an overview of scratchcard activities in SmartWinnr, helping admins track scratchcard distribution, user participation, redemption, and reward status.

* **Scratchcard Report:** shows users’ scratchcard activity, including when the scratchcard was received, scratched, and redeemed, along with the amount, status, and user details.

### 22.Global Points Report
Points Reports provide an overview of points earned by users through different activities in SmartWinnr, helping admins track point allocation and related activities.

* **Global Points Report:** provides an overview of points awarded to users, showing the related activity, object, points received, and the date they were awarded.

***Note:*** **Generated Reports** displays all generated reports that are available to download from SmartWinnr.
