> **At a glance** - SmartWinnr provides different report families to help you track learner activity, content engagement, coaching progress, assessments, rewards, and user administration. Instead of choosing from a long list of individual reports, select the report family based on the business question you want to answer. Each report focuses on a specific area, making it easier to find the right insights.

## When to use this
Use these reports to select the most appropriate report for your reporting and analytics needs. Consider these scenarios:
* Tracking quiz participation, scores, submissions, and proctored assessment results.
* Monitoring user login history, platform activity, and engagement levels.
* Reviewing coaching attempts, completion status, and learner progress.
* Measuring SmartPath assignments, batches, segments, attendance, and completion.
* Analyzing survey responses and form submissions.
* Viewing engagement with SmartFeeds, videos, documents, and Knowledge Hub content.
* Tracking KPI achievement, points earned, rewards, and leaderboard performance.
* Exporting user lists or reviewing audit and administrative activities.
* Accessing completed report downloads from the Generated Reports queue.

## Steps

### 1. SmartFeeds Reports
SmartFeeds reports provide insights into the performance of SmartFeed content and user engagement across the platform. You can download different reports to analyze SmartFeed activity, viewing trends, comments, and user-level engagement.

**Available SmartFeeds Reports**
- **SmartFeed Detailed Report**: Downloads detailed information for every SmartFeed created in SmartWinnr, including content details and engagement metrics.
- **Report by User**: Downloads SmartFeed activity for individual users, including the SmartFeeds they have viewed and their engagement details.
- **Report by View**: Shows the viewing activity of SmartFeed content, including the number of views and related user activity for each SmartFeed.
- **Report by SmartFeed Comments**: Downloads all comments made by users on SmartFeeds, along with the associated SmartFeed and user details.

**Generate the SmartFeed Detailed Report**
1. Navigate to **Editor**.
2. Go to **Learning and Knowledge**.
3. Select **View All SmartFeeds**.
4. Click the **hamburger menu**.
5. Select **Download SmartFeed Detailed Report**.
6. Apply filters such as **Date Range** and **Business Unit**, if required.
7. Generate and download the report.

Note: The report is processed in the background and will be available in the **Generated Reports** section once it is ready for download.

### 2. Question and Quiz Reports
Question and quiz reports provide detailed insights into quiz participation, performance, learner responses, and team analytics. These reports help administrators and managers evaluate quiz effectiveness and learner progress.

**Available Question and Quiz Reports**
- **Quiz Combined Report**: Downloads all quiz-related information in a single report, including participant details, quiz details, and question details.
- **Quiz Report**: Downloads a detailed report for a specific quiz with participant, quiz, and question information.
- **Team Analytics Report**: Shows category-wise performance, country-wise performance, user score distribution, leaderboard, and team knowledge analysis.
- **Team Progress Report**: Tracks quiz participation, completion status, and scores for each participant.
- **Quiz Comparison Report**: Compares participant performance across multiple quizzes over a selected time period.

**Download the Quiz Report**
1. Navigate to **Editor**.
2. Go to **Learning and Knowledge → Questions and Quizzes**.
3. Select **View All Quizzes**.
4. Click the **Download** icon next to the required quiz.
5. Select the report sheets you want to include and generate the report.

**Quiz Report Sheets**
The downloaded quiz report can include the following sheets:
- **Participants**: Participant details, completion status, score, correct and incorrect answers, and submission date.
- **Participant Answer Analysis**: Detailed breakdown of the options selected by each participant and the score received for every question.
- **Quiz Details**: Quiz information, settings, configuration, duration, and other quiz properties.
- **Quiz Question Details**: Complete list of questions included in the quiz along with question-related details.

Note: Generated quiz reports are processed in the background and become available in the **Generated Reports** section once they are ready for download.

### 3. Surveys Reports
Use the Surveys report family to analyze survey participation and responses. Choose the report based on whether you need an overall summary of survey results or detailed responses from individual users.

**Available Survey Reports**
- **Aggregate Report**: You want a consolidated summary of survey results, including each question, response options, and the percentage of responses for every answer.
- **User Wise Report**: You want detailed survey responses for each assigned user, including user details, completion status, survey completion date, and answers submitted.

**Download Survey Reports**
1. Navigate to **Editor → Surveys**.
2. Select the required survey and open **Analytics**.
3. Click the **hamburger menu** in the top-right corner.
4. Select **Aggregate Report** or **User Wise Report**.
5. Generate and download the report.

Note: The report is generated in the background and will be available in the **Generated Reports** section once it is ready for download.

### 4. SmartPath Reports
Use the SmartPath report family to track learner progress, completion, attendance, and evaluation across SmartPath batches and segments. Choose the report based on the level of detail you need.

**Available SmartPath Reports**
- **User Wise Report**: You want to monitor the progress of individual learners, including completion status, progress percentage, scores, and time spent in a SmartPath.
- **Overview Report**: You want a summary of SmartPath performance, including modules, segments, participation percentages, and associated batch details.
- **Evaluation & Feedback Report**: You want quiz evaluation results, coaching scores and feedback, survey responses, and trainer or manager feedback collected within a SmartPath.
- **Segment Wise Report**: You want learner progress and scores for each SmartPath segment to identify where learners are progressing or getting stuck.
- **Attendance Report**: You want attendance details for Learning Sessions within a SmartPath batch.
- **Attendance Summary Report**: You want a combined attendance summary across multiple SmartPath Learning Session batches.

**Download SmartPath Reports**
1. Navigate to **Editor → SmartPath**.
2. Open the required SmartPath or Batch.
3. Click the **hamburger menu** or **Download Report** option.
4. Select the required report type.
5. Generate and download the report.

Note: The Segment Wise Report is available only if it is enabled for your organization. All generated reports are available in the **Generated Reports** section once processing is complete.

### 5. Knowledge Hub (KHub) Reports
Use the Knowledge Hub (KHub) report family to track content usage and user engagement within the Knowledge Hub. Choose the report based on whether you need user-level activity or content view analytics.

**Available KHub Reports**
- **Report by User**: You want to monitor an individual user's KHub activity, including the content they have accessed and their engagement details.
- **Report by View**: You want to analyze the viewing activity of KHub content, including the number of views and user engagement for each item.

**Download KHub Reports**
1. Navigate to **Editor → Knowledge Hub (KHub)**.
2. Open the required KHub content or analytics page.
3. Click the **hamburger menu**.
4. Select **Report by User** or **Report by View**.
5. Generate and download the report.

Note: Generated KHub reports are processed in the background and become available in the **Generated Reports** section once they are ready for download.

### 6. Coaching Reports
Video Coaching Reports in SmartWinnr provide valuable insights into the performance of learners during video coaching assignments. Here’s what you need to know:

- **Purpose**: These reports help you analyze how learners are performing in specific video coaching scenarios, allowing you to evaluate their skills and effectiveness.
- **Insights Provided**: 
  - Performance metrics for each learner's video submission.
  - Evaluation based on criteria such as keyword usage, talk time, and overall structure.
  - Identification of strengths and areas for improvement for each learner.

**Available Coaching Reports**
- **Overall Coaching Summary Report**: Provides a comprehensive summary of all coaching activities across users.
- **Submission Analytics**: Analyzes learner submissions to track participation and performance trends.
- **Coaching Feedback Report**: Details feedback provided to learners after coaching sessions.
- **All Coaching User Assignment Report**: Lists all assignments given to users, along with their status.
- **Coaching Comparison Report**: Compares performance across different coaching scenarios or learners.

### 7. Field Coaching Reports
- **Count Report**: This report provides the count of coaching sessions created under each manager. Use this report when you want to quickly assess the number of coaching sessions assigned to each manager.
- **Combined Report**: This report gives a comprehensive view of all coaching sessions for all users in a CSV format. Opt for the Combined Report when you need a detailed overview of coaching activities across multiple users.

### 8. KPI Reports
Using Scorecards reports in SmartWinnr can be highly beneficial for several reasons:
- **Performance Measurement**: They provide a clear view of how individuals or teams are performing against set KPIs.
- **Data-Driven Decisions**: With concrete data, you can make informed decisions about coaching, training needs, and resource allocation.
- **Identifying Trends**: Scorecards help you identify trends over time, making it easier to spot areas of improvement or decline in performance.
- **Accountability**: They promote accountability by clearly outlining expectations and tracking progress against those expectations.
- **Motivation and Engagement**: By visualizing performance, scorecards can motivate team members to achieve their goals and engage in healthy competition.

In summary, Scorecards reports are a powerful tool for tracking performance, guiding development, and enhancing overall productivity within your team or organization. Using them can lead to more effective coaching and improved results.
