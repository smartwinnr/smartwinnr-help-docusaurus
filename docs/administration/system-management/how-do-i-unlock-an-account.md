> **At a glance** - An account may become locked after five consecutive incorrect password attempts. Admins can unlock accounts through the admin portal.

An account can be locked if a wrong password is entered five times consecutively. If an account is locked, it will remain locked for 20 minutes. Users can wait for this duration for the account to auto-unlock. Alternatively, they can contact the system admin or reach out to SmartWinnr support at [support@smartwinnr.com](mailto:support@smartwinnr.com) to request an account unlock.

## When to use this
This article is useful when:
- A user reports that their account is locked due to multiple failed login attempts.
- An admin needs to unlock a user's account to restore access.
- You want to understand the process for managing locked accounts within the platform.

## Steps

### 1. Access the Manage Users Section
Navigate to **Left Menu** > **ADMIN** > **Manage Users**.

### 2. Locate the Locked Account
Search for the user whose account is locked. Click on the user’s account to view their details.

![The Account details page.](/img/helpscout/admins/how-do-i-unlock-an-account-1.png)

### 3. Unlock the Account
On the Account details page, click on the hamburger menu in the top right corner. Select the **Unlock Account** option from the dropdown menu.

![On the Account details page click on the hamburger menu on the right top and click on the “Unlock Account” option.](/img/helpscout/admins/how-do-i-unlock-an-account-2.png)

## Tips
- Ensure that the user is aware of the account lockout policy to prevent future occurrences.
- Remind users to use a secure password and consider enabling two-factor authentication for added security.

## Things to know
- If the user continues to enter incorrect passwords, the account will remain locked, and they will need to wait for the auto-unlock period or contact support.
- Admins have the authority to unlock accounts at any time, regardless of the lockout duration.
